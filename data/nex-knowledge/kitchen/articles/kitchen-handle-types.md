# Kitchen Handle Types

Kitchen handles are both functional and decorative. The style, finish, and size of a handle can completely change the appearance of a kitchen — from traditional and classic to sleek and contemporary.

## Bar Handle

One of the most popular modern handle styles. A straight tubular handle mounted with two fixing points. Materials: Stainless Steel · Aluminium · Brass · Black Steel · Chrome. Best for modern · contemporary · commercial-style kitchens.

## T-Bar Handle

A cylindrical bar mounted on a central stem. Comfortable grip · contemporary appearance · easy installation. Popular on drawers and doors.

## Bow Handle

Curved handle with two fixing points. Comfortable to hold · timeless appearance · suitable for many kitchen styles.

## D Handle

Simple D-shaped pull handle. Best for family kitchens · utility rooms · modern kitchens.

## Bridge Handle

Flat rectangular handle with straight edges. Creates a clean architectural appearance.

## Square Bar Handle

Square profile instead of round. Sharp contemporary styling · minimalist appearance.

## Edge Pull Handle

Fixed to the top or side edge of the door. Discreet · modern appearance · clean cabinet lines. Popular in handleless-style kitchens.

## Finger Pull Handle

Small recessed grip allowing doors to be opened without a projecting handle. Minimal appearance · easy cleaning · contemporary styling.

## J-Pull Door

The pull is machined directly into the door. No separate handle required. Very popular in modern kitchens.

## True Handleless Rail System

Aluminium rails fitted between cabinets. Completely handle-free appearance · premium finish · continuous clean lines. Found in luxury kitchens.

## Cup Handle

Semi-circular pull fixed with two screws. Best for traditional · farmhouse · Shaker kitchens. Often used on drawers.

## Bin Cup Handle

A deeper version of the cup handle. Popular in Victorian and industrial-style kitchens.

## Knob Handle

Simple single fixing point. Shapes: Round · Square · Oval · Mushroom · Disc · Tapered. Suitable for cabinet doors and small drawers.

## Mushroom Knob

Rounded traditional knob. Very common on country kitchens.

## Ball Knob

Classic spherical knob. Suitable for cottage · painted · traditional furniture.

## Oval Knob

Elongated shape providing extra grip.

## Square Knob

Modern geometric appearance. Often finished in matt black or brushed brass.

## Recessed Handle

Installed flush into the door surface. Space saving · contemporary design · popular on sliding doors.

## Flush Pull Handle

Completely flush with the cabinet face. Ideal where doors slide or where a minimalist look is desired.

## Leather Pull Handle

Leather strap secured with decorative fixings. Best for Scandinavian kitchens · contemporary interiors · boutique designs.

## Wooden Handle

Manufactured from solid timber. Common timbers: Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple. Creates warmth and a natural feel.

## Integrated Timber Handle

Timber rail integrated into the door edge. Common in Scandinavian and bespoke kitchens.

## Shell Handle

Decorative half-moon handle. Popular in traditional · country · vintage furniture.

## Appliance Pull Handle

Long heavy-duty handle. Used on integrated fridge freezers · larder units · tall appliance cabinets.

## Handle Materials

Stainless Steel · Aluminium · Brass · Solid Brass · Chrome · Satin Nickel · Black Steel · Bronze · Copper · Pewter · Zinc Alloy · Timber · Leather · Acrylic · Glass · Ceramic.

## Popular Handle Finishes

Brushed Stainless Steel · Polished Chrome · Matt Black · Satin Nickel · Brushed Brass · Antique Brass · Antique Bronze · Gunmetal · Copper · Pewter · White · Gold · Rose Gold.

## Handle Sizes

Typical hole centres (distance between fixing screws): 96 · 128 · 160 · 192 · 224 · 256 · 320 · 416 mm. Long appliance handles can exceed 500 mm.

## Design Tips

- Match handle finishes with taps, sinks, and lighting for a coordinated look.
- Use longer handles on wide drawers for better balance and easier opening.
- Mix knobs on doors with cup or bar handles on drawers for a traditional style.
- In handleless kitchens, ensure there is enough finger clearance for comfortable use.
- Consider anti-fingerprint finishes such as matt black or brushed stainless steel in busy family kitchens.

## Choosing the Right Handle

Consider: kitchen style · cabinet door style · colour scheme · user comfort · ease of cleaning · household size · accessibility · finish durability · matching taps and appliances · budget.
