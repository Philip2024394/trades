# Kitchen Sink Types

The kitchen sink is one of the most frequently used fixtures in a home. It should be selected based on cabinet size · worktop material · cooking habits · household size · overall kitchen style.

## Single Bowl Sink

The most common sink design. One large bowl for washing dishes and food preparation. Large uninterrupted washing area · fits smaller kitchens · easy to clean · suitable for large pots and trays. Best for small kitchens · apartments · contemporary kitchens.

## 1.5 Bowl Sink

One full-size bowl with a smaller secondary bowl. Wash and rinse simultaneously · food preparation · defrosting · extra flexibility. One of the most popular choices for family kitchens.

## Double Bowl Sink

Two full-size bowls. Separate washing and rinsing · two users at once · excellent for large households · commercial-style workflow. Best for busy family kitchens · large kitchens.

## Triple Bowl Sink

Three individual bowls: main · secondary · preparation. Usually found in luxury kitchens.

## Undermount Sink

Mounted beneath the worktop. Seamless appearance · easy worktop cleaning · modern look · premium finish. Suitable worktops: Quartz · Granite · Marble · Porcelain · Solid Surface.

## Inset Sink

Dropped into a cut-out in the worktop. The sink rim sits on top of the work surface. Easy installation · suitable for most worktops · cost effective. Most common sink installation method.

## Flush Mount Sink

Sink sits level with the worktop surface. Premium appearance · easy cleaning · modern design. Requires precise installation.

## Belfast Sink

Traditional deep ceramic sink. Large single bowl · thick ceramic construction · exposed front. Best for farmhouse · country · traditional homes.

## Butler Sink

Similar to a Belfast sink but usually with a lower front. Traditional appearance · large washing capacity · timeless design.

## Farmhouse Sink

General term for exposed-front sinks. Available in Ceramic · Fireclay · Stainless Steel · Composite.

## Apron Front Sink

The front face of the sink remains visible. Popular in luxury farmhouse kitchens.

## Corner Sink

Designed to fit kitchen corners. Ideal for unusual layouts.

## Island Sink

Installed within a kitchen island. Often paired with preparation areas · breakfast bars.

## Preparation Sink

Small secondary sink. Typically used for washing vegetables · food preparation · drinks. Often installed in kitchen islands.

## Bar Sink

Compact sink. Common in home bars · utility rooms · entertainment areas.

## Utility Sink

Heavy-duty sink. Designed for cleaning equipment · laundry · household tasks.

## Integrated Sink

Manufactured from the same material as the worktop. Popular materials: Corian · Solid Surface · Quartz Composite. Provides a seamless appearance.

## Stainless Steel Sink

The world's most common sink material. Durable · hygienic · heat resistant · affordable · easy maintenance.

## Granite Composite Sink

Manufactured from granite particles and resin. Scratch resistant · heat resistant · modern appearance · available in many colours.

## Quartz Composite Sink

Made using engineered quartz. Strong · non-porous · easy to clean · premium finish.

## Ceramic Sink

Traditional glazed ceramic construction. Easy cleaning · stain resistant · classic appearance. Popular with farmhouse kitchens.

## Fireclay Sink

Premium ceramic sink fired at extremely high temperatures. Extremely durable · heat resistant · scratch resistant · luxury appearance.

## Copper Sink

Luxury handcrafted sink. Unique appearance · natural antimicrobial properties · ages with character.

## Cast Iron Sink

Heavy-duty sink coated with enamel. Extremely durable · traditional appearance · excellent longevity.

## Stone Sink

Manufactured from natural stone (granite · marble · limestone). Used in bespoke luxury kitchens.

## Glass Sink

Decorative sink for modern interiors. Less common in family kitchens.

## Sink Mounting Styles

Inset · Undermount · Flush Mount · Top Mount · Apron Front · Farmhouse · Integrated.

## Sink Shapes

Square · Rectangular · Round · Oval · Corner · D-Shaped.

## Sink Sizes

Typical cabinet sizes: 400 · 450 · 500 · 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 mm. Sink size should always suit the cabinet width and manufacturer's recommendations.

## Sink Accessories

Drainer · waste kit · basket strainer · chopping board · colander · drying rack · soap dispenser · glass rinser · overflow · waste disposal unit.

## Sink Colours

Stainless Steel · White · Black · Grey · Anthracite · Beige · Stone · Cream · Copper · Gold · Gunmetal.

## Recommended Sink Types

| Kitchen Style | Recommended Sink |
|---|---|
| Modern | Undermount Stainless Steel · Quartz Composite |
| Contemporary | Flush Mount · Granite Composite |
| Traditional | Belfast · Butler · Ceramic |
| Farmhouse | Belfast · Apron Front · Fireclay |
| Luxury | Integrated · Stone · Quartz Composite |
| Industrial | Stainless Steel |
| Scandinavian | White Composite · Undermount |
| Compact Apartment | Single Bowl Inset |
| Family Kitchen | 1.5 Bowl Composite or Double Bowl Stainless Steel |

## Choosing the Right Sink

Consider: kitchen size · cabinet width · household size · cooking frequency · worktop material · installation type · traditional or modern design · budget · maintenance requirements · whether a drainer is required.
