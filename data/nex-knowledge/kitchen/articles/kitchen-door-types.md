# Kitchen Door Types

Kitchen doors define the overall appearance and character of a kitchen. While cabinet construction is generally hidden, the door style is the most visible feature and largely determines whether a kitchen feels modern, traditional, luxury, or contemporary.

## Slab Door

The simplest and cleanest kitchen door — a completely flat door with no frame or decorative detailing.

**Best for:** Modern kitchens · contemporary homes · apartments · minimalist interiors.

**Advantages:** Easy to clean · modern appearance · affordable · large colour selection.

**Available finishes:** Matt · High Gloss · Satin · Wood Grain · Painted · Acrylic.

## Handleless Door

A modern door designed without traditional handles.

**Types:** True Handleless · J-Pull · Rail Handle System · Finger Pull.

**Advantages:** Minimal appearance · easy cleaning · modern styling · safe for family homes.

Popular in luxury contemporary kitchens.

## J-Pull Door

The handle is machined directly into the top of the door.

**Advantages:** No handles required · lower cost than true handleless · modern appearance.

## Shaker Door

The world's most popular kitchen door style — a framed door with a recessed centre panel.

**Characteristics:** Timeless design · suitable for almost every home · works in traditional and modern kitchens.

**Popular colours:** White · Sage Green · Navy · Cashmere · Grey · Black.

## Slim Shaker Door

A modern version of the traditional shaker with a narrow frame, cleaner appearance, contemporary styling. One of today's fastest-growing kitchen styles.

## Raised Panel Door

Traditional cabinet door with a raised centre panel. Elegant and decorative.

**Best for:** Period homes · luxury traditional kitchens · large country houses.

## Flat Panel Door

Frame with a completely flat centre panel. Simpler than raised panel · traditional but clean · easy maintenance.

## In-Frame Door

The cabinet has a solid frame, and the door fits inside it.

**Advantages:** Premium construction · excellent durability · high-end appearance · traditional craftsmanship.

Often found in bespoke kitchens.

## Beaded Shaker

Traditional shaker with decorative beading around the inner frame.

**Best for:** Victorian homes · Georgian homes · country kitchens.

## Tongue & Groove Door

Vertical grooves machined into the centre panel. Cottage · farmhouse · traditional · coastal.

## V-Groove Door

Fine V-shaped grooves create subtle shadow lines. Popular in Scandinavian kitchens.

## Fluted Door

Vertical rounded grooves running the full height. Luxury appearance · adds texture · modern feature kitchens. Growing rapidly in premium interiors.

## Reeded Door

Fine ribbed pattern giving a sophisticated textured finish. Often paired with bronze or brass handles.

## Glass Door

Cabinet doors fitted with glass panels. Glass types: Clear · Frosted · Reeded · Smoked · Bronze · Black tinted. Often used in display cabinets.

## Aluminium Frame Door

Slim aluminium frame with glass centre. Popular in contemporary luxury kitchens.

## Timber Veneer Door

Real timber veneer bonded to engineered board. Species: Oak · Walnut · Ash · Elm · Smoked Oak. Provides natural wood appearance with excellent stability.

## Solid Timber Door

Manufactured from solid hardwood. Timbers: Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple · Cherry. Premium handmade kitchens often use solid timber doors.

## Painted MDF Door

One of the most common premium painted kitchen doors. Smooth finish · stable construction · excellent paint quality · wide colour range.

## High Gloss Door

Reflective polished finish. Best for modern apartments · contemporary homes · small kitchens. Creates a bright spacious feel.

## Matt Door

Low-reflection finish. Modern appearance · fingerprints less visible · soft elegant finish. Very popular in current kitchen designs.

## Super Matt Door

Ultra-low sheen finish. Often incorporates anti-fingerprint technology.

## Acrylic Door

High-quality acrylic bonded to MDF. Deep gloss · excellent colour consistency · durable finish · UV resistant.

## Fenix Door

Advanced nanotechnology surface. Anti-fingerprint · scratch resistant · soft-touch finish · heat repair technology on minor scratches. Popular in premium handleless kitchens.

## Kitchen Door Opening Systems

Traditional Handles · Bar Handles · Knob Handles · Cup Handles · Edge Pull Handles · J-Pull · Finger Pull · Push-to-Open · Tip-On · Servo Drive (electric opening).

## Door Construction Materials

MDF · Moisture Resistant MDF · Solid Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple · Birch · Plywood · Veneered MDF · Melamine Faced Board · Acrylic · Glass · Aluminium · Composite Panels.

## Popular Kitchen Door Colours

White · Cashmere · Ivory · Stone · Dove Grey · Dust Grey · Graphite · Black · Navy Blue · Forest Green · Sage Green · Olive Green · Oak · Walnut · Smoked Oak · Concrete Grey.

## Choosing the Right Door

Consider: budget · home style · desired maintenance level · colour preferences · handle or handleless design · durability requirements · household size · presence of children or pets · desired lifespan · matching worktops and flooring.
