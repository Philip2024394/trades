# Kitchen Kick Boards (Plinth Boards)

A kitchen kick board (plinth board) is fitted at the bottom of base cabinets to conceal cabinet legs · plumbing · wiring · the recessed toe space.

Kick boards protect the cabinet base from dirt · moisture · accidental damage. This article covers ventilation requirements and installation methods. For the comprehensive plinth type catalogue see `kitchen-plinth-types.md`.

## Purpose of a Kick Board

A correctly fitted kick board will hide adjustable cabinet legs · conceal plumbing and pipework · hide electrical cables · protect cabinet bases · create a seamless kitchen appearance · improve hygiene by preventing dust build-up · complete the visual design.

## Kick Board Ventilation

Some kick boards include integrated ventilation grilles. **Required for:** Integrated refrigerators · Wine coolers · Freezers · Heat-producing appliances. Maintains airflow for safe appliance operation.

Without ventilation, integrated cooling appliances will overheat and fail prematurely.

## Installation Methods

Kick boards are commonly installed using: Clip-on plinth clips · Screw-fixed brackets · Magnetic fixing systems · Push-fit retaining clips.

They should be removable to allow access to plumbing, electrical services, and adjustable cabinet legs.

## Design Considerations

Consider: Cabinet leg height · Flooring thickness · Appliance ventilation · Moisture exposure · Colour matching · Lighting integration · Ease of future removal · Overall kitchen style.

## Nex Guidance

When advising users, recommend a kick board that complements the cabinet doors and worktop while also meeting practical needs such as moisture resistance · ventilation · easy access for future maintenance. Matching the kick board finish to the cabinetry creates a seamless look, while contrasting finishes such as brushed aluminium or stainless steel can be used to add a modern design feature.
