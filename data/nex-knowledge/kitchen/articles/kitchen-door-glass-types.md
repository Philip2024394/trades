# Kitchen Door Glass Types

Glass kitchen doors add light · depth · visual interest. They are commonly used on wall units · display cabinets · dresser units · islands · feature cabinets. Different glass types offer varying visibility · privacy · decorative style.

## Clear Glass

Completely transparent. Displays cabinet contents · brightens the kitchen · timeless appearance. Best for display cabinets · glass wall units · luxury kitchens.

## Ultra Clear Glass (Low Iron Glass)

Premium crystal-clear glass with reduced green tint. Exceptional clarity · true colour visibility · luxury appearance. Often used in high-end kitchens.

## Frosted Glass

Acid-etched or sandblasted to create a translucent finish. Provides privacy · softens cabinet contents · modern appearance · diffuses light. Popular in contemporary kitchens.

## Satin Glass

Smooth frosted appearance. Elegant finish · soft light transmission · easy to coordinate. Suitable for modern and minimalist kitchens.

## Etched Glass

Decorative patterns permanently etched into the surface. Designs: floral · geometric · traditional · contemporary. Ideal for feature cabinets.

## Sandblasted Glass

Glass surface blasted with fine abrasive. Frosted appearance · decorative finish · excellent privacy.

## Reeded Glass (Fluted Glass)

Vertical ribbed texture. Partial visibility · elegant appearance · vintage styling · softens cabinet contents. Extremely popular in modern luxury kitchens.

## Fluted Glass

Similar to reeded glass with deeper vertical grooves. Creates beautiful reflections and texture.

## Ribbed Glass

Textured surface providing limited visibility. Ideal for Shaker and contemporary kitchens.

## Seeded Glass

Contains tiny decorative bubbles. Best for country · farmhouse · traditional cabinetry. Adds handcrafted character.

## Antique Glass

Decorative aged appearance. Wavy surface · slight imperfections · vintage styling.

## Smoked Glass

Grey or bronze tinted glass. Luxury appearance · reduces visibility · modern styling. Very popular in designer kitchens.

## Bronze Glass

Warm bronze tint. Creates a sophisticated feature cabinet.

## Grey Glass

Neutral smoked appearance. Complements black kitchens · graphite kitchens · industrial interiors.

## Black Glass

Opaque or heavily tinted. Provides a sleek modern appearance.

## White Glass

Gloss white decorative glass. Popular in contemporary kitchens.

## Coloured Glass

Available in almost any colour: White · Black · Sage Green · Navy Blue · Blue · Red · Grey · Cream. Can match kitchen doors and splashbacks.

## Painted Glass

Back-painted toughened glass. High gloss finish · easy cleaning · colour matched.

## Mirror Glass

Reflective surface. Makes kitchens appear larger · reflects light · luxury appearance. Popular in display cabinets.

## Antique Mirror Glass

Decorative aged mirror finish. Suitable for traditional · luxury · boutique designs.

## Textured Glass

Decorative patterned surface. Examples: Linen · Wave · Rain · Hammered · Organic textures. Provides privacy while allowing light through.

## Patterned Glass

Decorative designs pressed into the glass. Available in numerous styles.

## Leaded Glass

Traditional glass with decorative lead cames. Best for country · traditional · period properties.

## Stained Glass

Coloured decorative glass panels. Handmade appearance · traditional craftsmanship · decorative feature. Often used in bespoke kitchens.

## Bevelled Glass

Glass edges polished at an angle. Creates attractive light reflections. Popular in classic kitchens.

## Toughened Safety Glass

Heat-treated safety glass. Stronger than standard glass · safer if broken · required for many kitchen applications. **Recommended for all kitchen cabinet glazing.**

## Laminated Glass

Two sheets of glass bonded together. Improved safety · reduced risk of shattering · noise reduction.

## Double Glazed Cabinet Glass

Rarely used but available for specialist cabinetry. Provides improved insulation.

## Glass Frame Styles

Glass can be fitted into Shaker doors · in-frame doors · aluminium frames · timber frames · slim contemporary frames · traditional moulded frames.

## Internal Cabinet Lighting

Glass doors are often paired with LED shelves · glass shelves · spot lighting · strip lighting · sensor lighting. Lighting enhances displayed items and creates a premium appearance.

## Choosing the Right Glass

Consider: kitchen style · display or concealed storage · privacy requirements · lighting design · cabinet contents · safety requirements · cleaning preferences · colour coordination · budget.

## Nex Guidance

When recommending glass kitchen doors, first determine whether the user wants to display cabinet contents or conceal them. Clear and ultra-clear glass are ideal for showcasing glassware and decorative items · frosted, satin, and reeded glass provide privacy without blocking light. For traditional kitchens, stained · leaded · seeded glass complements classic cabinetry, whereas fluted · smoked · bronze · ultra-clear glass are excellent choices for contemporary and luxury kitchen designs. For safety and durability, **toughened safety glass should always be recommended** for kitchen cabinet doors.
