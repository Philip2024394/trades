# Kitchen Cabinet Carcass Sizes and Cabinet Types

The kitchen carcass is the structural cabinet box that supports doors, drawers, worktops, and appliances. A well-designed carcass provides strength, storage, and a consistent layout throughout the kitchen.

## Standard Base Cabinet (Floor Unit)

The most common kitchen cabinet.

- **Height (without worktop):** 720 mm
- **Height with adjustable legs:** 870-900 mm
- **Depth:** 560 mm
- **Depth including door:** 580-600 mm
- **Widths:** 150 · 200 · 300 · 400 · 450 · 500 · 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 · 1200 mm

**Uses:** Cupboards · drawers · sink units · hob units · corner cabinets.

## Wall Cabinets

Mounted above the worktop.

- **Height:** 360 · 575 · 720 · 900 · 1080 mm
- **Depth:** 300 · 330 mm
- **Widths:** 300 · 400 · 450 · 500 · 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 mm

**Uses:** Glass cabinets · plate storage · food storage · display cabinets.

## Tall Larder Units

Full-height cabinets extending from floor to near ceiling.

- **Height:** 1970 · 2120 · 2300 mm
- **Depth:** 560 mm
- **Widths:** 300 · 400 · 500 · 600 mm

**Uses:** Pantry storage · pull-out larders · broom cupboards.

## Oven Housing Units

Designed for built-in ovens and microwaves.

- **Height:** 1970-2300 mm
- **Width:** 600 mm
- **Depth:** 560 mm

May also include warming drawer · microwave · storage cupboards.

## Fridge Housing Units

Designed around integrated refrigeration.

- **Width:** 600 mm
- **Height:** To suit appliance
- **Depth:** 560 mm

## Drawer Units

Base cabinets fitted with drawers. Common widths: 300 · 400 · 450 · 500 · 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 mm.

## Sink Base Units

Designed for kitchen sinks. Common sizes: 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 mm. Usually supplied without a fixed shelf to allow space for plumbing.

## Corner Cabinets

Maximise storage in kitchen corners.

**Types:** L-Shaped Corner · Blind Corner · Diagonal Corner · LeMans Pull-Out · Magic Corner · Carousel Corner.

## Island Cabinets

Used to form a kitchen island. Can include storage · seating · hob · sink · wine cooler · dishwasher. Depths vary depending on design.

## Appliance Cabinets

Specially designed for dishwashers · washing machines · tumble dryers · wine coolers · coffee machines.

## Open Shelving Units

No doors fitted. Used for books · decorative items · plants · display storage.

## End Display Units

Decorative open shelving fitted at the end of cabinet runs.

## Base Cabinet Shapes

- **Straight Cabinet** — Standard rectangular cabinet · most common kitchen unit.
- **Corner Cabinet** — Designed for 90° corners · available in several storage configurations.
- **Curved Cabinet** — Curved end panels or doors · provides softer transitions.
- **Angled Cabinet** — Used where walls are not square or for bespoke layouts.
- **Radius Cabinet** — Curved front with rounded edges · premium bespoke kitchens.
- **Diagonal Cabinet** — 45-degree cabinet fitted into corners · often supports corner sinks or hobs.

## Wall Cabinet Shapes

Straight · Corner · Curved · Glass Display · Open Shelf · Lift-Up Door · Bi-Fold Door.

## Cabinet Construction

Most carcasses are manufactured using: 18 mm MFC (Melamine Faced Chipboard) · Moisture Resistant MDF · Plywood · Birch Ply · Furniture Board. Premium manufacturers often use thicker backs and stronger fixing systems.

## Cabinet Back Panels

Typical thickness: 6 · 8 · 10 · 18 mm. Thicker backs improve cabinet rigidity.

## Shelving

Adjustable shelves usually use 18 mm board supported by shelf pins or locking shelf supports.

## Cabinet Legs

Adjustable plastic legs. Typical adjustment: 100 or 150 mm. Covered using removable plinth boards.

## Plinth Heights

Common sizes: 100 or 150 mm.

## Service Void

Many cabinets include a rear service void, typical depth 35-60 mm. Allows space for pipes · electrical cables · services.

## Common Reference Tables

**Widths:** 150 · 200 · 300 · 400 · 450 · 500 · 600 · 800 · 900 · 1000 · 1200 mm.

**Heights:** 360 · 575 · 720 · 900 · 1970 · 2120 · 2300 mm.

**Depths:** 300 · 330 · 560 · 580 · 600 mm.

## Nex Design Considerations

When recommending cabinet layouts, consider: room dimensions · ceiling height · door and window positions · appliance sizes · plumbing and electrical services · user storage requirements · accessibility · family size · workflow between sink hob and fridge · future flexibility for appliance replacement.

**Note:** Cabinet sizes can vary slightly between manufacturers. Treat these as typical industry dimensions and, when helping users order or replace cabinets, recommend confirming the exact sizes from the chosen kitchen manufacturer before purchase or installation.
