# Kitchen Plinth Types

A kitchen plinth (kickboard) is the panel fitted below base cabinets to conceal adjustable cabinet legs · plumbing · wiring · the gap between the cabinet and the floor. Plinths provide a clean, finished appearance while protecting the underside of kitchen cabinets from dust, dirt, and moisture.

## Standard Plinth

The most common plinth in fitted kitchens. Straight panel · clips onto adjustable legs · matches cabinet colour · easy to remove for maintenance. Typical heights: 100 or 150 mm.

## Recessed Plinth

Set back beneath the cabinet doors to create a toe-kick. Comfortable standing position · prevents toes striking the cabinet · clean modern appearance. Used in almost all modern kitchens.

## Flush Plinth

The plinth aligns with the cabinet face. Furniture-style appearance · traditional design · premium finish. Often used in in-frame kitchens.

## Curved Plinth

Manufactured to follow curved cabinets or island ends. Best for curved islands · radius cabinets · bespoke kitchens. Provides a seamless appearance around rounded units.

## Flexible Plinth

Made from flexible materials to follow curved layouts. Common in bespoke kitchen installations.

## Aluminium Plinth

Constructed from brushed or polished aluminium. Contemporary appearance · moisture resistant · easy to clean · durable. Popular in modern and industrial kitchens.

## Stainless Steel Plinth

Premium metal plinth. Commercial appearance · hygienic · water resistant · hard wearing. Often specified for luxury or professional-style kitchens.

## Mirror Plinth

Reflective finish creating the illusion that cabinets float. Luxury appearance · makes rooms feel larger · creates a striking feature. Common in designer kitchens.

## LED Illuminated Plinth

LED strip lighting installed behind or beneath the plinth. Soft ambient lighting · night-time guidance · premium appearance · floating cabinet effect. Available in Warm White · Cool White · RGB Colour Changing · Smart Lighting Systems.

## Timber Plinth

Manufactured from solid timber or timber veneer. Common timbers: Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple. Provides a natural furniture-style finish.

## Painted MDF Plinth

One of the most common premium options. Smooth finish · colour matched to doors · easy to replace.

## Melamine Plinth

Made from Melamine Faced Chipboard (MFC). Affordable · durable · scratch resistant · easy cleaning. The industry standard for most fitted kitchens.

## Waterproof PVC Plinth

Manufactured from PVC. Completely waterproof · moisture resistant · easy cleaning · suitable near sinks.

## Stone Effect Plinth

Decorative finish matching stone worktops. Popular finishes: Marble · Concrete · Slate · Granite.

## Handleless Rail Plinth

Designed for true handleless kitchens. Works alongside aluminium rail systems for a seamless appearance.

## Furniture Style Plinth

Decorative moulded plinth resembling freestanding furniture. Common in country · farmhouse · traditional kitchens.

## Decorative Moulded Plinth

Includes routed profiles or decorative detailing. Often paired with cornices · pilasters · decorative end panels.

## Floating Plinth

Plinth recessed deeply with integrated LED lighting to create the illusion of floating cabinets. Very popular in luxury contemporary kitchens.

## Common Plinth Heights

100 · 120 · 150 · 170 · 200 mm (bespoke).

## Common Plinth Thicknesses

16 · 18 · 19 mm.

## Common Plinth Materials

MFC · MR-MDF · Solid Oak · Walnut · Ash · Birch Plywood · PVC · Aluminium · Stainless Steel · Acrylic · Veneered MDF.

## Common Plinth Colours

White · Cashmere · Stone Grey · Dust Grey · Graphite · Black · Oak · Walnut · Sage Green · Navy Blue · Anthracite · Stainless Steel · Aluminium.

## Plinth Ventilation

Some plinths include ventilation grilles for integrated refrigerators · wine coolers · freezers · appliances requiring airflow. Ventilation openings should never be blocked, as this can reduce appliance performance and may lead to overheating.

## Choosing the Right Plinth

Consider: kitchen style · cabinet construction · flooring type · moisture exposure · lighting requirements · appliance ventilation · budget · colour coordination · whether a floating or furniture-style appearance is desired.
