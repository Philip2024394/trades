# Kitchen Plumbing

Kitchen plumbing is the network of water supply · waste drainage · valves · fittings · appliances that deliver clean water and remove wastewater safely and efficiently.

## Hot Water Supply

Provides heated water to kitchen sink · instant boiling water tap · dishwasher · secondary sinks · utility sink. Common pipe materials: Copper · PEX · Multilayer Composite Pipe (MLCP).

## Cold Water Supply

Provides fresh mains water to sink · filter tap · fridge water dispenser · ice maker · boiling water tap · dishwasher.

## Waste Water System

Carries wastewater from sink · dishwasher · washing machine · instant hot tap · water softener.

## Kitchen Sink Waste

Standard waste outlet fitted beneath the sink. Common sizes: 40 · 50 mm. May include basket strainer · plug waste · pop-up waste.

## Bottle Trap

Compact trap beneath the sink. Space saving · easy cleaning · modern appearance. Common in kitchen cabinets.

## P Trap

Traditional waste trap. Prevents sewer gases · reliable · easy maintenance. One of the most widely used traps.

## S Trap

Similar to a P trap but exits vertically through the floor. Less common in modern kitchens.

## Waste Trap

Every sink should include a water trap. Prevents odours · stops sewer gases · maintains hygiene.

## Basket Strainer Waste

Built into the sink. Filters food waste · protects pipework · easy cleaning. Very common on stainless steel sinks.

## Pop-Up Waste

Controlled by a lever. Allows easy opening and closing of the sink waste.

## Overflow System

Safety outlet preventing sink overflow. Standard on most modern sinks.

## Stop Valve (Isolation Valve)

Allows water to individual appliances to be isolated. Commonly fitted to sink taps · dishwasher · fridge water supply · boiling water tap. Essential for future maintenance.

## Main Stopcock

Controls the main water supply entering the property. Should remain easily accessible.

## Flexible Tap Connectors

Braided flexible hoses connecting taps to water supplies. Easy installation · reduces vibration · suitable for modern taps.

## Copper Pipework

Traditional plumbing material. Long lifespan · heat resistant · proven reliability.

## PEX Pipe

Flexible plastic plumbing pipe. Easy installation · corrosion resistant · fewer joints. Increasingly common in modern homes.

## Multilayer Composite Pipe

Combines plastic and aluminium. Flexible · strong · low expansion · long lifespan.

## Push-Fit Plumbing

Quick-connect plumbing fittings. Fast installation · no soldering · easy maintenance.

## Compression Fittings

Mechanical plumbing joints. Common with copper pipe installations.

## Appliance Connections

- **Dishwasher** — cold water feed · waste connection · isolation valve.
- **Washing machine** — cold water supply · waste standpipe or trap connection · isolation valve.
- **Boiling water tap** — cold water connection · hot water tank (depending on model) · electrical supply · filter system.
- **Ice maker** — cold water feed · isolation valve · water filter (recommended).
- **Refrigerator water supply** — dedicated water connection for ice dispensers · chilled water.

## Water Filter System

Improves drinking water quality. May reduce chlorine · sediment · limescale · odours.

## Water Softener

Installed in hard water areas. Reduces limescale · protects appliances · improves tap lifespan.

## Waste Disposal Unit

Installed beneath the sink. Grinds food waste · reduces bin waste · convenient cleaning. Requires compatible plumbing and electrical connections.

## Vent Pipe (Air Admittance)

Helps prevent negative pressure in the drainage system. Improves waste flow and prevents trap siphonage.

## Kitchen Plumbing Accessories

Isolation valves · flexible hoses · pipe clips · compression fittings · push-fit connectors · waste kits · traps · overflow kits · hose connectors · appliance valves.

## Leak Detection

Modern kitchens may include smart leak sensors · automatic shut-off valves · water monitoring systems · flood alarms. These help minimise water damage.

## Plumbing Design Considerations

Sink location · appliance locations · hot and cold water supplies · drainage route · water pressure · future maintenance access · pipe insulation · water filtration · hard water conditions · compliance with local plumbing regulations.

## Common Plumbing Layout

Typical kitchen plumbing includes: hot water pipe · cold water pipe · sink mixer tap · isolation valves · flexible tap connectors · bottle or P trap · waste pipe · dishwasher connection · washing machine connection · water filter · overflow · main waste outlet.

## Recommended Plumbing Systems

| Kitchen Type | Recommended Plumbing |
|---|---|
| Modern | PEX or Multilayer Pipe with Isolation Valves |
| Contemporary | Hidden Pipework with Bottle Trap |
| Traditional | Copper Pipework with Chrome Traps |
| Luxury | Boiling Water Tap · Water Filter · Leak Detection |
| Family Kitchen | Water Softener · Dishwasher Connection · Deep Sink |
| Utility Kitchen | Twin Appliance Connections · Heavy-Duty Waste System |

## Nex Guidance

When planning a kitchen, recommend plumbing layouts that minimise long pipe runs · keep isolation valves accessible · provide adequate space beneath the sink for traps and appliance connections · allow for future maintenance. If the kitchen includes boiling water taps · integrated coffee machines · American-style fridge freezers · water softeners · or waste disposal units, ensure that the required water · waste · electrical services are incorporated into the design at the planning stage rather than added later.
