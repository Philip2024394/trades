# Kitchen Splashback Types

A kitchen splashback is the wall surface installed behind worktops · hobs · sinks to protect the wall from water · grease · food splashes · heat. Splashbacks are also an important design feature.

## Ceramic Tile Splashback

Affordable · easy to clean · heat resistant · huge choice of colours and patterns. Best for traditional · Shaker · family homes.

## Porcelain Tile Splashback

Manufactured from dense porcelain. Very durable · low water absorption · scratch resistant · heat resistant. Suitable for busy kitchens.

## Metro Tiles (Subway Tiles)

Rectangular brick-style tiles. Popular sizes: 100 × 200 mm · 75 × 300 mm. Styles: Horizontal · Vertical · Herringbone · Stacked · Brick Bond. One of the most timeless kitchen splashback designs.

## Large Format Tiles

Large porcelain or ceramic tiles with minimal grout lines. Modern appearance · easy cleaning · fewer grout joints. Popular in contemporary kitchens.

## Mosaic Tiles

Small tiles mounted on mesh sheets. Materials: Glass · Stone · Ceramic · Metal. Ideal for feature areas.

## Glass Splashback

Manufactured from toughened safety glass. Seamless appearance · hygienic · heat resistant · easy cleaning · available in any colour. Popular in modern kitchens.

## Acrylic Splashback

Lightweight decorative plastic panel. Affordable · lightweight · easy installation · available in many colours · gloss or matt finish. Less heat resistant than glass · not recommended directly behind high-output gas burners unless manufacturer-approved clearances are followed.

## Stainless Steel Splashback

Commercial-style metal panel. Hygienic · heat resistant · easy cleaning · professional appearance · extremely durable. Popular behind cooking areas.

## Aluminium Splashback

Lightweight metal alternative. Modern appearance · corrosion resistant · easy installation.

## Copper Splashback

Luxury metal finish. Unique appearance · develops natural patina · premium feature. Often used in bespoke kitchens.

## Brass Splashback

Decorative luxury finish. Suitable for designer kitchens · boutique interiors.

## Stone Splashback

Manufactured from natural stone: Granite · Marble · Slate · Limestone · Travertine. Creates a luxury appearance.

## Quartz Splashback

Made from engineered quartz. Often matches the kitchen worktop. Seamless appearance · durable · easy maintenance.

## Porcelain Slab Splashback

Large porcelain panels. Minimal joints · heat resistant · scratch resistant · luxury appearance.

## Solid Surface Splashback

Acrylic solid surface materials (Corian · HI-MACS · Staron). Can be seamlessly joined to matching worktops.

## Laminate Splashback

Matches laminate worktops. Affordable · coordinated appearance · easy installation. Suitable for budget-friendly kitchens.

## Timber Splashback

Solid timber or veneered panels. Common timbers: Oak · Walnut · Ash. Requires suitable sealing and is generally better suited away from direct heat and constant water exposure.

## Brick Effect Splashback

Decorative brick-style panels or tiles. Popular in industrial · rustic interiors.

## Concrete Effect Splashback

Industrial contemporary appearance. Available in Porcelain · Laminate · Acrylic · Composite panels.

## Mirror Splashback

Reflective glass or mirror finish. Makes kitchens feel larger · reflects light · luxury appearance.

## Printed Glass Splashback

Custom printed images sealed behind toughened glass. Designs: city skylines · nature scenes · abstract artwork · marble effects.

## Matching Worktop Splashback

The splashback uses the same material as the worktop. Popular materials: Quartz · Granite · Porcelain · Solid Surface · Laminate. Creates a continuous designer look.

## Popular Finishes

Matt · Satin · Gloss · Textured · Brushed Metal · Polished Stone · Honed Stone.

## Common Colours

White · Black · Grey · Cashmere · Beige · Marble Effect · Concrete Effect · Oak Effect · Sage Green · Navy Blue · Stainless Steel · Copper · Brass.

## Recommended Splashbacks

| Kitchen Style | Recommended Splashback |
|---|---|
| Modern | Glass · Quartz · Porcelain Slab |
| Contemporary | Large Format Tiles · Acrylic · Glass |
| Traditional | Ceramic Tiles · Metro Tiles |
| Shaker | Metro Tiles · Stone · Painted Glass |
| Farmhouse | Ceramic · Stone · Brick Effect |
| Scandinavian | White Glass · Porcelain · Oak Effect |
| Industrial | Stainless Steel · Concrete Effect · Brick Effect |
| Luxury | Bookmatched Porcelain · Quartz · Marble · Mirror Glass |

## Design Considerations

Consider: kitchen style · worktop material · heat resistance · ease of cleaning · grout maintenance · moisture exposure · colour coordination · budget · lighting reflections · overall durability.
