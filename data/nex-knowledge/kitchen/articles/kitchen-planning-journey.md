# Where to Start When Planning Your Kitchen

Planning a new kitchen can seem overwhelming, but by following a structured process you can create a kitchen that suits your home, lifestyle, and budget. The first step is not choosing cabinet colours or worktops — it is understanding how the kitchen will be used.

## Step 1 – Understand Your Needs

Ask yourself:

- Why am I replacing my kitchen?
- What don't I like about my current kitchen?
- What works well?
- How many people use the kitchen?
- Do I cook every day?
- Do I entertain guests?
- Do I need a family space?
- Do I work from the kitchen?

Your answers will shape every design decision.

## Step 2 – Measure the Room

Accurate measurements are essential.

**Record:** Room length · Room width · Ceiling height · Window positions · Door positions · Radiators · Soil pipes · Boilers · Electrical consumer unit · Water supplies · Waste pipes.

**Also note:** Chimney breasts · sloping ceilings · structural columns · beams · bulkheads.

## Step 3 – Decide Your Budget

Typical budget categories: Economy · Mid-range · Premium · Luxury · Bespoke.

Remember to allow for: Cabinets · Worktops · Appliances · Plumbing · Electrical work · Flooring · Decorating · Installation · Contingency.

## Step 4 – Choose Your Kitchen Style

Think about the overall appearance you want: Modern · Contemporary · Shaker · Traditional · Farmhouse · Scandinavian · Industrial · Handleless · Classic · Luxury.

Collect inspiration from magazines, websites, or photos you like.

## Step 5 – Choose a Layout

Select the layout that best suits your room: Straight · Galley · L-shaped · U-shaped · Peninsula · Island · Double island · Open-plan. See `kitchen-layouts.md` for the full selection guide.

## Step 6 – Think About Storage

Good storage makes a kitchen enjoyable to use.

Consider: Pan drawers · Corner storage · Larder units · Spice storage · Recycling bins · Tall cupboards · Open shelving · Plate storage · Cutlery drawers · Appliance garages.

## Step 7 – Plan Your Appliances

Decide which appliances you need: Oven · Microwave · Induction hob · Gas hob · Extractor · Dishwasher · Fridge freezer · Wine cooler · Coffee machine · Warming drawer.

Knowing your appliance choices early allows the cabinetry to be designed around them.

## Step 8 – Select Your Worktop

Choose a worktop based on appearance · durability · budget · maintenance · heat resistance.

Popular choices: Quartz · Granite · Porcelain · Solid timber · Solid surface · Laminate · Compact laminate. See `kitchen-worktop-types.md` for the complete guide.

## Step 9 – Choose Cabinet Doors

Think about door style · colour · finish · handle type · glass doors · internal lighting. Your cabinet doors define much of the kitchen's visual character. See `kitchen-door-types.md` and `kitchen-door-styles.md`.

## Step 10 – Plan Lighting

Good lighting transforms a kitchen. Include: Ceiling lighting · Under-cabinet lighting · Pendant lights · Plinth lighting · Feature lighting · Inside cabinet lighting.

## Step 11 – Flooring

Choose flooring that complements the kitchen: Luxury Vinyl Tile (LVT) · Engineered wood · Porcelain tiles · Ceramic tiles · Laminate · Natural stone. Consider durability · maintenance · slip resistance.

## Step 12 – Plumbing and Electrical Services

Plan service locations before installation.

**Plumbing:** Sink position · Dishwasher · Boiling water tap · Water filter.

**Electrical:** Electrical sockets · USB charging · Appliance supplies · Lighting controls.

Planning services early helps avoid costly changes later.

## Step 13 – Choose Finishing Details

Complete the design with handles · splashbacks · end panels · plinths · cornices · pelmets · decorative mouldings · internal accessories. These details create the finished look.

## Step 14 – Installation

A successful installation includes:

1. Removing the old kitchen
2. Preparing walls and floors
3. First-fix plumbing and electrics
4. Cabinet installation
5. Worktop fitting
6. Appliance installation
7. Splashback fitting
8. Second-fix plumbing and electrics
9. Decoration
10. Final inspection

## Step 15 – Enjoy Your New Kitchen

Once complete: Register appliance warranties · Learn how to care for your worktops · Clean and protect timber surfaces · Adjust hinges and drawers if required · Keep instruction manuals together. Regular maintenance will help your kitchen look its best for many years.

## The Nex Kitchen Journey (Conversational Flow)

Rather than asking hundreds of technical questions immediately, Nex should guide the user through a simple conversation:

1. **Tell me about your home** — Is it a house, flat, or extension?
2. **Tell me about your family** — How many people use the kitchen?
3. **How do you use your kitchen?** — Cooking, entertaining, baking, working from home?
4. **What style do you love?** — Modern, shaker, farmhouse, contemporary?
5. **What's your budget?** — This helps tailor recommendations.
6. **Let's design your layout** — Choose the most practical arrangement.
7. **Let's choose the details** — Doors, worktops, appliances, lighting, and finishes.

By leading users through these stages, Nex becomes more than a search tool — it acts like an experienced kitchen designer, building confidence and ensuring no important decisions are overlooked. This same guided journey can later be adapted for staircases · bedrooms · bathrooms · home offices · and other joinery projects.
