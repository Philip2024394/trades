# Where Should I Buy My Kitchen?

There is no single *"best"* place to buy a kitchen. The right choice depends on budget · level of customisation needed · quality expected · whether you want supply only or a complete design + manufacture + installation service. Nex should help users compare the different options before making a decision.

## DIY Stores

Examples: large home improvement retailers · DIY warehouse stores.

- **Best for:** budget kitchens · quick availability · simple layouts · DIY installation.
- **Advantages:** lower prices · easy to replace components · good stock availability.
- **Considerations:** limited custom sizes · fewer design options · quality varies.

## Kitchen Retailers

Dedicated kitchen showrooms.

- **Best for:** homeowners wanting professional design · mid-range to premium kitchens · complete supply and installation.
- **Advantages:** design service · large product range · professional planning · appliance packages.

## Bespoke Kitchen Manufacturers

Companies that manufacture kitchens to order.

- **Best for:** luxury homes · unique layouts · period properties · high-quality craftsmanship.
- **Advantages:** made to measure · premium materials · unlimited design flexibility · personal service.

## Local Joinery Companies

Many joinery workshops manufacture kitchens alongside other bespoke woodwork.

- **Best for:** custom kitchens · matching existing joinery · renovations · character homes.
- **Advantages:** flexible sizes · handmade quality · direct communication with the manufacturer.

## Staircase Manufacturers

Some bespoke staircase manufacturers also produce kitchens.

- **Advantages:** excellent woodworking skills · bespoke craftsmanship · matching staircases · kitchens · doors and panelling · consistent timber finishes.
- **Nex should check:** kitchen planning experience · cabinet hardware knowledge · appliance integration · installation team.

See `_shared/trade-business/articles/trade-capability-crossover.md` for the full capability assessment.

## Independent Kitchen Studios

Smaller design-led businesses. Personal service · creative designs · high-quality products · often supply premium manufacturers.

## Online Kitchen Suppliers

Supply kitchens directly online.

- **Advantages:** competitive prices · convenient ordering · large choice.
- **Considerations:** limited face-to-face support · accurate measurements are essential.

## Interior Design Companies

Some interior designers offer complete kitchen design services. Suitable for whole-home renovations · luxury homes · coordinated interior design.

## House Builders

Some new-build developers include kitchen options during construction.

## How to Compare Suppliers

When comparing kitchen suppliers, don't focus only on price. Consider: quality of cabinets · door construction · drawer systems · hinge quality · worktop options · design expertise · installation experience · warranty · after-sales service · lead time · customer reviews · ability to supply replacement parts.

## Questions to Ask Before Buying

- Is the kitchen made to standard sizes or fully bespoke?
- What material are the cabinet carcasses made from?
- Are the cabinet backs full thickness?
- Which hinges and drawer systems are used?
- Are soft-close fittings included?
- What worktop options are available?
- Is installation included?
- What warranty is provided?
- Can additional matching cabinets be ordered in the future?
- What happens if a door is damaged years later?

## Which Supplier is Right for Me?

| Your Priority | Best Option |
|---|---|
| Lowest price | DIY retailer |
| Good value | Kitchen retailer |
| Professional design | Kitchen showroom |
| Luxury finish | Bespoke kitchen manufacturer |
| Unique handmade kitchen | Local joinery company |
| Matching staircase and kitchen | Bespoke staircase / joinery company |
| Full home renovation | Interior design company |

## Nex Guidance

Rather than asking *"Where do you want to buy your kitchen?"*, ask:

1. What is your budget?
2. Do you want a standard or bespoke kitchen?
3. Are you renovating your whole home?
4. Would you like your kitchen to match your staircase · doors · flooring · wall panelling?
5. Do you need design only · supply only · or full installation?

By understanding the user's goals first, Nex can recommend the type of supplier that best fits their project, helping them make an informed decision instead of choosing based on price alone.

This approach also creates an opportunity for companies that manufacture staircases · doors · other bespoke joinery — they may be able to provide a complete coordinated interior solution rather than just a kitchen.
