# Kitchen Door and Cabinet Carcass Materials

The material used for kitchen doors and cabinet carcasses affects durability · appearance · moisture resistance · weight · cost · lifespan. Different materials are chosen depending on the quality level, budget, and intended use of the kitchen.

## Hardwood Species

- **Solid Oak** — One of the most popular premium hardwoods. Strong · durable · attractive natural grain · long lifespan · can be painted or stained. Best for Shaker · in-frame · traditional · luxury kitchens.
- **American White Oak** — Light golden colour · excellent stability · modern appearance · fine grain · very durable. Popular in Scandinavian and contemporary kitchens.
- **European Oak** — Strong · classic grain pattern · excellent for painted and stained finishes.
- **Walnut** — Luxury hardwood · rich dark brown · beautiful grain · premium appearance · excellent stability. Often found in designer kitchens.
- **American Black Walnut** — One of the highest quality furniture timbers · luxury veneer doors · bespoke kitchens · architectural interiors.
- **Ash** — Light-coloured hardwood · pronounced grain · strong · flexible · excellent for painted finishes · modern appearance.
- **Maple** — Fine-grained hardwood · smooth finish · very hard wearing · excellent paint finish. Common in premium painted kitchens.
- **Birch** — Strong hardwood · cabinet construction · plywood manufacture · drawer boxes · excellent dimensional stability.
- **Cherry** — Warm reddish hardwood · darkens naturally with age · fine grain · premium appearance. Often used in traditional kitchens.
- **Beech** — Hardwearing timber · smooth grain · durable · cost-effective hardwood. Suitable for cabinet components and doors.

## Softwood Species

- **Pine** — Softwood · affordable · easy to machine · traditional appearance. Popular in country and cottage kitchens.
- **Douglas Fir** — Strong structural softwood · occasionally used in bespoke kitchens.
- **Cedar** — Naturally aromatic timber · rarely used for kitchens but occasionally specified in bespoke furniture.

## Engineered Boards

- **MDF (Medium Density Fibreboard)** — The most common material for painted kitchen doors. Perfectly smooth surface · stable · no knots · excellent paint finish · easy machining. Common uses: Shaker doors · slab doors · painted kitchens.
- **Moisture Resistant MDF (MR-MDF)** — Special MDF for humid environments. Improved moisture resistance · better durability around sinks · ideal for kitchens and bathrooms.
- **HDF (High Density Fibreboard)** — Denser than MDF. Used for decorative panels · door inserts · furniture components.
- **Plywood** — Cross-laminated timber veneers. Very strong · stable · moisture resistant · excellent screw holding. Premium kitchens often use birch plywood carcasses.
- **Birch Plywood** — Premium plywood from birch veneers. High strength · attractive exposed edge · excellent durability. Popular in Scandinavian kitchens.
- **Marine Plywood** — Water-resistant plywood · used where maximum moisture resistance is required.

## Cabinet Carcass Materials

- **Particleboard (Chipboard)** — The most common cabinet carcass material. Affordable · stable · flat · good for mass production. Usually finished with melamine.
- **Melamine Faced Chipboard (MFC)** — The industry standard for cabinet carcasses. Scratch resistant · easy to clean · cost effective · available in many colours. Common thickness: 18 mm.
- **Veneered MDF** — MDF covered with real timber veneer · provides the appearance of solid timber with improved stability.
- **Veneered Plywood** — Real wood veneer bonded to plywood · often specified for luxury kitchens.

## Timber Veneers

Common veneer species: Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple · Cherry · Elm · Smoked Oak.

## Specialty Panels

- **Bamboo** — Rapidly renewable material. Sustainable · strong · modern appearance. Used for eco-friendly kitchens.
- **Fenix Panels** — High-performance engineered panels. Anti-fingerprint · soft-touch finish · scratch resistant · modern appearance. Popular in premium handleless kitchens.
- **Acrylic Panels** — High-gloss decorative boards. Mirror finish · UV stable · easy cleaning.
- **Laminate Panels** — Decorative laminate bonded to engineered board. Available in wood grain · stone effect · concrete effect · matt · gloss.

## Typical Kitchen Door Materials

Painted MDF · Moisture Resistant MDF · Solid Oak · Solid Walnut · Ash · Maple · Cherry · Pine · Timber Veneer · Acrylic · Fenix · Laminate · Glass · Aluminium Frame.

## Typical Cabinet Carcass Materials

18 mm Melamine Faced Chipboard (MFC) · Moisture Resistant MFC · Birch Plywood · Furniture Board · Plywood · MDF · Moisture Resistant MDF.

## Drawer Box Materials

Solid Oak · Birch Plywood · Beech · Metal drawer systems · Solid Timber · Laminated Board.

## Premium Kitchen Construction

High-end kitchens often feature: Birch plywood carcasses · Solid oak drawer boxes · Oak veneer interiors · Walnut veneer feature units · Moisture-resistant cabinet construction · Soft-close hardware throughout.

## Choosing the Right Material

Consider: budget · painted or natural wood finish · modern or traditional style · moisture exposure · durability requirements · sustainability preferences · expected lifespan · ease of maintenance · kitchen usage.
