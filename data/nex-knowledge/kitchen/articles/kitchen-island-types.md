# Kitchen Island Types

A kitchen island is a freestanding cabinet positioned away from the main kitchen units, providing additional worktop space · storage · seating · often housing appliances. An island is often the centrepiece of a modern kitchen.

## Standard Kitchen Island

The most common island design. Worktop on all sides · base cabinet storage · accessible from every direction · can include drawers and cupboards. Best for medium to large kitchens · open-plan living.

## Preparation Island

Designed primarily for food preparation. Large uninterrupted worktop · chopping area · storage for utensils · easy access from all sides. Ideal for keen home cooks.

## Cooking Island

Includes a built-in cooking appliance: induction hob · gas hob · ceramic hob · downdraft extractor · storage below. Creates a focal point while cooking.

## Sink Island

Features an integrated sink. Food preparation · washing vegetables · social cooking · easy workflow. Often includes dishwasher · waste bins · recycling storage.

## Breakfast Bar Island

Includes an overhanging worktop for seating. Seating capacity: 2 · 3 · 4 · 6+ people. Perfect for casual dining and family gatherings.

## Multi-Level Island

Features worktops at different heights. Cooking area · dining area · food preparation zone · decorative feature. Suitable for large kitchens.

## Waterfall Island

The worktop continues vertically down both ends of the island. Luxury appearance · modern design · continuous material finish. Popular with quartz · granite · porcelain · solid surface worktops.

## Furniture Style Island

Designed to resemble freestanding furniture. Decorative legs · end panels · cornices · pilasters · traditional detailing. Ideal for country and shaker kitchens.

## Curved Island

Rounded ends or fully curved cabinetry. Soft appearance · improved movement · bespoke design. Popular in luxury homes.

## Circular Island

Round island with seating around the perimeter. Suitable for large open-plan kitchens.

## Peninsula Island

Connected to the main kitchen rather than freestanding. Uses less space · additional worktop · extra storage · good for smaller kitchens.

## Mobile Island

Freestanding island on castors. Portable · flexible · compact · additional workspace. Ideal for smaller kitchens.

## Double Island

Two separate islands within one kitchen. One may be used for cooking while the other is used for dining or entertaining. Found in very large luxury kitchens.

## Entertaining Island

Designed for hosting guests. Seating · drinks storage · wine cooler · display shelving · charging points. Perfect for open-plan family homes.

## Island Storage Options

Drawers · cupboards · deep pan drawers · pull-out larders · wine racks · open shelving · plate storage · spice drawers · bin systems · recycling units.

## Island Appliances

Induction hob · gas hob · Teppanyaki plate · downdraft extractor · sink · dishwasher · wine cooler · microwave · warming drawer · fridge drawers · freezer drawers · ice maker.

## Island Seating

Breakfast bar · casual dining · bar stools · upholstered seating · bench seating.

**Recommended space per person:** 600 mm minimum · 650-700 mm for greater comfort.

## Common Island Sizes

**Width:** 600 · 900 · 1000 · 1200 · 1500 mm.
**Length:** 1200 · 1800 · 2400 · 3000 mm · bespoke sizes.

## Recommended Clearances

- **Minimum walkway:** 900 mm.
- **Comfortable walkway:** 1000-1200 mm.
- **Luxury kitchens:** 1200-1500 mm.

Adequate clearance allows cabinet doors, drawers, dishwashers and ovens to open without obstructing walkways.

## Popular Island Worktops

Quartz · Granite · Porcelain · Solid Surface · Solid Timber · Dekton · Marble · Laminate · Compact Laminate.

## Island Lighting

Pendant lights · linear LED fittings · spotlights · decorative chandeliers · recessed ceiling lights. Lighting should provide both task illumination and visual impact.

## Island End Panels

Shaker panels · tongue and groove panels · slatted timber panels · oak feature panels · fluted panels · painted panels · raised and fielded panels.

## Island Features

Pop-up sockets · USB charging · wireless charging pads · hidden storage · pull-out chopping boards · pet feeding stations · integrated bins · LED plinth lighting · display shelving.

Consider: kitchen size · number of users · cooking habits · dining requirements · appliance locations · walkway clearances · storage needs · plumbing and electrical services · lighting design · overall kitchen style.
