# The Staircase and Kitchen Relationship

In modern home design, the staircase is no longer hidden in a hallway. Instead, it often becomes part of the living space. When this happens, **the staircase should be designed as furniture and architecture — not simply as a means of getting upstairs**. The kitchen and staircase become the two largest bespoke joinery items in the house.

## Think of Them as One Project

Instead of asking *"What kitchen shall I buy?"*, ask *"How should my kitchen and staircase work together?"*

## Shared Design Language

### Timber

European Oak · American White Oak · Walnut · Ash · Maple · Sapele · Mahogany. Using the same timber creates visual continuity.

### Colours

Paint colours should coordinate. Examples: Kitchen painted Sage Green + Staircase painted Sage Green · Kitchen Natural Oak + Staircase Natural Oak · Black Kitchen + Black Staircase + Oak flooring.

### Mouldings

If the kitchen has Shaker doors → the staircase may use square newels · shaker panelling · simple strings · square handrails. If the kitchen is traditional → the staircase may include turned newels · moulded handrails · decorative strings · carved brackets.

### Flooring Connection

One floor can connect everything: Oak flooring → Kitchen → Dining → Hallway → Staircase → Living Room. No visual breaks.

### Wall Panelling

Panelling can continue through Hall → Staircase → Kitchen → Dining room. The house feels professionally designed.

### Lighting

Both should share colour temperature · brightness · control systems. Kitchen: LED cabinet lighting · pendant lighting · island lighting. Staircase: LED tread lighting · wall lights · handrail lighting.

### Metal Finishes

Choose one finish throughout: Brushed Brass · Satin Nickel · Matte Black · Stainless Steel · Bronze. Used on cabinet handles · staircase brackets · sockets · taps · light fittings · door handles.

### Glass

Kitchen glass cabinets · staircase glass balustrades · glazed internal doors. The home feels lighter.

### Stone

Use one stone. Quartz in kitchen worktop + splashback · staircase feature plinth + entrance floor · bathroom vanity tops.

## Modern Design

Floating staircase → Large kitchen island → Glass balustrade → Minimal doors → Hidden lighting → Open ceiling. Everything is minimalist.

## Traditional Design

Oak staircase → Shaker kitchen → Oak beams → Oak doors → Oak flooring → Panelled walls. Everything feels timeless.

## Open Plan Homes

Many self-builds now have Entrance → Feature staircase → Kitchen → Dining → Living all visible at once. The staircase and kitchen should therefore be designed together because they're VIEWED together.

## Customer Benefits

One designer · one survey · one quotation · one project manager · one installation programme · one finish · one maintenance guide. Instead of dealing with five different companies.

## Nex Design Rules (10)

1. Does the staircase match the kitchen?
2. Does the flooring connect both?
3. Does the wall panelling continue?
4. Do the timber species match?
5. Do the paint colours coordinate?
6. Are handles and metal finishes consistent?
7. Is the lighting designed together?
8. Are the moulding styles compatible?
9. Does the staircase complement the kitchen island?
10. Will someone standing at the front door see both?

**If yes to #10 → design them together.**

## The Future of Bespoke Joinery

One of the biggest opportunities for Nex is helping joinery companies expand beyond a single product. A workshop that already manufactures high-quality staircases often has the skills · machinery · finishing facilities · craftsmanship needed to produce staircases · kitchens · utility rooms · home offices · boot rooms · libraries · wardrobes · wine rooms · wall panelling · media walls · window seats · alcove furniture · internal doors · reception desks · commercial fit-outs.
