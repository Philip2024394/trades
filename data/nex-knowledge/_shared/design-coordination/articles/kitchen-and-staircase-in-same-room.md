# Can the Kitchen and Staircase be in the Same Room?

**Yes — and in modern homes they very often are.** One of the biggest trends in high-end home design is to make the staircase and kitchen part of one coordinated open-plan space.

## Why It Works

When the staircase and kitchen share the same room, they become the two main architectural features:

- The **staircase** creates the vertical focal point.
- The **kitchen** becomes the horizontal focal point.

Together they define the character of the entire ground floor.

## Benefits

### Complete Design Continuity

The same materials can be used throughout: Oak staircase · Oak kitchen · Oak flooring · Oak wall panelling · Oak doors. The whole house feels designed rather than assembled.

### Matching Finishes

Everything can share the same finish: Natural oak · Smoked oak · Walnut · Painted Shaker · Black stained oak · White lacquer · Hardwax oil · Satin lacquer.

### One Manufacturer

Many bespoke joinery companies can manufacture staircase · kitchen · utility room · media wall · home office · pantry · boot room · wall panelling · wine room — giving perfect colour matching · identical timber selection · matching mouldings · consistent craftsmanship · one installation programme.

### Open-Plan Living

Typical layout:

```
----------------------------
 Living Room

          Staircase

 Dining Area

      Kitchen Island
----------------------------
 Garden
```

The staircase becomes almost a piece of furniture.

## Design Ideas

The staircase can include glass balustrades · timber slatted walls · under-stair wine storage · pantry · hidden cupboards · coffee station · bookshelves · seating · LED lighting · display shelving.

## Match the Details

Nex should recommend matching:

- Cabinet doors ↔ staircase newel posts
- Kitchen handles ↔ staircase metalwork
- Worktop timber ↔ handrail timber
- Plinth shadow gaps ↔ staircase shadow gaps
- Wall panelling ↔ staircase panelling
- Oak flooring ↔ staircase treads

## Modern Homes

Today it's common to see kitchen · dining room · living room · staircase all within one large open-plan space. The staircase is designed as a feature, not hidden away in a hallway.

## Traditional Homes

Older houses often have separate hallway · separate staircase · separate kitchen · separate dining room. Many renovations remove walls to visually connect these spaces while retaining structural integrity.

## The Nex Design Principle

**"Design the staircase and kitchen together, not separately."**

If they occupy the same visual space, they should be considered one coordinated design project.

Nex should automatically check:

- ✓ Do the timber species match?
- ✓ Do the paint colours complement each other?
- ✓ Are the mouldings consistent?
- ✓ Is the hardware finish coordinated?
- ✓ Does the lighting style unify both features?
- ✓ Is the flooring continuous?
- ✓ Does the overall style (modern · Shaker · traditional · industrial etc.) remain consistent?

**This is where bespoke joinery companies have a major advantage over standard kitchen retailers:** a skilled joinery workshop can often design and manufacture the staircase · kitchen · wall panelling · utility room · fitted furniture · other interior joinery as one coordinated package — creating a home with a unified design language rather than a collection of unrelated products.
