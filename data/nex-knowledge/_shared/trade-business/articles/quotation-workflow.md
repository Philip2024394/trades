# Quotation Workflow

The quotation turns a site visit into a priced proposal. A good quotation wins the job; a bad quotation loses it — regardless of the underlying craftsmanship.

## Quote vs Estimate (matters legally)

- **Quotation** — a fixed price. Legally binding when accepted. Only issue a quotation when the specification is fully known.
- **Estimate** — an approximate price, subject to change. Use when the scope has unknowns.

Never blur the two. Customers expect the price on a "quotation" to be what they pay.

## The Anatomy of a Nex-Standard Quotation

Every quotation includes:

### Header
- Business name · logo · registered address · company number if applicable.
- Quotation number (sequential · unique).
- Date of issue.
- Valid-until date (typically 30 days).
- Customer's name + address + contact.

### Scope
- Clear one-paragraph description of what is being quoted.
- Reference to the site visit date if applicable.

### Line Items
Each with:
- Quantity.
- Description (specific enough that another tradesperson could execute it).
- Unit price.
- Line total.

### Optional Upgrades
- Listed separately with clear "adds" not "included" pricing.
- Customer can pick and choose.

### Notes / Assumptions
- What is included.
- What is NOT included (electrical works · plastering · decoration · flooring).
- Access requirements.
- Any customer responsibilities (clearing the room · providing electricity).

### Pricing Summary
- Subtotal.
- VAT (if registered).
- Total.

### Payment Terms
- Deposit percentage and when due.
- Interim payments (if staged).
- Final balance and when due.

### Delivery + Install Timeline
- Estimated manufacturing lead time.
- Estimated installation duration.
- Both dependent on customer confirming quote by X date.

### Signature Block
- Name + role + direct contact of the person issuing the quote.
- CTA: "Accept quote · reply YES · call {number}"

## The Rules of Good Quoting

### 1. Show the Working
Customers value transparency. A quote that shows 14 treads @ £110 · handrail 3.6m @ £48/m · install 1 day @ £550 reads more trustworthy than a lump sum.

### 2. Value First, Price Second
Lead with what the customer gets before the number. Every line item explains what value it delivers.

### 3. Never Bundle Uncertainty
If a scope has unknowns, ITEMISE them as "provisional sums" or exclude them entirely. Never hide uncertainty in a total.

### 4. Version Your Quotes
If a customer requests changes, issue Quotation 2 (not "revised") with a new number. Keep every version in the CRM for audit.

### 5. Follow Up Twice, Then Stop
Send quote → follow up in 7 days → follow up again in 14 days → stop. Chasing more than twice damages the relationship.

### 6. Valid-Until Matters
30-day validity is standard. Prices and lead times genuinely change; the valid-until date protects you AND signals urgency without being pushy.

### 7. Never Undercut Yourself
If a customer negotiates, be prepared to explain what changes if the price changes (specification · timeline · materials). Never simply drop the number — that signals the original was inflated.

## Handling Variations After the Quote

When the customer wants to change something after accepting:

1. Never do the work first and bill later.
2. Issue a **Variation Order** (VO) with:
   - What's changing.
   - Cost delta (+ or -).
   - Timeline delta.
   - New total.
3. Get written approval before starting the varied work.

Composes with the Variations / Change Orders shared process.

## Deposit Structure

Typical UK install trade deposit structure:

- **20-30% deposit** on acceptance (secures manufacturing slot).
- **60-70% interim** on delivery to site / start of install.
- **10% final** on completion + snag clearance.

Adjust per the trade — larger jobs may stage more finely.

## The Never-Guess Rule for Quoting

Composes with Nex Foundation Brain 14. Never guess:
- Material costs (verify current supplier prices).
- Labour days (base on actual measured scope).
- Delivery dates (check current supplier lead times).
- Regulatory costs (planning · Building Control · commissioning).

If any is unknown, either exclude it (provisional) or delay the quote until verified.

## Quote Presentation

Options in order of impressiveness:
1. **In person** — best conversion; use for jobs >£10k.
2. **Video call walk-through** — good middle ground; use for £2k-£10k.
3. **PDF emailed with a personal cover email** — standard for <£2k.

Never send a bare number by SMS or a supplier's own quote portal without your business branding.

## Trade-Specific Overrides

Domain-specific quote templates override this shared process:

- **Staircase** — quote itemises treads · risers · newels · handrail · balustrade · installation. Adds regulatory notes for Approved Doc K.
- **Kitchen** — quote itemises cabinets · worktops · appliances · services · installation · decoration. Adds appliance lead time notes.
- **Windows** — quote itemises frames · glazing · fitting · trims · certification.
