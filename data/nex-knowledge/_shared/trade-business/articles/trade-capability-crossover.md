# Can My Staircase Manufacturer Build My Kitchen?

The answer is **yes, in many cases**. Companies that manufacture bespoke staircases already possess many of the woodworking and joinery skills required to build high-quality kitchens. Both staircases and kitchens rely on precision manufacturing · timber machining · cabinet construction · finishing · detailed installation.

However, building a kitchen also requires specialist knowledge that goes beyond traditional joinery.

## Skills That Transfer Directly

A professional staircase manufacturer will already understand: timber selection · wood movement · cabinet construction · precision machining · CNC manufacturing · sanding and finishing · spray painting · veneering · solid timber work · joinery techniques · quality control · site measuring · installation · customer design consultation.

These are major advantages.

## Additional Kitchen Knowledge Required

A specialist kitchen company also understands:

### Kitchen Ergonomics
Work triangle · appliance spacing · comfortable worktop heights · walkway clearances · seating requirements.

### Cabinet Standards
Base cabinet sizes · wall cabinet heights · tall housing units · appliance housing · filler panels · service voids.

### Kitchen Hardware
Soft-close hinges · drawer systems · lift-up mechanisms · pull-out storage · corner storage · pantry systems. Manufacturers: Blum · Hettich · Grass · Häfele.

### Appliance Integration
Ovens · microwaves · fridges · dishwashers · wine coolers · coffee machines · extractors.

### Plumbing Knowledge
Sink positioning · waste pipe routes · water supplies · boiling taps · water filters · dishwashers.

### Electrical Planning
Socket locations · under-cabinet lighting · island supplies · appliance circuits · LED lighting.

### Worktops
Quartz · granite · porcelain · solid timber · laminate · solid surface — including templating and fitting.

### Kitchen Workflow
Food preparation · cooking · washing up · storage · family use · entertaining.

## What a Staircase Company Already Does Better

Many staircase manufacturers actually EXCEED kitchen companies in: hardwood craftsmanship · complex joinery · curved work · CNC machining · bespoke timber work · hand finishing · traditional joinery · spray finishing · attention to detail. Especially companies producing high-end bespoke staircases.

## The Biggest Difference

The difference is usually **experience, not ability**. A kitchen company may have completed 2,000 kitchens while a staircase company may have completed 2,000 staircases. The woodworking skills are very similar, but each company has developed specialist knowledge through repetition.

## Can a Staircase Company Become a Kitchen Manufacturer?

**Absolutely.** If they add knowledge in kitchen planning · cabinet standards · appliance integration · plumbing coordination · electrical coordination · kitchen hardware · worktop systems · interior design, they can manufacture kitchens to the same standard as specialist kitchen companies.

Many successful bespoke joinery firms already produce staircases · kitchens · bedrooms · home offices · media walls · boot rooms · libraries · wine rooms · utility rooms — using the same machinery, workshop, and skilled craftsmen.

## Nex Guidance — Assessing Capability

Nex should not assume every staircase manufacturer can build a complete kitchen. Instead, assess whether the company has:

- Experience in cabinet making
- Knowledge of kitchen design principles
- Access to quality kitchen hardware
- Experience integrating appliances
- Understanding of plumbing and electrical coordination
- Skilled finishing and installation teams

If those capabilities are in place, a bespoke staircase manufacturer can often produce a kitchen that equals — or even surpasses — the quality of many standard kitchen suppliers, particularly where custom design · premium timber · fine craftsmanship are priorities.
