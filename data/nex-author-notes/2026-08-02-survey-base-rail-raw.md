## Survey Brain golden principles (verbatim)

- *"A staircase is only as accurate as the survey it is built from."*
- *"The survey is the foundation of the entire staircase project."*
- *"Never guess a dimension that can be measured."*
- *"Always work from a consistent reference point (datum)."*
- *"Finished floor levels are more important than temporary construction levels."*
- *"Measure twice. Record once."* — if measurements disagree, stop and find out why
- *"A survey is not about proving the surveyor is right; it is about giving the company the best possible information."*
- *"The surveyor doesn't design the staircase — surveyor provides information, designer determines geometry."*
- *"The best surveyors prevent problems that never become visible."*
- *"Poor surveys cost everyone — extra drawings · phone calls · site revisits · manufacturing delays · component alterations · installation delays · customer frustration."*
- *"Every survey improves the next one."*
- *"Confidence begins with accurate information."*

## Common survey mistakes (verbatim from Philip)

- Measuring to unfinished floors instead of finished floor levels
- Forgetting floor coverings
- Assuming walls are square
- Missing steel beams
- Overlooking pipes or cables
- Measuring from different reference points
- Incomplete photographs
- Surveying before the building is ready

## Base Rail Brain principles (verbatim)

- *"A base rail is not simply a piece of timber under the balusters. It is a structural and decorative component that locates, supports and aligns the balusters."*
- *"The infill slip is not the primary structural fixing. The balustrade's overall strength comes from the complete system — newel posts provide the main anchorage, handrail ties the balusters together, base rail locates their lower ends."*
- *"The groove acts as a datum for the balusters."*
- *"The grooved base rail helps control cumulative error."*
- *"The base rail contributes to the load path but is only one part of the complete structural system."*
- *"The groove and infill slip system was developed long before CNC machinery existed. Traditional joiners using spindle moulders, routers and hand tools relied on this method because it was accurate, repeatable and practical."*
- *"Whether a company calls it a base rail or a shoe rail, the important concept for Nex is to understand the function rather than assume a single 'correct' name."*
