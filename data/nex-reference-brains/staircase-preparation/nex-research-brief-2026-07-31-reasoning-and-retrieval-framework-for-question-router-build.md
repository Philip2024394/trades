# NEX Research Brief — 2026-07-31 (Phase 2 Router Build · Reasoning and Retrieval Framework)

## Philip's Research Brief (preserved verbatim as authored)

### What Claude Should Research

*I think what you're really looking for is not a "better prompt", but a **Reasoning and Retrieval Framework**.*

*I'd ask Claude to research things like:*

### 1. Intent Classification

*Instead of keywords.*

*Examples:*

- Buy
- Learn
- Compare
- Design
- Quote
- Diagnose
- Install
- Materials
- Images
- Troubleshoot

### 2. Multi-stage Question Routing

*Exactly like your architecture.*

```
Question
   ↓
Intent
   ↓
Subject
   ↓
Brain
   ↓
Knowledge Domain
   ↓
Information Type
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Compose Answer
```

### 3. Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)

*How modern AI systems retrieve evidence before answering.*

*Not vector search.*

*Real evidence orchestration.*

### 4. Knowledge Graphs

*How knowledge links together.*

*For example:*

```
Straight Flight
   ↓
uses
   ↓
Newel Posts
   ↓
made from
   ↓
Oak
   ↓
priced by
   ↓
Estimator
```

### 5. Conversation Planning

*How AI decides:*

- Should I answer?
- Should I ask a question?
- Should I browse?
- Should I quote?
- Should I compare?

### 6. Response Planning

*Modern assistants don't just retrieve documents.*

*They build a plan.*

*For example:*

```
User: How much for straight flight stairs?
   ↓
Need price
   ↓
Need material
   ↓
Need height
   ↓
Need width
   ↓
Need balustrade
   ↓
Ask two questions
   ↓
Estimate
```

### Companies / Technologies to Research

*I think Claude should research companies like:*

- OpenAI
- Anthropic
- Google DeepMind
- Microsoft Copilot
- Perplexity
- IBM Watson (knowledge systems)
- Haystack
- LlamaIndex
- LangGraph

*Not to copy them.*

*To understand how modern AI assistants:*

- plan
- retrieve
- compose
- ask follow-up questions
- combine multiple knowledge sources

### Proposed Research Prompt (authored by Philip · ready to execute)

> *Research the state of the art for AI knowledge orchestration and conversational reasoning. Do not focus on prompt engineering alone. Focus on how modern AI assistants determine user intent, select the correct knowledge source, retrieve multiple pieces of evidence, decide when clarification is required, compose natural responses from structured knowledge, and avoid keyword-based retrieval failures. Compare the architectural approaches used by leading AI systems and identify the principles that would apply to a multi-brain knowledge operating system like Nex.*

### One Thing I Would Change

*I wouldn't ask Claude to generate "questions and answers."*

*From everything you've shown me over the last day, I think that would move the project backwards.*

*Instead, I'd ask Claude to research:*

> ***How does an AI generate its own responses from evidence instead of retrieving pre-written answers?***

*That is a much better research question.*

*It's also completely aligned with one of your constitutional ideas:*

> ***Knowledge is permanent. Responses are temporary.***

*That principle is stronger than a large database of handcrafted Q&A because it allows Nex to compose answers dynamically from trusted evidence rather than searching for the closest matching response.*
