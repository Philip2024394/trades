# NEX Voice Production System · Standard v2 Candidate (Philip 2026-07-31 · verbatim)

When NEX generates spoken responses, the process mirrors how the human brain produces speech.

## 1. Language Planning · Wernicke's Area
- Understands language · selects words · builds meaning · processes grammar
- **NEX equivalent:** intent understanding · vocabulary selection · sentence construction · context management

## 2. Speech Programming · Broca's Area
- Converts language into speech movements · plans pronunciation · controls timing · organises syllables
- **NEX equivalent:** text-to-speech planning · pronunciation engine · speech rhythm · word sequencing

## 3. Voice Control · Laryngeal Motor Cortex
- Controls vocal cords · adjusts pitch · controls loudness · starts and stops phonation
- **NEX equivalent:** voice synthesis · pitch control · volume control · speaking style

## 4. Speech Movement · Motor Cortex
- Controls lips · tongue · jaw · soft palate · face · vocal muscles
- **NEX equivalent:** phoneme generation · mouth-shape simulation · articulation model

## 5. Breathing System · Brainstem
- Pre-Bötzinger Complex: breathing rhythm
- RAmVOC Neurons: coordinate breathing with speech
- Timing · speech pauses · natural inhalation · sentence pacing
- **NEX equivalent:** natural pauses · breath simulation · speaking cadence · conversational timing

## 6. Hearing Feedback · Auditory Cortex
- Hears own voice · detects errors · adjusts speech in real time
- **NEX equivalent:** audio monitoring · pronunciation validation · speech quality control

## 7. Emotion & Expression · Limbic System (Amygdala · Cingulate Cortex · Insula)
- Happiness · excitement · calmness · sympathy · confidence
- **NEX equivalent:** emotional tone · expressive voice · conversational warmth · speaking personality

## 8. Timing & Precision · Cerebellum
- Smooth speech · precise timing · coordinated movement · natural flow
- **NEX equivalent:** smooth transitions · speech pacing · natural rhythm · reduced robotic delivery

## Complete Pipeline

```
User Input → Language Understanding (Wernicke) → Speech Planning (Broca) →
Voice Generation (Laryngeal) → Articulation (Motor Cortex) → Breathing (Brainstem) →
Emotion (Limbic) → Timing (Cerebellum) → Speech Output
```

## NEX Voice Goal

NEX should produce speech that sounds: natural and conversational · smooth and well-paced · emotionally appropriate · clear and articulate · expressive without exaggeration · consistent in tone and personality.

This model gives NEX a conceptual framework inspired by human speech production while recognising that an AI voice is ultimately generated by a text-to-speech system rather than biological brain structures.
