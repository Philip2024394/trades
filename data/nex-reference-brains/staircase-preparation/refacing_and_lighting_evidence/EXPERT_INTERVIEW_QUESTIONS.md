# Expert Interview Questions

Questions for certified staircase experts to answer, drawn from every `expert_experience_required` flag + Section 13 trade-experience areas.

**Use these questions in structured expert interviews. Answers become citable `origin_type: named_expert` content (Rule C).**

## section_13_trade_experience · customer_mistakes

*Craft knowledge from repeated customer interactions across hundreds of installs. Not documented in BWF/TRADA/CIBSE publications because it is behavioural rather than technical.*

- What are the three most common mistakes homeowners make when specifying a new staircase, and how do you head them off at the quote stage?
- How often do customers choose a tread material that is unsuitable for the traffic level in their home, and what are the tell-tale signs at survey?
- When customers ask for open risers, how many change their mind after you explain the 100mm-sphere rule under Approved Document K and the child-fall interaction?
- What percentage of your customers underestimate the width required at the top and bottom for furniture moves, and how do you counsel this before joinery is cut?
- Which decorative aspiration causes the most warranty callbacks in your experience — glass, painted, or oil-finished oak?

## section_13_trade_experience · design_regrets

*Follow-up feedback 3-10 years post-install is held only by manufacturers with long relationships to their customer base; not written up.*

- For floating staircase installs, what post-install regrets do you hear most (echo · dust visibility · lighting glare · pet safety)?

## section_13_trade_experience · lighting_positions_that_work_best

*CIBSE LG16 tells you WHERE to measure lux; only installers know which positions actually stay working, don't blind users mid-descent, and don't cast confusing shadows on the tread edge in a real home.*

- Given a straight domestic flight, where do you fit LED step-lights (riser · underside of tread · string face · wall-mounted at ankle height) and why?
- How do you decide between recessed wall step-lights (kick-height) and tread-underside LEDs when the staircase runs against a plasterboard wall?
- For a cut-string cantilever staircase, where do you route the LED wiring so it doesn't compromise the mitre-return joint at the tread nosing?
- What is your rule of thumb for LED colour temperature on stairs shared by elderly residents?
- When do you specify motion-activated PIR vs. photocell-triggered dusk-to-dawn on a domestic stair?

## section_13_trade_experience · refacing_mistakes
