# Value + property

Money questions — what a staircase costs, what it's worth, what it adds to the house, and where the value actually lives.

---

## A · Cost

### Q126 — How much does a UK staircase cost?
- What am I looking at price-wise for a new stair?

**Notes:** wide range — standard softwood few thousand, bespoke hardwood £8-25k+, luxury feature £25-100k+. Depends on shape, timber, complexity, installation.

### Q127 — What makes one staircase quote higher than another for the same house?
- Two companies gave me very different prices — why?

**Notes:** specification differences: timber grade, string thickness, joinery quality, finishing, installation scope, and aftercare.
**Related:** Q116

### Q128 — Can I make my staircase cheaper without losing quality?
- How do I stay in budget without a rubbish stair?

**Notes:** simpler design, standard shape, painted timber, straightforward balustrade — savings without compromising structure.

## B · Valuing an existing staircase

### Q129 — How do I get my existing staircase valued?
- Who values a staircase — an estate agent?

**Notes:** manufacturer values physical replacement cost; surveyor values contribution to property; heritage specialist for historic staircases.

### Q130 — What's the difference between replacement value and market value?
- Are those the same number?

**Notes:** replacement = cost to reproduce today. Market = what a buyer pays — considers the whole property, not the staircase alone.

### Q131 — What's an insurance reinstatement value?
- What figure would I give my insurer?

**Notes:** cost to rebuild if destroyed — often higher than market value because of skilled labour + rare components.

### Q132 — What information do I need to give someone valuing my staircase?
- What should I photograph and note before asking for a valuation?

**Notes:** complete stair view, close-ups of newels/handrail/balusters/string detail, width, tread count, timber species, age, country, whether factory or handmade.

## C · Effect on house value

### Q133 — Does a new staircase increase house value?
- Will I get my money back if I put a new stair in?

**Notes:** contribution comes from first impression, quality workmanship, appropriate style. Depends on overall property + local market.

### Q134 — Which staircase features add the most house value?
- Where's the money best spent for value uplift?

**Notes:** quality handrail, well-proportioned newels, feature starting step, timber selection, lighting, clean finishing.

### Q135 — Is it worth renovating stairs before selling?
- Should I replace the staircase before I list the house?

**Notes:** depends on current condition, buyer expectations for the property, and cost vs likely uplift.

### Q136 — When does age make a staircase MORE valuable rather than less?
- My staircase is 80 years old — is that a plus or minus?

**Notes:** rarity + craftsmanship + originality + character. A well-made period staircase in good condition can be a selling point.

### Q137 — What makes a bespoke staircase valuable?
- Why do custom stairs cost so much more?

**Notes:** design time, skilled machining, unique manufacturing, custom components, installation complexity, materials.
