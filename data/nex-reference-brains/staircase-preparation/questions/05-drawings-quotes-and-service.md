# Drawings, quotes, and service

The customer journey around commissioning a staircase — the paperwork, the site visits, and what to do when things go wrong.

---

## A · Drawings + surveys

### Q111 — Do UK staircase companies charge for the initial design drawing?
- Is there a fee just for the first quote drawing?

**Notes:** many provide a free initial layout + simple 3D visual as part of the quotation. Others use a deposit / design-fee model.

### Q112 — What's the difference between a customer approval drawing and a manufacturing drawing?
- Which drawing shows the actual construction?

**Notes:** approval drawing = plan/elevation for customer sign-off. Manufacturing drawing = full workshop detail (housings, cutting list, joint positions).

### Q113 — Do I need a site survey before ordering my staircase?
- Should the stair maker visit before we agree the design?

**Notes:** most quality manufacturers require a survey rather than working from customer measurements alone.

### Q114 — Why can't the staircase company just work from my floor plan?
- I've got the architect's drawings — isn't that enough?

**Notes:** existing buildings differ from drawings; finished floor levels, walls, and openings often unknown until measured.
**Related:** Q113

### Q115 — What information should be on my final manufacturing drawing?
- What do I check before I sign off?

**Notes:** stair type, rise/going, width, newel positions, handrail, materials, finish, timber species, delivery + installation scope.

---

## B · Quotes + deposits

### Q116 — How do I compare quotes from different staircase companies?
- Two quotes look similar — how do I tell which is the better deal?

**Notes:** check what's included: manufacture only, installation, finishing, delivery, aftercare. Compare specifications not just totals.

### Q117 — Why does one staircase company want a deposit before drawings?
- Do I have to pay before I see anything?

**Notes:** design-fee model protects manufacturer from spending hours on unpurchased designs. Deposit often deducted from final price if the order proceeds.

### Q118 — Is it normal to pay a deposit before manufacture starts?
- How much upfront is reasonable?

**Notes:** varies. Materials and workshop allocation typically require an upfront payment.

### Q119 — What questions should I ask the staircase company before ordering?
- What should I check before I say yes?

**Notes:** timescale, warranty, aftercare, who installs, what happens if it doesn't fit, VAT included, and drawings I'll receive.

---

## C · When things go wrong

### Q120 — I've paid a deposit and the company has stopped replying — what do I do?
- The staircase maker has gone quiet after my deposit — help?

**Notes:** collect all paperwork (quote, contract, drawings, payments, messages). Send a formal written message with a reasonable deadline for a reply.

### Q121 — Who's responsible if the staircase doesn't fit on installation day?
- If the stairs don't fit, whose fault is it?

**Notes:** depends on who measured, who designed, and whether site conditions changed. Contract terms matter.

### Q122 — What are my options if a staircase company won't cooperate?
- Where do I go if they won't fix problems?

**Notes:** consumer protection routes, trade-association complaints (BWF Stair Scheme members), mediation, formal dispute resolution.

### Q123 — Can I return a bespoke staircase if I change my mind?
- What if I decide I don't want it after all?

**Notes:** bespoke work rarely refundable once manufacturing has started — materials and workshop time committed.

---

## D · Aftercare

### Q124 — How long is a UK staircase warranty typically?
- What guarantee do I get?

**Notes:** varies by manufacturer. Ask specifically about defects vs seasonal movement (the latter is often not covered but is normal).

### Q125 — What aftercare should I expect after installation?
- Do they come back to check the staircase after fitting?

**Notes:** many manufacturers offer a follow-up visit to address seasonal settling — small adjustments common in the first year.
