## A · Titanic's Grand Staircase

### Q138 — Who built the Titanic Grand Staircase?
- Where was the Titanic staircase actually made?

**Notes:** Harland and Wolff shipyard, Belfast, Northern Ireland. Not one staircase maker — a large team across multiple specialist trades.

### Q139 — What timber was used in the Titanic Grand Staircase?
- What wood was in the Titanic staircase?

**Notes:** primarily oak — staircase structure + surrounding panelling.

### Q140 — What trades built the Titanic Grand Staircase?
- Who actually worked on the Titanic stair?

**Notes:** joiners, cabinetmakers, wood carvers, wrought-iron and bronze metalworkers, polishers/finishers, installers — coordinated interior craft team.

### Q141 — Is the original Titanic Grand Staircase still around?
- Did any of the staircase survive?

**Notes:** the original went down with the ship in 1912. Interior did not survive the wreck condition. No complete original recovered.

### Q142 — Where can I see a Titanic staircase replica?
- Are there any recreations open to visit?

**Notes:** most famous examples are recreations rather than the original — Titanic Belfast is a well-known reference.

### Q143 — What made the Titanic staircase famous?
- Why do people still talk about that staircase?

**Notes:** designed as a floating hotel entrance — proportion + oak + wrought iron + glass dome + "Honour and Glory Crowning Time" clock. Represented peak Edwardian craftsmanship.

### Q144 — Could you build a Titanic-style staircase today?
- Can that kind of stair still be made?

**Notes:** technically yes — skills exist. Challenge is assembling a coordinated team of specialist trades at the required level.

## B · Period properties + restoration

### Q145 — I've got a Victorian staircase — what should I be aware of?
- What are the considerations for restoring a period stair?

**Notes:** original details often irreplaceable — turned newels, decorative balusters, moulded handrails may need matching by hand.

### Q146 — Should I restore or replace an old staircase?
- Is my old stair worth keeping?

**Notes:** condition + character + regulatory compliance. Restoration preserves value where structure is sound.

### Q147 — What staircase style suits a period property?
- What's right for a Georgian / Victorian / Edwardian house?

**Notes:** house architecture drives the answer. Traditional details typically suit period homes.

### Q148 — Where do I find replacement components for a heritage staircase?
- I need a matching baluster / newel / handrail moulding — where do I look?

**Notes:** specialist heritage joinery, custom turning workshops, sometimes salvage.

### Q149 — Is my heritage staircase compliant with modern building regulations?
- Do I have to bring it up to current regs?

**Notes:** replacement triggers current regs; restoration in situ usually protected. Check with local building control.

## C · Rarity + significance

### Q150 — When does a staircase become historically significant?
- What makes an old stair actually valuable?

**Notes:** rarity + craftsmanship + originality + difficulty of reproduction. Age alone doesn't create value.
**Related:** Q136

### Q151 — Are there examples of famous UK staircases I can look at?
- Where can I see great staircases in Britain?

**Notes:** heritage-property staircases, National Trust properties, historic universities — many well-documented examples.
