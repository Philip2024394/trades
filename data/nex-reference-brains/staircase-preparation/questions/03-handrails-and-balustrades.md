# Handrails + balustrades

Regulated heights and gaps, positioning choices, and the visual + safety trade-offs on the balustrade system.

---

## A · Handrail heights + positions

### Q073 — What handrail height is required on a UK domestic flight?
- How tall should the handrail be over the stairs?

**Notes:** 900-1000mm from pitch line. 900mm the most common workshop default.

### Q074 — What's the handrail height on a landing (guarding)?
- Is the landing rail the same height as the stair rail?

**Notes:** minimum 900mm above landing floor for domestic; 1100mm commonly required for commercial guarding.

### Q075 — Does a UK private staircase need handrails on both sides?
- Do I have to have a rail on the wall side too?

**Notes:** stair width dependent — under 1000mm typically one rail is acceptable.

### Q076 — Do I need a wall-side handrail on a winder staircase?
- Should I fit an extra rail on the wall for the winder?

**Notes:** not a strict rule; many quality manufacturers add one for the inside corner feel.
**Related:** Q035

### Q077 — How is the handrail height measured on stairs vs a landing?
- Is it measured differently over the flight?

**Notes:** stairs — vertical from pitch line to top of handrail. Landing — vertical from floor.
**Related:** Q073, Q074

## B · Balusters + gaps

### Q078 — What's the maximum gap between balusters?
- How far apart can the spindles be?

**Notes:** 100mm sphere test — Approved Doc K.

### Q079 — Why 100mm — where does that number come from?
- What's the sphere-test rule?

**Related:** Q078

### Q080 — What baluster centres do UK manufacturers typically use?
- How far apart do stair makers actually put them?

**Notes:** commonly 90-100mm centres to satisfy 100mm max opening.
**Related:** Q078

### Q081 — Can I use fewer balusters for a modern minimalist look?
- Is it OK to have wider spacing if the design calls for it?

**Notes:** spacing must still meet safety requirements. Appearance can change; the sphere test can't.
**Related:** Q078

### Q082 — Are metal balusters governed by the same 100mm rule?
- Do glass or steel balustrades follow the same spacing?

**Related:** Q078

## C · Newel post spacing + strength

### Q083 — Is there a rule for the maximum spacing between newel posts?
- How far can two newels be apart?

**Notes:** no regulated maximum spacing — controlled by handrail strength, appearance, and deflection.

### Q084 — What newel spacing do UK manufacturers typically use?
- What's the normal newel-to-newel distance?

**Notes:** straight flight 1800-2400mm typical; quarter turn — at corners + landings.
**Related:** Q083

### Q085 — When would I add an intermediate newel on a long flight?
- Do I need more newels if the stair is long?

**Notes:** long unsupported handrail runs benefit from intermediate newels for strength + appearance.
**Related:** Q083

### Q086 — Is there a legal minimum width between newel posts?
- Do the regs set a stair-narrowing rule for newels?

**Notes:** no — Approved Doc K governs usable stair width, not newel-to-newel dimension.

## D · Materials + configuration

### Q087 — Can I mix materials on my balustrade (glass + timber, metal + timber)?
- Is it OK to combine glass panels with an oak handrail?

**Notes:** design decision. All materials must meet loading + safety requirements.

### Q088 — Are glass balustrades safe for a family home?
- Is glass suitable if I've got young children?

**Notes:** correctly specified glass systems designed for domestic use. Correct thickness + fixing critical.

### Q089 — What handrail profiles are common in UK domestic staircases?
- What's a mopstick? What's a pig ear?

**Notes:** rectangular, mopstick, pig ear, wall-rail — profile names refer to cross-section shape.

### Q090 — Can I add a handrail after the staircase is installed?
- We didn't fit a rail originally — can we add one now?

**Notes:** yes — retrofit handrails common, especially wall-mounted. Fixing points + finished height matter.
