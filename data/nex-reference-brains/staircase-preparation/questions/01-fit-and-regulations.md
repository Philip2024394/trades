## B · Rise · going · pitch (UK & Ireland)

### Q011 — What is the maximum rise for a UK private staircase?
- How tall can each step be under Part K?

**Notes:** Approved Doc K · 220mm max for dwellings.
**Related:** Q012, Q014, Q027

### Q012 — What is the minimum going for a UK private staircase?
- How deep does each tread have to be?

**Notes:** Approved Doc K · 220mm min for dwellings.
**Related:** Q011, Q013

### Q013 — What's the maximum pitch angle allowed?
- How steep can a domestic staircase be?

**Notes:** Approved Doc K · 42° max for dwellings.
**Related:** Q011, Q012

### Q014 — Do all rises and goings have to be the same?
- Can the first or last step be a different height?

**Notes:** consistency requirement — Approved Doc K.
**Related:** Q011

### Q015 — Does the 2R + G formula still apply?
- What's the sum-of-two-rises-plus-going meant to be?

**Notes:** *2R + G = 550-700mm* rule of thumb.

### Q016 — Are the Irish rules the same as English Part K?
- Republic of Ireland vs UK — any differences?

**Notes:** rise/going/pitch identical; flight length differs (Q027).
**Related:** Q027, Q028

### Q017 — Where's the Irish equivalent of Approved Document K?

**Notes:** Technical Guidance Document K (Ireland).
**Related:** Q016

### Q018 — Is my staircase compliant with these rise/going numbers?

**Notes:** worked example from Philip's transcript — 200mm rise / 225.3mm going / 41.6° pitch on a 13T/14R flight.
**Related:** Q019

### Q019 — How close am I to the legal maximum on this design?

**Related:** Q018

### Q020 — What's the ideal (not just legal) rise and going range?

**Notes:** recommended range 170-190mm rise · 240-280mm going per the family design envelope.

## D · Width — clear, overall, and inside-string

### Q025 — What's the difference between overall width and clear width?
- Is the width you quoted the outside or the walking width?

**Notes:** overall = outside string to outside string; clear = between strings; NEX geometry standard captures both.
**Related:** Q026, Q030

### Q026 — If I want a specific clear walking width, what overall width do I need?
- I want 800mm clear — what does that make my overall?

**Notes:** overall = clear + left string + right string. Depends on string thickness.
**Related:** Q025, Q041

### Q027 — Does thicker string mean a wider staircase?
- If the strings are 45mm instead of 32mm, does the whole thing get bigger?

**Notes:** yes if clear width is fixed; no if overall width is fixed (clear width shrinks).
**Related:** Q026, Q041

### Q028 — How do UK manufacturers dimension "stair width" on drawings?
- What's the standard reference dimension on a workshop drawing?

**Notes:** most UK manufacturers work from overall (outside-string-to-outside-string).
**Related:** Q025

### Q029 — Is there a minimum width for a private domestic staircase?
- Do UK regs set a minimum stair width for houses?

**Notes:** private dwellings — no fixed universal minimum; commercial differs.
**Related:** Q123 (commercial)

### Q030 — What are typical UK domestic staircase widths?
- Is 860mm normal? Is 900mm better?

**Notes:** 800-900mm common; 1000-1100mm premium.
**Related:** Q006

## E · Flight length + landings + winders

### Q031 — How many risers can a single UK private flight have before needing a landing?
- Do I have to break the flight after a certain number of steps?

**Notes:** England private — Approved Doc K limits change of direction beyond **36** consecutive risers. Recommended max 16 for comfort.
**Related:** Q032, Q033, Q027

### Q032 — Is the 16-riser limit a legal rule or best practice?
- I read 16 was the max — is that actually in the regulations?

**Notes:** 16 is the recommended domestic max in UK practice AND the legal max in Ireland. In UK regs, 36 is the legal number.
**Related:** Q031, Q033

### Q033 — What are Ireland's flight-length rules?
- Does Ireland allow the same 36 risers as England?

**Notes:** Ireland's TGD K limits to 16 risers per flight — landing required beyond.
**Related:** Q031, Q032

### Q034 — Can I have 20 risers straight without a landing?
- I've got a tall floor-to-floor — is a single flight OK?

**Notes:** legally yes in England (under 36), no in Ireland. In practice both jurisdictions would recommend a landing or turn.
**Related:** Q031, Q033

### Q035 — Are there rules about the maximum number of winders?
- Am I limited to 2 winders? Can I have 3?

**Notes:** private domestic — no cap on 3 winders in a quarter turn. Consistent going required.
**Related:** Q036

### Q036 — Winders or a landing — which is better?
- Which should I choose for a quarter turn?

**Related:** Q035

### Q037 — What's the minimum width at the narrow end of a winder tread?
- How thin can the point of a wedge tread be?

**Notes:** BWF — 50mm minimum at narrow end (nosing-to-nosing).
**Related:** Q038

### Q038 — How is the walking line measured on winders?
- Where do you measure the going on a winder staircase?

**Notes:** arc from the newel centre.
**Related:** Q037

### Q039 — How close can a winder tread come to the newel post?
- Is there a rule for the shoulder around the newel?

**Notes:** no regulated dimension. Manufacturer decides (typical 18-25mm shoulder).
**Related:** Q037
