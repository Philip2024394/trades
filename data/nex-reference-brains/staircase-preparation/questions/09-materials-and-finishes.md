# Materials + finishes

Timber species, terminology, finishes, and the long-term behaviour of the material after installation.

---

## A · Terminology + species

### Q152 — What's the difference between "wood" and "timber"?
- Are wood and timber the same thing?

**Notes:** wood = natural material from a tree. Timber = wood prepared for construction (cut, seasoned, graded). Consumer/trade language distinction.

### Q153 — Do I call it a wooden staircase or a timber staircase?
- Which term is right?

**Notes:** both correct. Wooden = consumer term. Timber = trade/construction term.
**Related:** Q152

### Q154 — What's the difference between European Oak and American White Oak?
- Which oak should I choose?

**Notes:** grain, colour, availability, cost, machining behaviour all differ. Both suitable for staircases; workshop preference varies.

### Q155 — What timber species work for a UK staircase?
- What choices do I have for the timber?

**Notes:** oak (most common hardwood), ash, walnut, sapele, mahogany (heritage), pine/redwood (paint-grade), engineered options.

### Q156 — Is engineered timber inferior to solid timber?
- Should I avoid anything that isn't solid wood?

**Notes:** engineered offers stability + movement control. Not automatically inferior — different tool for different situation.

---

## B · Finishes

### Q157 — What's the difference between oil and lacquer for a stair finish?
- Oil or lacquer — which is better?

**Notes:** oil = natural feel, easier local repair. Lacquer = tougher surface, easier cleaning. Choice depends on use + repair preference.

### Q158 — Should I stain my oak staircase or leave it natural?
- Natural or coloured — what do people typically do?

**Notes:** design choice. Natural oak ages warmly; stains lock a specific tone.

### Q159 — Can I paint an oak staircase?
- Is it a mistake to paint over the grain?

**Notes:** design choice. Common on modern designs — often two-tone (painted strings/newels + natural handrail).

### Q160 — Which finish lasts longest under family traffic?
- What's the most durable finish?

**Notes:** lacquer typically more durable; oil requires periodic maintenance but easier local touch-up.

## C · Long-term behaviour

### Q161 — Why has my oak staircase gone darker over time?
- The colour of the wood has changed — is that normal?

**Notes:** yes — UV exposure, oxidation, finish ageing. Natural process.

### Q162 — Does sunlight damage a timber staircase?
- If a window shines onto the stairs, will they fade?

**Notes:** UV changes timber colour + can affect finishes. Uneven exposure = uneven colour change.

### Q163 — Why has my staircase changed appearance in different rooms?
- One side looks different to the other — what's going on?

**Related:** Q162

### Q164 — How often should I refinish or maintain my staircase?
- What's the maintenance schedule for oak stairs?

**Notes:** depends on finish type + traffic. Oil more frequent + easier; lacquer less frequent + harder to touch up.

### Q165 — Can old stairs be sanded and refinished?
- Can I bring old stairs back to life?

**Notes:** depends on veneer thickness (if engineered), previous sanding depth, and remaining timber section.
