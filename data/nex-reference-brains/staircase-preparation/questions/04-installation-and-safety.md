# Installation + safety

Where site conditions meet workshop precision — flooring, trimmer, back finishing, and the long-term issues that appear once the staircase is in use.

---

## A · Flooring + trimmer coordination

### Q091 — Should flooring go under the staircase or around it?
- Do I lay the floor first or fit the stairs first?

**Notes:** flooring continues UNDER the staircase in most quality installations. Staircase sits on finished floor level, not cut around.

### Q092 — Why does it matter which order flooring and staircase go in?
- What goes wrong if the floor comes after the stairs?

**Notes:** first riser height becomes different from all others if the finished floor level was assumed but built up later.
**Related:** Q091

### Q093 — What is a trimmer and how does it interact with the staircase?
- The joist at the top of the stairs — what's its role?

**Notes:** the trimming joist forms the ceiling opening. Top tread bears on it; top riser terminates against its face.
**Related:** Q061

### Q094 — Who's responsible for coordinating floor heights with the staircase maker?
- Who has to talk to whom before the stair is manufactured?

**Notes:** staircase maker + flooring installer + builder must agree finished floor levels before manufacture.
**Related:** Q091

## B · Back of staircase + finishing

### Q095 — What timber should I use to frame the back of a staircase for panels?
- What batten do I use behind the staircase for sheet material?

**Notes:** 2" × 2" battens suitable for rigid panels (plywood, T&G, veneered).
**Related:** Q096

### Q096 — What timber should I use to frame the back of a staircase for plasterboard?
- What if I'm plasterboarding the back?

**Notes:** 4" × 2" preferred — wider fixing face, better support, less flex.
**Related:** Q095

### Q097 — Can I nail plasterboard to the back of a staircase?
- Do I use nails or screws for the plasterboard?

**Notes:** use screws — staircase movement over time pops nails.

### Q098 — Should I plaster the back of the stairs or use tape and joint?
- Which finish handles staircase movement better?

**Notes:** tape and joint often preferred — more tolerant of small movement + easier repair.

## C · Top step + top riser

### Q099 — Why is my top step loose?
- The top step lifts when I walk on it — what's happening?

**Notes:** critical safety issue — top step forms the transition and gets repeated loading. Any lift = trip hazard.

### Q100 — How do I fix a lifted top step properly?
- What's the correct repair for a moving top step?

**Notes:** drill, screw, and plug into the trimmer. Nails alone insufficient — they gradually loosen.
**Related:** Q099

### Q101 — Is a small gap above the top riser normal?
- I've noticed a gap at the top — should I worry?

**Notes:** small cosmetic gaps often seasonal timber movement. Any TREAD or RISER movement = investigate immediately.
**Related:** Q099, Q108

## D · Radiators and heat

### Q102 — Can I put a radiator under my staircase?
- Is a radiator under the stairs a bad idea?

**Notes:** avoid where possible. Continuous warm/dry air rises through the staircase, drying timber unevenly.

### Q103 — What happens to a staircase if a radiator dries the timber?
- What damage does a radiator underneath actually cause?

**Notes:** opening joints, squeaks, movement, shrinkage around strings and newels, visible gaps.
**Related:** Q102

### Q104 — What if I already have a radiator under my staircase?
- Can I mitigate the damage without moving the radiator?

**Notes:** reduce or turn off during unnecessary periods; keep house temperature reasonably stable.
**Related:** Q102

---

## E · Squeaks + movement + repairs

### Q105 — Why does my staircase squeak?
- What causes the creak on my stairs?

**Notes:** movement between components (tread-string, riser-tread), fixing issues, seasonal timber change, glue-line breakdown.

### Q106 — How do I stop a squeaking stair without pulling the whole flight apart?
- Can I fix a squeak from above or below?

**Notes:** depends on access. From underneath: re-wedge, re-glue, screw fix. From above: limited.

### Q107 — What causes a gap to appear between a tread and a riser?
- I've got a gap on one of my treads — is it dangerous?

**Notes:** timber movement, glue failure, wedge failure. Size, location, and whether it's stable determine severity.

### Q108 — Why does my handrail feel loose after a few years?
- My handrail wobbles now — how do I fix it?

**Notes:** loose newel fixing, loose bracket, movement at handrail joint, or failed adhesive.
**Related:** Q083

## F · Structural + surrounding building

### Q109 — Can I remove the wall next to my staircase?
- I want to open up the staircase — can I take the wall out?

**Notes:** wall may be structural or contain services. Requires structural assessment before removal.

### Q110 — Can I move my staircase to a different position in the house?
- Can we relocate the whole staircase?

**Notes:** affects floor openings, trimmer, headroom, building regs. Major project.
