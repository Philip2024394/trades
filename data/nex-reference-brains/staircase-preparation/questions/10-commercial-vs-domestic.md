# Commercial vs domestic

Where the rules change once the building isn't a private dwelling — width, geometry, nosings, and structural expectations.

---

## A · Geometry differences

### Q166 — How are commercial staircase rules different from domestic?
- What changes if it's an office not a house?

**Notes:** stricter rise + going + width + nosing requirements. Occupancy-based sizing.

### Q167 — What's the typical minimum width for a commercial staircase?
- How wide does a stair need to be in a public building?

**Notes:** based on occupancy calculation. Small commercial ~1000mm+; larger public 1200-1500mm+.

### Q168 — What are commercial rise + going limits?
- What's max rise + min going for a public stair?

**Notes:** typical guidance — 170mm max rise, 250mm min going (needs verification against current commercial regs).

### Q169 — Is the same 2R + G formula used for commercial stairs?
- Does the rise/going relationship stay the same?

**Notes:** yes — the comfort relationship applies. Numeric limits tighter for commercial.
**Related:** Q015

---

## B · Nosings + surface

### Q170 — Do commercial staircases need slip-resistant nosings?
- Are anti-slip strips required in public buildings?

**Notes:** yes — nosing must be visibly contrasting + slip-resistant in non-domestic buildings.

### Q171 — What's the maximum nosing projection?
- How far can the nosing overhang the riser?

**Notes:** 25mm typical maximum projection to avoid trip hazard.

### Q172 — What slip-resistant nosing materials are common?
- What products are used for commercial nosings?

**Notes:** aluminium insert, PVC, GRP, rubber, abrasive mineral strip.

## C · Structural + handrail differences

### Q173 — Do commercial stairs need handrails on both sides?
- Both sides or just one for a commercial stair?

**Notes:** generally required both sides for commercial + public stairs.
**Related:** Q075

### Q174 — What's the handrail height for commercial stairs?
- Same 900mm as domestic?

**Notes:** 900-1000mm range same as domestic. Landing guarding often 1100mm.
**Related:** Q073, Q074

### Q175 — What tread thickness is needed for heavy-duty commercial use?
- Do commercial stairs need thicker treads?

**Notes:** engineered to design load. 40-50mm common for solid timber commercial treads. No fixed regulation number.
