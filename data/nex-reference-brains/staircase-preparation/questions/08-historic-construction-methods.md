# Historic construction methods

Split from `07-heritage-and-history.md` at Philip's direction 2026-07-30.

**07 Heritage** covers *"who owns / values / preserves a period staircase"* — famous examples (Titanic), period-property restoration, rarity, significance.

**08 Historic Construction Methods** covers *"how staircases were made in past eras"* — jointing methods, tools, timber selection, hand-cut vs machine-cut, apprenticeship-era workshop practice. This is a craft-technique file, not a heritage-value file.

---

## Why it exists as a separate file

Philip's classification rule (2026-07-30):

> *"Instead of '07 Heritage and History', split into '07 Heritage' and '08 Historic Construction Methods'. That keeps architectural history separate from famous staircases."*

The distinction matters because a Layer 2 module authored from historic construction methods needs a different attribution shape than a Layer 2 module authored from heritage-property ownership. The former needs a named historian or restoration specialist; the latter typically needs the property record or documented archive.
