## Questions and Answers (informed by batch 7)

**Q4.b7.001** · What is a "true spiral staircase (screw type)"?
A · A staircase where wedge-shaped triangular treads spiral around a central vertical column. All treads meet at the central column and radiate outward. Highly space-efficient (occupies a single circular footprint). img-59 is the first in NEX Brain — after 6 batches of coverage this was the biggest remaining gap. Distinct from a **helical/curved sweep** (which curves without a central column).

**Q4.b7.002** · What is a "riveted black steel plate stringer"?
A · A stringer (side apron) made from thick black-painted steel plate with visible rivets around the perimeter — resembling a ship hull or industrial machinery. Purely industrial-loft aesthetic. img-23 is the only batch example.

**Q4.b7.003** · What is "Shou Sugi Ban" (flame-scorched / charred pine)?
A · A traditional Japanese wood-finishing technique where pine is scorched with an open flame until the surface chars, then brushed and oiled. Creates a distinctive dark charred surface with visible grain pattern. img-40 is the only batch example. Never seen in classical UK settings.

**Q4.b7.004** · What are "backlit acrylic balusters with LED-spot indicators"?
A · A rare architectural lighting design where the balusters are made from white frosted acrylic (light-diffusing plastic) with bright warm LED spots inset at 2-3 positions along each baluster's length. At night the balusters appear as glowing white columns with bright spots. img-54, 55 show this design.

**Q4.b7.005** · What is a "hybrid oak-and-metal composite spindle"?
A · A spindle made from two materials — light oak square upper and lower sections joined to a brushed stainless steel/chrome cylindrical centre section. Reads as both timber and metal simultaneously. img-51, 52 show this innovation.

**Q4.b7.006** · What are "diamond-chequer grip tread overlays"?
A · Rectangular anti-slip strips (typically black rubber or metal with diamond-chequer / chequer-plate pattern) applied to each tread nosing edge for slip resistance. Industrial safety use. img-19-22, 24 show this treatment on residential timber treads.

**Q4.b7.007** · What is a "perforated metal dot panel riser"?
A · A riser face clad in silver/brushed metal panel with a decorative dot perforation pattern. Industrial-luxury feature. img-32 is the only example.

**Q4.b7.008** · What is a "brushed stainless steel plate riser"?
A · A full-width brushed stainless steel panel forming the riser face (front vertical face between treads). Same industrial-luxury idea as the perforated dot version but solid rather than perforated. img-33.

**Q4.b7.009** · What is a "vine/leaf fretwork balustrade"?
A · A fretwork balustrade panel where the cut-out pattern resembles a botanical vine or leaf silhouette. Similar concept to batch 6's branch/geometric/diamond fretworks but with a more literal plant-inspired pattern. img-34.

**Q4.b7.010** · What is a "dual black steel side-stringer" staircase?
A · A cantilever open-riser stair where treads are supported by two visible black steel stringers running along both sides underneath, rather than a hidden central spine. img-43 and img-61 show variants.

**Q4.b7.011** · What is a "cage / basket" wrought-iron baluster?
A · A wrought-iron baluster with an elongated open cage / basket / rectangular frame at its centre section (creating a decorative void within the baluster silhouette). Distinct from a scroll or bulb detail. img-05, 47, 48 show variants.

**Q4.b7.012** · What is a "bow / loop" wrought-iron baluster?
A · A wrought-iron baluster with a curved outward bow shape (like a stretched bow-and-arrow silhouette) at its centre — more organic than a rectangular cage. img-49 shows this.

**Q4.b7.013** · What is a "carved scroll stair-bracket"?
A · A decorative carved element (often acanthus scroll or cartouche) applied to the outer face of the stringer at each tread edge — creating a fluid ornate silhouette along the descending diagonal. img-04 shows this masterwork detail alongside barley-sugar twisted spindles.

**Q4.b7.014** · What is a "centre-medallion spindle"?
A · A slim white painted spindle with a small circular light-oak decorative disc / medallion inserted at approximately mid-height. Creates rhythm across the balustrade without changing spindle profile. img-29.
