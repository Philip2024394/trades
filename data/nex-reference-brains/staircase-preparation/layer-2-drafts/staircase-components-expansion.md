### Structural integrity rules (renovation cladding)

- **Decorative covers should not be used to hide structural problems.** *(L454)*
  - **type:** directive/rule
  - **verification note (C15 fixed):** re-tagged from "expert perspective" to "directive/rule" — this is an instruction, not a viewpoint
- Cladding hides but does not fix loose treads, damaged strings, moisture damage, or movement. Covered-over structural problems become worse — moisture trapped · movement continues · repair now requires removing the cladding as well. *(L526)*
  - **type:** expert perspective
- **Before fitting any cladding, verify:** *(L449)*
  - the staircase structure is solid *(L450)*
  - no rot or water damage *(L451)*
  - no loose treads or strings *(L452)*
  - any squeaks or movement are repaired first *(L452)*
  - **type:** directive/rule
  - **verification note (C16 fixed):** re-tagged from "process" to "directive/rule" — this is a preflight gate/verification instruction, not a procedural sequence
