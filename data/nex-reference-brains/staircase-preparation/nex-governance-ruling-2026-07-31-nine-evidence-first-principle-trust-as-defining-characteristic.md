*After reading everything you've shared today, I think there is an unwritten principle that has quietly become one of the most important parts of your Constitution.*

*It isn't about brains.*

*It isn't about metadata.*

*It isn't about routing.*

*It's about evidence.*

*I would express it like this:*

> ## Evidence First
>
> ***Never claim what has not been observed.***
>
> ***Never classify what has not been inspected.***
>
> ***Never estimate when evidence is required.***
>
> ***Unknown is preferable to incorrect.***

- images
- documents
- tools
- materials
- regulations
- customer questions

**Everything.**
