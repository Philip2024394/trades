# Knowledge Gap Register

This register catalogues **on-brain and partial-on-brain corpus questions that have no matching Reference Brain evidence**. Each entry is a genuine gap in NEX's staircase knowledge — flagged honestly.

**Locked rules:**
- Every gap here maps to a real corpus question NEX cannot fully answer today
- Never fabricate content to close a gap — gaps close only when real evidence is added to the Reference Brain
- A gap correctly identified is a **✅ SUCCESS outcome** in the 9-outcome test taxonomy, not a failure
- Gaps are a roadmap for future Reference Brain content, not a punishment metric

## How this register is populated

Each entry is generated by the corpus analyser (`scripts/nex-brain/analyse-full-corpus.mjs`):
1. For each unique corpus question, check the Reference Brain source-evidence heuristic
2. If routing is `on_brain` or `partial_on_brain` AND no evidence match → register as gap
3. Group by concept family for readability
4. Never invent an answer to close a listed gap

## Gap categories observed in the 2026-08-14 corpus

### GAP-CATEGORY A · Design language / colour / paint (~40+ questions)
Examples:
- "How do I make a narrow wooden staircase look wider using paint?"
- "What are the best color combinations for painting a rustic staircase?"
- "How can I use dark charcoal paint to modernize stair spindles?"
- "What are the best ways to finish raw white oak treads?"

**Status:** partially covered by `staircase-timbers-2026-08-14.md` §4 (choosing timber) and batches 8-10 (balustrade family scenes). Not covered: specific colour combination advice, paint brand recommendations, finish product specifics.

**How NEX responds today:** offer style/direction advice from what's in the Brain (traditional vs modern language, timber-vs-painted trade-offs), honestly note that specific paint colour or product recommendations are outside current NEX scope.

### GAP-CATEGORY B · Fabrication + framework construction (~50+ questions)
Examples:
- "How do I calculate the total rise and run of a staircase?"
- "What is the mathematical formula for laying out stair stringers?"
- "How do I cut the birdsmouth joint on stringers?"
- "How do I anchor a steel mono-stringer to a concrete floor?"

**Status:** `partial_on_brain` — some design coverage in `staircase-handrail-components-2026-08-14.md` (wedges, base rails), but NEX does not carry construction math / stringer layout / joint cutting.

**How NEX responds today:** honestly route to trade specialist. NEX helps with design decisions; construction math is a joiner's competency + carpentry reference material.

### GAP-CATEGORY C · Specific product / brand / kit recommendations (~30+ questions)
Examples:
- "What are the best waterproof lighting options for outdoor stairs?"
- "What are the best smart home sensors for detecting stair falls?"
- "What are the best battery-operated puck lights for dark stairs?"
- "What is the best non-slip tape for outdoor wooden steps?"

**Status:** gap. NEX does not recommend specific products or brands.

**How NEX responds today:** describe the *class* of product (e.g. "waterproof LED strip lights with IP rating suitable for outdoor use") without brand recommendations. Direct the customer to a lighting specialist or trade supplier for specific product picks.

### GAP-CATEGORY D · Layout / spatial / footprint calculations (~35+ questions)
Examples:
- "How do I calculate the winders for a 90-degree stair turn?"
- "How do I figure out the footprint size of a spiral staircase?"
- "What is the minimum wellhole opening size for a loft staircase?"
- "How do I determine stringer length using the Pythagorean theorem?"

**Status:** `partial_on_brain` — NEX carries the design vocabulary (helical, spiral, dog-leg, winder, switchback) but not layout math.

**How NEX responds today:** explain the layout type and its trade-offs; honestly note that specific dimensions need a stair calculator or a joiner-drawn plan against actual site measurements.

### GAP-CATEGORY E · Layout types not yet in Reference Brain (~10+ questions)

**Never say:**

- "Let me make something up..." (fabrication)
- A fabricated specific answer

## Path from gap → coverage

A gap moves off this register only when:
1. Real evidence is added to the Reference Brain (a knowledge doc · an image manifest entry · an image with sufficient tags)
2. The intent-pattern is updated to route to the new evidence
3. The corpus test re-scores as ✅ correctly answered (not gap)

Score-hacking a gap off the register by fabricating content is a **hard fail** per the never-fabricate constitution.
