CQI Reframe (Philip 2026-07-31)

Generation 2 measured routing quality (single pass rate). Generation 3 measures thinking quality (nine dimensions). Overall CQI is the geometric mean of the nine dimensions once each is measurable. Currently 3 of 9 measured · 6 await Generation 3 builds. Aligns with the philosophy: use the right processing at the right time.
