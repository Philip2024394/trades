# BWF Domestic Timber Stairs Design Guide — reference file

Spine document for many workshop-practice claims about UK domestic timber staircases. Cited across multiple other evidence files in this directory. This file is the reference stub — the other files carry the specific claims and cite this one as their source.

## What it is

The British Woodworking Federation (BWF) publishes design guidance for domestic timber staircases as part of its Stair Scheme. It sits alongside Approved Document K but covers material that Part K deliberately does not — engineering options, dimensional practice, and manufacturing method.

**Part K governs performance** (rise · going · pitch · headroom · guarding · consistency).
**BWF Design Guide covers construction** (string thickness options · housing depth · wedge fitting · joint design · timber selection).

Both are needed. Neither replaces the other.
