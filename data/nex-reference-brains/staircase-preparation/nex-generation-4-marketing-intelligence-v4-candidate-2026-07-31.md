# NEX Generation 4 · Marketing Intelligence Layer (Philip 2026-07-31 · verbatim)

## Trigger

Should ONLY activate when user goal changes from *"Help me understand staircases"* to *"Help me grow my business."*

```
User Goal
   │
   ├── Technical → Semantic Router
   └── Business → Marketing Intelligence
```

## The Eight Modules

| # | Module | Impact | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Market Analyst** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Understand the market before recommending. Inputs: search trends · customer questions · sales history · product performance · competitor observations · seasonal demand. Outputs: growing markets · declining products · new opportunities · risk warnings. |
| 2 | **Customer Insight Engine** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Empathy through evidence. Analyses FAQs · quote requests · search terms · purchase patterns · common objections. Example: customers search *"curved staircase start"* — not *"volute handrail"*. NEX adapts content accordingly. |
| 3 | **Innovation Engine** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | DISCIPLINED innovation · not random creativity. Pipeline: Customer Problem → Existing Solutions → Engineering Constraints → Manufacturing Constraints → Cost Analysis → Generate → Evaluate → Recommend. New designs only suggested if manufacturable · safe · durable · commercially viable. |
| 4 | **Campaign Planner** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Objective · target audience · key message · supporting visuals · landing page · success metrics · budget assumptions · risks. Structured campaign · not "run Facebook ads". |
| 5 | **Risk & Evidence Engine** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Every recommendation carries: Confidence · Evidence · Assumptions · Risks · Expected outcome. Differentiates NEX from generic AI marketing tools. |
| 6 | **Competitive Intelligence** | Understand the market · don't copy competitors. Which questions remain unanswered · which products underserved · which trends emerging. |
| 7 | **Opportunity Finder** | Proactive. Customers ask "Can my carpenter install the staircase?" → nobody has content · Opportunity Score 92% → recommend article + video + image. Marketing driven by customer demand. |
| 8 | **Content Composer** | Once opportunity identified: plan blog · FAQ · images · diagrams · social posts · email sequence · video script. All from same governed knowledge. |

## The Missing Module (Philip 2026-07-31)

**Marketing Evidence Engine** — every marketing recommendation must answer *"Why?"*

Example:
- **Recommend:** Reclaimed wood content
- **Evidence:** 18 customer enquiries · rising search interest · competitor coverage weak
- **Confidence:** 82%
- **Risk:** Low
- **Expected benefit:** Increase qualified enquiries

## Opportunity Scoring Framework

Every idea receives a weighted score:

| Factor | Weight |
|---|---|
| Customer demand | 30% |
| Profit potential | 20% |
| Engineering feasibility | 15% |
| Manufacturing feasibility | 15% |
| Marketing uniqueness | 10% |
| Safety & compliance | 10% |

Total ≥ threshold → recommended. Prevents intuition-driven idea generation.

## Explicit Rejection (Philip's discipline)

**❌ NOT** — aim for NEX to invent marketing ideas simply because they are "new."

**✅ Instead** — *"NEX should generate ideas that are novel, evidence-supported, technically feasible, commercially viable, and safe to implement. Novelty by itself isn't enough. The most valuable ideas are the ones that are both original and practical."*

## Constitutional Anchor (Philip 2026-07-31 · locked in NEX-CONSTITUTION-v1.md Principle 12)

> *"NEX never recommends a strategy because it is fashionable. It recommends a strategy because the available evidence indicates it is likely to create value while remaining technically, commercially, and ethically sound."*

## Final Architecture (with Marketing layered on)

```
UNDERSTANDING   →   KNOWLEDGE           →   REASONING            →   EVIDENCE          →   GOVERNANCE
User State           Engineering              Technical Advice          Confidence            Creator Approval
Intent               Manufacturing            Diagnosis                 Calculations          Protected Core
Subject              Installation             Estimation                Historical Results    Validation
                     Marketing                Marketing Strategy        Opportunity Score
```

*"Marketing recommendations should be held to the same standard as engineering advice: explainable, measurable, and evidence-based."*
