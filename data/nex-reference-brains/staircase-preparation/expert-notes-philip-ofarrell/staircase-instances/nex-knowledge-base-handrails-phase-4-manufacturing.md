# Handrails · Phase 4 · Manufacturing · CNC · Joinery · Curves · Bends · Joints & Quality Control

## 1. From Raw Material to Finished Handrail

A premium handrail is not simply a length of timber or metal. It is an engineered component designed to achieve correct staircase geometry · comfortable grip · structural connection · visual continuity · long-term durability.

**Manufacturing quality affects appearance · installation time · safety · customer satisfaction.**

## 2. Timber Handrail Manufacturing Process

**Stage 1 · Material Selection** — timber species · moisture content · grain direction · defects · colour matching. Premium projects often select boards specifically for straight grain · consistent appearance · stability.

## 3. Timber Preparation

Cutting to length · flattening · machining · removing defects. The goal is a stable blank ready for profiling.

## 4. Profiling the Handrail

**Traditional Router Profiling** — standard profiles · repeat production.

**CNC Machining** — bespoke profiles · complex designs · high accuracy. Allows repeatability · accurate curves · custom shapes.

## 5. Straight Timber Handrails

The simplest type. Used for straight flights · wall rails · standard staircase runs. Manufacturing involves machining profile · sanding · finishing.

## 6. Curved Handrails

Curved staircases require specialist manufacturing. Examples: spiral stairs · sweeping staircases · curved landings.

## 7. Methods Used to Create Curved Timber Handrails

**Method 1 · Laminated Construction** — layers of timber are glued together around a former. Strong · stable · accurate curves. Used for luxury staircases.

**Method 2 · Steam Bending** — timber is softened using heat and moisture. Traditional craftsmanship. Timber species dependent · complex shapes are difficult.

**Method 3 · CNC Carved Curves** — a machine creates the required geometry. Used for complex architectural designs.

## 8. Handrail Bends and Easings

**Gooseneck** — a rise or fall transition between levels. Landing changes · staircase returns.

**Volute** — a spiral ending detail. Common in traditional staircases.

**Quarter Turn Easing** — where staircase changes direction.

**Continuous Curves** — luxury feature staircases.

## 9. Handrail Jointing

High-quality joints should be strong · accurate · visually discreet.

**Traditional Timber Joints** — mortise and tenon · scarf joints · housed joints.

**Modern Hidden Fixings** — threaded rods · concealed connectors · specialist brackets. Cleaner appearance.

## 10. Metal Handrail Manufacturing

**Stainless Steel Process** — cutting · forming · welding · grinding · polishing · finishing.

## 11. Stainless Steel Bending

Methods: tube bending · rolling · fabrication around templates.

Accuracy is important because reflections show imperfections · joints become visible.

## 12. Brass and Bronze Manufacturing

Luxury metals require specialist finishing. Casting · machining · polishing · patination.

## 13. Glass Handrail Systems

Glass-associated handrails require precise measurements · correct fittings · engineered support.

Components include clamps · channels · posts · top rails.

## 14. Factory Quality Control

**Dimensions** — length · profile · angles · connection points.

**Surface Quality** — scratches · sanding marks · finish consistency.

**Joint Quality** — alignment · strength · appearance.

**Material Quality** — timber movement · metal finish · glass condition.

## 15. Common Manufacturing Failures

**Timber Cracking** — poor timber selection · moisture problems · incorrect fixing.

**Poor Curves** — incorrect templates · inaccurate measurements.

**Visible Joints** — poor craftsmanship · wrong joining method.

**Uneven Finish** — inconsistent sanding · incorrect coating.

## 16. Bespoke Handrail Manufacturing Workflow

Customer idea → Site measurements → CAD drawing → Material selection → Manufacturing → Quality inspection → Installation → Final adjustment.

## 18. Premium Manufacturing Rule

**The quality of a staircase is often judged by the details people touch:** handrail smoothness · joints · curves · finish · connection points.

A premium handrail is a combination of engineering and craftsmanship.
