## Customer Question

> ***Can my staircase maker also make a matching hallway table or other furniture?***

*In many cases, yes.*

*Many bespoke staircase manufacturers are skilled joiners who can also produce complementary furniture using the same timber, finishes and craftsmanship as the staircase. This allows homeowners to create a consistent look throughout their property.*

## Matching Furniture That Can Often Be Made

A staircase maker may be able to manufacture:

- Hallway console tables
- Side tables
- Coffee tables
- Dining tables
- Benches
- Window seats
- Storage units
- Shelving
- Floating shelves
- Oak mantels
- Boot room furniture
- Under-stair storage
- Bookcases
- Feature wall panelling
- Matching handrail display shelves

*Using the same timber species, grain selection and finish helps create a coordinated interior design.*

## Advantages

Having the staircase and furniture made together offers several benefits:

- Matching timber species.
- Consistent colour and grain selection.
- Identical stain or clear finish.
- Matching edge profiles and detailing.
- Better visual continuity throughout the home.
- Efficient use of offcuts from staircase production where suitable.
- One manufacturer managing both projects.

## Things to Consider

*Not every staircase manufacturer offers bespoke furniture as part of their services.*

Factors include:

- Workshop capacity.
- Machinery available.
- Production schedule.
- Design complexity.
- Size of the furniture project.

*Some companies specialise exclusively in staircases, while others operate as full bespoke joinery workshops capable of manufacturing a wide range of timber products.*

## NEX Summary

*Many bespoke staircase manufacturers have the skills and equipment to produce matching furniture alongside a staircase. Hallway tables, benches, shelving, under-stair storage, mantels and other bespoke joinery can often be manufactured using the same timber, grain selection and finish as the staircase. Producing these items together helps achieve a consistent appearance throughout the home while ensuring materials and finishes complement one another. Availability depends on the manufacturer's services, workshop capacity and production schedule, so it's always worth asking if matching furniture can be included as part of the project.*
