## Overall NEX Assessment

*This is a traditional closed-string oak staircase designed around a 180° turn using three winders instead of an intermediate landing. The staircase features:*

- Closed-string construction with housed treads and closed risers.
- Three winder steps forming a smooth 180° turn.
- A curved bullnose starting step that creates a welcoming entrance.
- Square oak balusters and square newel posts with pyramid caps.
- A matching first-floor gallery balustrade that continues the design around the landing.
- Natural oak finish paired with white walls and oak internal doors, giving the home a classic yet contemporary appearance.

*For NEX Premium, this should be stored as a parametric staircase family where the system can vary the number of treads, stair width, timber species, baluster design, newel style, starting step, gallery layout, and turning method (winders or landing) while keeping the same fundamental staircase architecture.*
