## NEX Design Summary

*This is a contemporary luxury straight-flight staircase with closed-string construction and a dark walnut timber finish. The entrance is emphasized by a three-tier feature platform wrapped in warm LED lighting. The staircase uses slim brushed stainless-steel balusters with a rectangular walnut handrail, while the standout feature is the pair of illuminated square newel posts, each incorporating vertical recessed LED light panels and illuminated caps. An LED strip mounted along the wall above the handrail creates a continuous architectural wash of light, reinforcing the modern aesthetic.*

*For NEX Premium, this design should be stored as a parametric staircase family where the timber species, stain colour, stair dimensions, newel design, baluster type, LED configuration, handrail profile, and entrance platform can all be interchanged while preserving the same overall architectural style.*
