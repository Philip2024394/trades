# Modern Floating Glass Staircase

![Luxury contemporary floating staircase with thick timber floating treads, frameless structural glass balustrades and concealed LED lighting integrated into a decorative stone base bed, set in a double-height open-plan entrance hall](https://ik.imagekit.io/5vv5pw26q/Untitledxcxcdvdfsdfdfdsasddsfsdfdfsdfasdssdsasdddsfsdfdssdsddasdasd.png)

Modern engineering and architectural design can transform a staircase into a striking feature rather than simply a way of moving between floors.

## About this staircase style

A luxury contemporary floating staircase typically includes:

- Floating timber treads
- Frameless toughened glass balustrades
- Concealed steel support structure (hidden within the wall or central support)
- Open riser design
- Contemporary minimalist styling
- Feature LED lighting around the staircase base
- Decorative stone bed beneath the staircase
- Large open entrance hall with double-height ceiling
- Glass landing balustrade to match the staircase
- Clean architectural lines with premium finishes

This style is commonly specified in luxury new-build homes, architect-designed properties and high-end renovations.

## Popular design choices

This type of staircase can be customised with:

- Oak, ash, walnut or other hardwood treads
- Clear, low-iron or tinted glass
- Timber or metal handrails
- Concealed LED tread lighting
- LED lighting beneath the staircase
- Stone, porcelain or timber flooring
- Painted or exposed steel supports
- Open or enclosed feature areas beneath the stairs

Many homeowners also incorporate feature walls, architectural lighting or indoor planting to further enhance the staircase as a focal point.

## Is this style suitable for every home?

Not always.

Floating staircases generally require careful structural design and are best planned early in a new build or major renovation. In some existing homes, additional steelwork or wall reinforcement may be needed to support the staircase safely.

If a true floating staircase isn't practical, manufacturers can often achieve a very similar appearance using alternative structural designs.
