# NEX Premium Straight Flight Staircase

*This concept image represents a NEX Premium Straight Flight Staircase. It is more of a future architectural visualization than a conventional staircase, blending premium joinery with integrated lighting and architectural features. Some elements are realistic and manufacturable today, while others (such as the illuminated newel posts) are conceptual design features rather than standard staircase components.*

## Staircase Type

- Single straight-flight staircase
- Continuous flight
- Open cut-string design
- Premium residential feature staircase
- Contemporary architectural style
- Suitable for open-plan entrance halls or feature living spaces

## Construction

### Left Side

Cut string

**Features:**
- Exposed tread profiles
- Oak string
- Black outer trim creating a floating visual edge
- Modern square geometry

### Right Side

Wall string
- Painted white
- Simple contemporary finish
- Flush against plaster wall

## Timber

Appears to be premium European Oak

**Finish:**
- Natural matte lacquer
- Warm golden tone
- Consistent grain selection
- Carefully colour matched

**Used on:**
- Treads
- Strings
- Handrail
- Balusters
- Landing rail

## Treads

Open appearance

**Features:**
- Thick oak treads
- Square nosing
- Clean modern profile
- Precision-machined edges
- LED lighting beneath each tread

The lighting creates the illusion of floating steps.

## Risers

Closed risers

Integrated concealed LED strip lighting beneath each tread illuminates the riser below.

This lighting is technically achievable today but requires concealed wiring and removable access where maintenance is needed.

## Handrail

**Location:** Left side only

**Style:**
- Continuous oak handrail
- Square contemporary profile
- Minimal interruptions
- Flush transitions

No wall-mounted handrail is shown.

## Balusters

**Material:** Square timber balusters

**Colour:** Natural oak

**Design:**
- Slim square profile
- Two per tread appearance
- Uniform spacing
- Modern interpretation of traditional timber balusters

Black metal base shoes visually anchor each baluster to the string.

## Newel Posts

Large architectural feature newels

**Material:** Black painted timber or engineered composite cladding over a structural timber core.

**Features include:**
- Oak cap
- Integrated vertical LED light
- Hidden planter niche
- Concealed service cavity for electrical components
- Flush access panel (suggested by the design)

These newels are a premium architectural statement rather than a traditional staircase detail.

## Lighting

- LED under every tread
- Continuous handrail lighting effect
- Vertical illuminated newel posts
- Wall-mounted architectural LED cubes
- Warm colour temperature (approximately 2700–3000 K)

The lighting is layered to emphasize the staircase without overpowering the timber.

## Flooring

Wide-plank oak flooring

**Finish:**

- Continuous throughout the room

The flooring is coordinated with the staircase timber.

## Walls

Smooth painted plaster

**Colour:** Warm greige / soft beige

Minimal decoration to allow the staircase to remain the focal point.

## Interior Style

Luxury contemporary

## Artwork

Minimal black-framed artwork arranged in a gallery composition, reinforcing the modern aesthetic.

## Furniture

Simple painted console table with oak top

Decor kept intentionally understated to maintain focus on the staircase.

## Manufacturing Assessment

### Realistic Today

- Straight flight construction
- Cut string
- Oak treads
- Timber balusters
- Contemporary handrail
- Concealed LED tread lighting
- Modular balustrade components

### Conceptual / Premium Features

- Illuminated newel posts with integrated vertical lighting
- Concealed planter within the newel
- Extensive integrated electrical routing
- Decorative black architectural string trim

These features are feasible but would require bespoke design, careful engineering, and accessible service routes.

## NEX Design Philosophy

This staircase aligns well with a future NEX Premium approach because every major element can be understood as part of a modular system:

- Structural core: strings, treads, risers, and newels.
- Replaceable balustrade components.
- Serviceable LED lighting with concealed wiring.
- Oak components designed for refinishing or replacement.
- Lighting treated as an enhancement rather than the primary feature.
- Craftsmanship remains the defining characteristic.
