# NEX – Detailed Visual Analysis

*This is a straight-flight industrial luxury staircase with a warm contemporary loft interior. The design blends dark timber, exposed steel, diamond plate steel, and concealed LED lighting.*

## Stair Structure

- Straight staircase with approximately 13 visible treads leading to a landing.
- Closed string construction on both sides.
- Both stair strings are heavy solid timber with a dark walnut/aged oak stain.
- Strings appear approximately 45–55mm thick with substantial visual mass.
- Timber treads project slightly past the risers.
- No stair nosing overhang beyond the timber edge.

## Treads

- Solid hardwood treads.
- Dark stained timber with visible natural grain.
- Approximately 35–40mm thick.
- Satin/matt finish.
- Full-width treads between both strings.

## Risers

- Every riser is clad in steel diamond tread plate.
- Plate has the classic raised checker pattern.
- Steel has a brushed/aged industrial appearance.
- Plates fit tightly between timber treads.
- Gives an industrial warehouse aesthetic.

## Stair Lighting

- Warm white concealed LED strip under every tread.
- Light washes evenly down each steel riser.
- Continuous lighting across full stair width.
- Approximately 2700–3000K warm colour temperature.
- LEDs are hidden from direct view.

## Balustrade

Located on the open side only.

### Balusters

- Slim vertical round steel balusters.
- Even spacing.
- Dark graphite or gunmetal finish.
- Each baluster sits inside a small cylindrical base shoe.
- Top of each baluster fixes into underside of handrail.
- Modern industrial appearance.

### Handrail

- Timber handrail matching stair strings.
- Thick rectangular profile.
- Slightly eased edges.
- Same stain and finish as treads and strings.
- Mounted directly over balusters.
- Continuous from bottom newel to upper landing.
- No volutes or decorative fittings.

## Newel Post

One large feature newel at stair start.

**Construction:**

- Fabricated from heavy steel.
- Square box-section appearance.
- Rusted/weathered corten-style finish.
- Decorative oversized structural bolts on all visible faces.
- Heavy welded base plate.
- Base plate bolted into floor.
- Industrial fabricated look rather than traditional timber joinery.

**Approximate size:**

- Around 170–200mm square.
- Visually oversized as a statement feature.

## Fixings

Industrial detailing throughout:

- Oversized exposed bolts.
- Heavy steel brackets.
- Structural appearance.
- Decorative engineering aesthetic.

## Landing

- Stair continues to an upper landing.
- Same timber handrail continues.
- Same baluster spacing.
- Same timber string.
- Landing edge matches stair construction.

## Walls

- Smooth painted white walls.
- Minimal decoration.
- Modern recessed square wall lights.

## Wall Lighting

- Square recessed LED wall lights.
- Warm white.
- Installed along stair wall.
- Even vertical spacing.

## Flooring

- Wide plank timber flooring.
- Mid-brown natural oak tones.
- Matt finish.

## Interior Style

Industrial modern loft.

**Materials include:**

- Brick
- Timber
- Steel
- Warm lighting
- Black metal
- Rustic finishes

## Background

- Exposed brick walls.
- Brick archway.
- Modern artwork.
- Indoor plant beneath staircase.
- Leather chair visible in adjoining room.
- Minimalist décor.
- High-end residential interior.

## Colour Palette

- Dark walnut timber
- Gunmetal steel
- Rusted steel
- Warm LED lighting
- White walls
- Exposed brick
- Brown timber flooring
- Black accents

## Overall Design Language

*The staircase combines traditional heavy timber construction with industrial fabricated steel detailing. The rusted steel feature newel, exposed bolts, diamond plate risers, and concealed LED lighting create a premium industrial aesthetic, while the dark timber strings, treads, and handrail soften the design into a luxury residential staircase. The result is a robust, architectural focal point that feels engineered rather than decorative.*
