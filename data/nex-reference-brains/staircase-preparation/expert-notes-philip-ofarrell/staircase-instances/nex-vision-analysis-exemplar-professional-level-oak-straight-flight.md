# Staircase Vision Analysis

## Staircase Type

- Straight flight staircase
- Single flight from ground floor to first floor
- Closed string construction
- Residential staircase
- Contemporary minimalist design
- Symmetrical layout
- **Approximate pitch: 40–42°**

## Overall Dimensions (Estimated)

- Stair width: **900–1000 mm**
- Number of visible treads: **15–16**
- **Going (tread depth): 250–270 mm**
- **Rise (riser height): 175–185 mm**
- Overall rise: approximately **2700–2900 mm**
- Handrail height: **900 mm**
- Newel post height: approximately **1050–1100 mm above floor level**

## Timber Species

- European Oak
- Quarter-sawn appearance
- Straight grain
- Light honey-brown colour
- Natural satin finish
- Clear protective lacquer or hardwax oil

## Treads

- Solid oak
- Bullnose front edge
- Approximately **32–40 mm thick**
- Full-width treads
- Uniform grain orientation
- Square ends concealed within the string

## Risers

- Matching solid oak
- Approximately **18–22 mm thick**
- Flush fitted beneath each tread
- Continuous grain appearance
- Natural oak finish

## Strings

**Wall String**

- Closed string
- Painted white to match wall
- Hidden fixing

**Outer String**

- Solid oak
- Straight profile
- Approximately **38–45 mm thick**
- Matching finish to treads

## Handrail

- Solid oak
- Traditional rounded profile
- Continuous length
- Smooth sanded finish
- Satin lacquer

## Balusters

- Square oak balusters
- Approximately **32 × 32 mm**
- Evenly spaced
- Modern minimalist style
- Vertical installation
- Mortised into handrail and base rail

## Base Rail

- Solid oak
- Matching timber
- Straight profile
- Houses all balusters

## Newel Posts

- Square solid oak construction
- Approximate section: **90 × 90 mm**
- Flat newel cap
- Clean contemporary edges
- Structural support for handrail

## Flooring

- European oak plank flooring
- Wide boards
- Natural finish
- Colour matched with staircase
- Satin sheen

## Joinery Quality

- Bespoke manufacture
- Tight mitred joints
- Precision fitted
- Minimal visible gaps
- Furniture-grade finish

## Construction Method

- Traditional housed string staircase
- Timber mortise and tenon joints
- Mechanical fixings concealed
- Glued timber assembly
- Site-installed

## Finish

- Smooth sanded surface
- Natural oak colouring
- Low-sheen satin coating
- Durable protective finish

## Interior Environment

- White painted walls
- White skirting boards
- White architraves
- Oak internal doors
- Modern residential hallway
- Bright natural daylight
- Clean minimalist décor

## Lighting

- Natural daylight from adjacent rooms
- Soft ambient illumination
- Even lighting across staircase
- No harsh shadows

## Condition

- Brand new installation
- Excellent workmanship
- No visible defects
- No scratches, dents, or wear

## Architectural Style

- Modern
- Scandinavian influence
- Contemporary residential
- Minimalist timber detailing
- Warm natural materials
