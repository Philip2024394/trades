# Contemporary Straight Flight Closed String Staircase with Built-in Under-Stair Storage

*This image shows a contemporary straight flight timber staircase with a closed string design and a fully integrated under-stair storage and display unit. The staircase combines painted structural components with stained hardwood and black metal balusters, creating a clean architectural appearance while making full use of the space beneath the staircase.*

*Unlike traditional staircases with open space underneath, this design transforms the under-stair area into a functional built-in feature.*

## Walking Line

The walking line begins at the centre of the bullnose starting step and continues in a straight line to the upper floor.

This staircase has: No turns · No winders · No intermediate landing · No change in direction.

*It is the most direct and efficient staircase layout.*

## Staircase Layout

The staircase consists of: Bullnose starting step · Straight flight · Closed string · Closed risers · Contemporary balustrade · Upper floor landing · Fully integrated under-stair storage system.

## Stair Parts

### Starting Step

- Bullnose starting step · Semi-circular front edge · Wider than the staircase · Creates an attractive entrance · Supports the first newel post

### Treads

- **Material:** Hardwood timber · **Colour:** Medium walnut brown · **Finish:** Smooth satin lacquer
- **Construction:** Closed riser staircase · Rounded nosings · Consistent tread spacing

### Risers

- **Material:** Timber · **Colour:** White painted finish
- **Style:** Closed risers · Smooth painted surface · Clean contemporary appearance

The contrast between the white risers and walnut treads creates a balanced two-tone design.

### Strings

**This staircase uses a closed string construction.**

- Smooth continuous side string · Hidden tread ends · Hidden riser ends · No exposed stair joints · No decorative cut string profile · No string brackets

The uninterrupted diagonal line of the string is the defining feature of a closed string staircase.

### Handrail

- **Material:** Hardwood timber · **Colour:** Medium walnut brown
- **Style:** Straight contemporary profile · Continuous pitch · Matches the treads and newel posts

### Balusters

- **Material:** Powder-coated steel · **Colour:** Matte black
- **Style:** Slim square profile · Contemporary minimalist design · Evenly spaced · Mounted between the handrail and string

### Newel Posts

- **Material:** Hardwood timber · **Colour:** Medium walnut brown
- **Style:** Large square contemporary newel posts · Decorative recessed panel detailing · Heavy architectural appearance · Pyramid caps
- **Locations:** Bottom of staircase · Top of staircase

*These substantial newel posts provide both structural support and a visual focal point.*

## Teaching Notes for Nex

*This image demonstrates that a staircase can be more than just a means of moving between floors—it can become an integrated architectural feature within the home.*

From a staircase construction perspective, this is a straight flight, closed string staircase. The smooth, continuous side string conceals the ends of the treads and risers, creating the clean, modern appearance typical of closed string construction.

The image also shows how the unused triangular space beneath a straight staircase can be transformed into custom-built under-stair cabinetry. Rather than leaving the area open, it has been fitted with shelving, cupboards, drawers and integrated LED lighting to create a practical and visually appealing storage solution.

*For Nex, this image should be classified under both Staircase Construction and Under-Stair Design Ideas. It teaches users not only how to identify a closed string staircase, but also how the space beneath a staircase can be incorporated into the overall interior design through bespoke joinery and integrated storage.*
