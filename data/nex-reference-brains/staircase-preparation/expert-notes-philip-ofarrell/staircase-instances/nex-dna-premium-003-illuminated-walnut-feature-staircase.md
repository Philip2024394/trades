## Unique Design Features Recognized by NEX

*This staircase stands out because it combines traditional timber construction with integrated architectural lighting:*

- Straight closed-string staircase with concealed structural joinery.
- Dark walnut-stained oak with a consistent grain and premium satin finish.
- Large illuminated newel post featuring recessed vertical LED light channels.
- Continuous warm-white LED lighting beneath each tread and around the entrance step, creating a floating effect.
- Rectangular timber balusters with concealed stainless-steel support rods, blending timber warmth with modern engineering.
- Double bullnose starting step with rounded front corners and integrated perimeter lighting.
- Luxury contemporary aesthetic, suitable for high-end custom homes, architect-designed residences, and boutique hospitality projects.

*This design belongs in a NEX Premium Signature collection because the integrated lighting is a core part of both its visual identity and its manufacturing specification.*
