## NEX Assessment

*NEX should classify this as an ultra-luxury architectural floating staircase. Its defining characteristics include:*

- A straight-flight floating staircase with the appearance of cantilevered solid oak treads supported by a concealed structural steel spine.
- Frameless low-iron glass balustrades fixed into recessed base channels, creating an uninterrupted transparent guard.
- A slim brushed brass handrail mounted to the glass, adding warmth and a premium finish.
- Integrated warm-white LED lighting recessed beneath every tread and around the three-tier feature entrance platform, producing a floating effect.
- A double-height contemporary interior with large-format concrete-effect wall panels, stone flooring, panoramic glazing, and an open mezzanine landing.
