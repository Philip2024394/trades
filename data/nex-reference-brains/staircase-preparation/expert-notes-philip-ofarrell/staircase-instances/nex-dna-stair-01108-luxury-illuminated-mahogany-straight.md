## NEX Design Summary

*This is a premium straight-flight closed-string staircase manufactured from genuine mahogany, distinguished by its rich reddish-brown tone and elegant straight grain. The design combines traditional hardwood craftsmanship with modern architectural detailing. Key features include a two-tier entrance platform, large illuminated square newel posts with vertical recessed LED panels, a continuous wall-mounted LED wash, brushed stainless-steel round balusters, and brushed aluminium anti-slip nosing inserts integrated into each tread. The overall appearance is clean, symmetrical, and suited to high-end contemporary residential interiors.*

*For NEX Premium, this staircase should be stored as a parametric family with interchangeable timber species, stair width, tread count, balustrade materials, lighting options, and newel designs while preserving its core architectural proportions and manufacturing logic.*
