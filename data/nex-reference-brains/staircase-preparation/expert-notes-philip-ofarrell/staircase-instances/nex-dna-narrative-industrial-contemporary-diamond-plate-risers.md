## Architectural Style

*Inspired by modern warehouse conversions and luxury loft homes, this staircase combines premium hardwood with industrial steel elements. The diamond plate risers, exposed hardware, and dark timber framing create a bold architectural statement while maintaining a warm, high-end finish suitable for luxury residential or boutique commercial interiors.*

## NEX Material Specification

- **Treads:** Solid walnut hardwood
- **Strings:** Dark stained hardwood
- **Handrail:** Solid walnut hardwood
- **Newel Posts:** Heavy square hardwood posts
- **Balusters:** Black powder-coated steel horizontal rails
- **Risers:** Black steel diamond tread plate
- **Fixings:** Exposed stainless steel architectural bolts
- **Finish:** Premium matte timber oil with powder-coated metal components

## Overall Description

*The NEX Premium Industrial Straight Flight Staircase is a luxury staircase that blends traditional timber craftsmanship with industrial steel detailing. The rich walnut treads contrast with dark timber strings and black diamond plate risers, while exposed architectural bolts and steel balustrades give the staircase a distinctive engineered appearance. It is designed for premium homes, loft apartments, commercial offices, and modern architectural spaces where a bold industrial aesthetic is desired.*
