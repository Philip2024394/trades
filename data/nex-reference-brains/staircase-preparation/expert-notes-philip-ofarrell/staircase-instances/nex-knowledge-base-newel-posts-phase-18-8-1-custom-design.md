# Newel Posts · Phase 18.8.1 · Custom Design & Bespoke Manufacturing

## 1. From Standard Component to Bespoke Feature

A newel post can be a standard catalogue item · a modified existing design · a fully bespoke manufactured piece.

Premium staircases often use newels as a **design feature**, not just a structural component. A custom newel can define the entire staircase character.

Examples: oversized oak feature newel · sculpted timber post · stainless steel architectural post · brass luxury detail · glass integrated system · mixed-material statement piece.

## 2. Can I Get My Own Newel Post Design Made?

**Yes.** A customer can create a custom newel post design if the manufacturer has the capability.

The design can begin from customer sketch · architect drawing · CAD file · staircase photograph · Nex concept image · inspiration image.

The manufacturer then checks structural requirements · material suitability · manufacturing method · installation method.

## 3. Custom Newel Design Process

**Step 1 · Design Concept** — customer provides style preference · staircase type · material preference · dimensions · finish. Example: *"I want a 120mm square oak newel with a modern flat cap and hidden fixing."*

**Step 2 · Design Development** — manufacturer determines proportions · joinery · fixing method · machining requirements. Possible tools: CAD · CNC programming · 3D modelling.

**Step 3 · Engineering Review** — design must consider load requirements · handrail connection · balustrade fixing · staircase movement · installation method. **A beautiful design must still perform correctly.**

**Step 4 · Manufacturing** — methods may include: (Timber) machining · turning · CNC carving · hand finishing. (Metal) cutting · welding · polishing · powder coating. (Glass Systems) specialist fabrication · precision fixing components.

## 4. Can Any Shape Be Made?

Not every shape is practical. A design must balance appearance · strength · manufacturing ability · cost.

**Square Newels** — most common premium style. 90/100/120mm. Modern, shaker, contemporary homes.

**Turned Newels** — created on a lathe. Curves · decorative sections · traditional profiles. Classic staircases, period properties.

**Sculpted Newels** — custom carved designs. Luxury projects, feature staircases.

**Oversized Feature Newels** — large architectural posts. Entrance halls, grand staircases, commercial interiors.

## 5. Can a Newel Post Be Made From Any Material?

A newel can be manufactured from many materials — but the material must suit structural needs · fixing method · environment · design purpose.

**Timber** — oak · walnut · ash · maple. Warm appearance · traditional craftsmanship · easy to customise.

**Stainless Steel** — modern appearance · corrosion resistance · precision fabrication. Used with glass · cable systems · modern balustrades.

**Mild Steel** — strong · flexible fabrication · industrial appearance.

**Brass / Bronze** — luxury appearance · decorative impact. Premium residential · hotels · commercial interiors.

**Hybrid Newels** — combining timber · metal · glass. Example: oak body + stainless steel insert + glass balustrade.

## 6. Can Nex Create a New Newel Post Design?

Yes, Nex can help create a **design concept**.

However, Nex must separate:

**Design Concept** — Nex can provide visual ideas · style development · material suggestions · design variations.

**Manufacturing Reality** — a staircase manufacturer must confirm can it be made · correct dimensions · fixing details · safety requirements · final cost.

Correct response: *"I can help develop a newel post concept and specification. Before manufacturing, a staircase professional should review the design to confirm it can be built safely."*

## 7. Using Nex Images for Custom Designs

Customer: *"Can you make this newel post?"* — Nex should explain: the image may represent a real installed staircase · a manufacturer photograph · an architectural visual · an AI-generated concept.

The next step is to create a manufacturing brief.

## 8. Converting an Image Into a Supplier Brief

Nex extracts:

- **Design** — style · shape · proportions · details
- **Material** — timber · metal · glass · finish
- **Construction** — square or turned · fixing method · handrail connection
- **Quantity** — number of newels required

Example brief: Modern square feature newel · solid oak · approx 100mm square · natural oil · residential staircase · 4 posts · matching handrail and balustrade required.

## 9. Why Custom Newels Cost More

Additional costs come from design time · engineering · programming · specialist labour · material selection · finishing.

**A standard newel may be produced repeatedly. A bespoke newel may be produced once.**

## 10. Premium Staircase Principle

A custom newel is not simply a bigger post. It is a designed architectural element that must balance beauty · proportion · strength · craftsmanship · installation.
