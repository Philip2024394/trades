# NEX Premium Enclosed Straight Flight Staircase

## Staircase Type

- Straight-flight timber staircase
- Enclosed between two full-height walls
- Premium contemporary residential design
- Closed-riser construction
- Minimalist architectural appearance
- Traditional timber stair engineering with modern finishes

## Structural Construction

The staircase is positioned within a dedicated stairwell between two structural walls.

The walls are architectural elements enclosing the staircase and are not concealing the stair strings.

The staircase is built using timber structural strings located directly beneath the treads and risers, forming the load-bearing structure of the flight. These strings are integrated into the staircase assembly and are not visible from this viewpoint.

This allows the staircase to retain the clean appearance of an enclosed stair while remaining a conventionally manufactured timber staircase.

## Treads

**Material:** Premium European Oak

**Finish:** Natural matte lacquer or hardwax oil

**Features:**
- Full-depth timber appearance
- Square contemporary nosing
- Consistent grain selection
- Precision-machined edges
- Uniform tread depth

## Risers

**Construction:** Closed timber risers

**Material:** Matching oak finish

**Premium Feature:**

Each riser incorporates a 15 mm brushed stainless steel strip fitted horizontally across the face of the riser, immediately beneath the tread nosing.

**Purpose:**
- Premium architectural detailing
- Protects the riser edge from foot scuffs
- Creates visual definition between each step
- Reflects ambient lighting
- Adds a subtle contemporary contrast to the natural oak

The strip is decorative and protective, not a structural component.

## Stair Strings

**Construction:**
- Traditional timber structural strings
- Located directly beneath the treads and risers
- Fully integrated into the staircase construction
- Not externally visible

The enclosing walls are separate building elements and should not be interpreted as the stair strings.

## Handrail

**Location:** Right-hand side when ascending

**Style:**
- Wall-mounted
- Solid oak
- Square contemporary profile
- Concealed fixing brackets
- Continuous uninterrupted rail

No balustrade is required because both sides of the staircase are enclosed by walls.

## Balustrades

None. The surrounding walls provide the necessary edge protection within the visible section of the staircase.

## Newel Posts

None visible. The enclosed design eliminates the need for exposed newel posts within the stairwell.

## Stair Width

**Estimated finished width:** Approximately 1000–1100 mm

This wider-than-standard proportion contributes to a spacious, premium feel despite the enclosed layout.

## Flooring

**Ground floor:**
- Large-format porcelain tiles
- Warm light grey
- Matte finish
- Minimal grout joints

The tiled floor provides a durable contrast to the warm oak staircase.

## Walls

- Smooth painted plaster
- Warm white finish
- Minimal architectural detailing
- Clean, uninterrupted surfaces emphasizing the staircase geometry

## Ceiling

- Flat plaster ceiling
- Recessed LED downlights
- Concealed perimeter LED cove lighting at the upper landing

## Lighting

Lighting is architectural rather than decorative.

Includes:
- Recessed ceiling downlights
- Concealed LED cove lighting at the landing
- Natural daylight from the upper floor

The staircase itself does not include integrated tread lighting.
