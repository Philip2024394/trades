# Oak Staircase
## Why oak is the go-to timber for premium staircases

Oak is the go-to timber for premium staircases. It has a warm colour and is hardwearing, which is why it consistently comes out on top for clients wanting quality that lasts.

## European oak vs American white oak

European oak is the UK default and what most clients picture when they think of an oak staircase. American white oak is paler and gives a more modern feel, so it suits contemporary interiors where a lighter tone is wanted.

## Pairing oak with glass balustrade

Oak pairs well with glass balustrade, combining the warmth of the timber with a clean modern look. Children can safely use glass balustrades when installed properly.

## Finishes: matte vs gloss

Matte finish is popular right now on oak staircases. Avoid gloss unless a traditional look is specifically wanted.

## Staining oak darker

Oak can be stained darker, but staining always changes the character of the timber. Go into it knowing you are trading some of the natural oak feel for the darker tone.

## Making use of under stair space

Under stair space is valuable and should not be wasted. It can be used for drawers, wine storage, or a small office nook.

## Handrail quality matters

Never skimp on the handrail. People touch it every day, so it needs to feel right in the hand and be built to last.

## New build vs renovation vs loft conversion

A new build gives you the most flexibility for designing an oak staircase, as there are no existing constraints. A renovation needs to work with the existing structure, which limits some choices. A loft conversion usually needs a space saver design due to the tighter footprint.
