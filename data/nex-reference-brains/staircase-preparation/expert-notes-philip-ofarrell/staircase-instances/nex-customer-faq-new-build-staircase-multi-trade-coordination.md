## Customer Question

> ***Is installing a staircase on a new build just the responsibility of the staircase company?***

*No. While the staircase manufacturer is responsible for designing and producing the staircase, a successful installation relies on close coordination with the wider site team throughout the construction process.*

## Every Trade Has a Role

*A staircase occupies one of the most important structural spaces in a home, so changes made by one trade can affect many others.*

### Site Manager

*The site manager coordinates the construction programme and ensures the staircase installation fits within the build schedule. They also communicate design changes and resolve issues between trades.*

### Carpenters and Framers

*The structural carpentry team is responsible for building the stairwell opening, floor joists and trimmers that support the staircase.*

If site conditions change, they may need to:

- Adjust stairwell openings.
- Modify trimming joists.
- Correct floor levels.
- Alter structural framing.
- Increase or maintain headroom clearance.

*Even small framing changes can affect the staircase dimensions.*

### Plumbers

*Pipework often passes through or near staircases.*

Before installation they may need to:

- Relocate heating pipes.
- Move waste pipes.
- Reroute underfloor heating services.
- Prevent pipework from conflicting with strings or newel posts.

### Electricians

*Electrical installations around staircases require planning.*

This may include:

- Moving cables.
- Installing lighting for stairs and landings.
- Wiring LED handrails or feature lighting.
- Preparing power supplies before the staircase is fitted.

### Plasterers

*Wall finishes influence the final staircase fit.*

Plaster thickness can slightly alter:

- Staircase width.
- String gaps.
- Handrail positioning.
- Skirting and finishing details.

*Accurate measurements should reflect the stage of construction.*

### Flooring Contractors

*The finished floor level directly affects the staircase.*

Changes to flooring thickness can alter:

- The first step.
- The last step.
- Overall compliance with building regulations.

*The staircase manufacturer needs to know the final floor build-up before production.*

### Painters and Decorators

Once installed, the staircase may require:

- Priming.
- Painting.
- Staining.
- Clear lacquer.
- Final finishing after other trades have completed dusty work.

*Protecting the staircase during the remaining construction phase is equally important to avoid damage.*

## Why Communication Matters

Good communication between the staircase manufacturer and the site team helps prevent:

- Incorrect measurements.
- Staircases that no longer fit altered openings.
- Insufficient headroom.
- Service clashes with pipes or cables.
- Installation delays.
- Unexpected site modifications.
- Additional manufacturing costs.

*On larger developments, regular communication ensures everyone is working from the latest drawings and specifications.*

## NEX Summary

*A new-build staircase is the result of collaboration between many trades, not just the staircase manufacturer. Site managers, carpenters, plumbers, electricians, plasterers, flooring contractors and decorators all contribute to creating the correct space for the staircase and ensuring it can be installed safely and accurately. Changes to framing, pipework, wiring, floor levels or wall finishes can all affect the staircase design, so clear communication throughout the build is essential. When every trade works together, the staircase fits correctly, meets building regulations and integrates seamlessly into the finished home.*
