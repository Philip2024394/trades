# Newel Posts · Phase 18.5 · Glass Newel Manufacturing, CNC, Factory Processes, Quality Control and Bespoke Production

## 1. Introduction — How Premium Glass Stair Systems Are Manufactured

A high-end glass staircase is not simply a collection of glass sheets and metal parts. A professional manufacturer creates a complete engineered system: design drawings · measurements · structural calculations · material selection · glass processing · metal fabrication · timber integration (if required) · quality inspection · installation preparation.

**The quality of the finished staircase depends heavily on the accuracy of every stage before installation.** A glass panel manufactured 5mm incorrectly cannot simply be adjusted like timber.

## 2. Design Stage Before Manufacturing

### Site Survey Information

Collected: floor-to-floor height · stair width · stair angle · opening dimensions · finished floor levels · wall positions · fixing locations.

Important measurements: exact glass height · panel lengths · newel positions · handrail alignment.

### CAD Drawings

Professional systems use CAD drawings · 3D modelling · fabrication drawings. These define glass shape · drilling positions · bracket locations · post spacing · handrail details.

**Customer approval normally happens before production.**

## 3. Glass Manufacturing Process

**Step 1 · Glass Selection** — clear · low-iron · tinted · frosted · laminated · toughened. Selection depends on design · safety requirements · size · installation method.

**Step 2 · Cutting the Glass** — large sheets are cut using automated cutting tables · CNC glass cutting machines. Important: exact dimensions · clean edges · correct shapes.

**Step 3 · Edge Processing** — after cutting, glass edges are finished.

- **Flat Polish** — smooth premium edge · high-quality appearance
- **Pencil Polish** — rounded edge · softer appearance
- **Bevel Edge** — decorative luxury detail

**Step 4 · Drilling and Cut-Outs** — for systems using clamps or point fixing, glass may require holes · slots · cut-outs. **Important: glass holes cannot be drilled after toughening.** The sequence is: cut → drill → polish → toughen.

**Step 5 · Heat Treatment** — safety glass is produced through controlled heating and cooling. Purpose: increase strength · improve safety performance. **After toughening: glass cannot be cut or drilled.**

## 4. CNC Metal Manufacturing for Glass Newels

Premium metal systems use CNC processes. Materials: stainless steel · aluminium · brass · powder-coated steel.

**CNC Cutting** creates accurate post lengths · bracket plates · mounting components.

**CNC Drilling** creates fixing holes · glass clamp locations · handrail connections.

**Welding** used for custom newel assemblies · hidden brackets · special designs. Professional welding requires controlled heat · clean finish · accurate alignment.

## 5. Stainless Steel Finishing

**Brushed Stainless** — most popular. Satin lines · modern luxury.

**Mirror Polished** — reflective · high-end architectural.

**Powder Coated** — colours: black · bronze · custom finishes. Requires correct surface preparation.

## 6. Combining Timber Newels With Glass

Premium hybrid staircases require coordination between timber manufacturer · glass supplier · installer.

Example: Oak newel + glass panel system. Factory must consider timber shrinkage · fixing positions · glass tolerances · handrail alignment.

## 7. Factory Quality Control

**Glass Inspection** — thickness · dimensions · edge quality · scratches · chips · polishing quality.

**Metal Inspection** — weld quality · surface finish · dimensions · fixing holes.

**Assembly Testing** — before delivery, components may be checked for correct fit · alignment · hardware compatibility.

## 8. Digital Manufacturing and 3D Scanning

Premium manufacturers increasingly use laser measurement · 3D scanning · digital templates.

Benefits: fewer errors · accurate complex stair shapes · curved staircase precision.

Especially useful for renovation projects · curved glass · irregular walls.

## 9. Manufacturing Challenges

**Challenge 1 · Incorrect Site Measurements** — glass does not fit. Solution: professional survey · digital templates · approval drawings.

**Challenge 2 · Building Movement** — buildings are not perfectly static. Manufacturers allow for tolerances · adjustment systems · flexible connections.

**Challenge 3 · Complex Curved Glass** — curved glass requires specialist moulds · controlled heating · longer production times.

## 10. Bespoke Glass Staircase Production Timeline

**Design** — 1–3 weeks. Includes consultation · drawings · approval.

**Manufacturing** — several weeks depending on complexity. Factors: glass type · custom metalwork · timber work · finishing requirements.

**Installation Preparation** — before delivery: components labelled · hardware checked · installation drawings prepared.

## 11. Premium Questions Nex Can Answer

**"Can you make any glass staircase design?"** — many designs are possible, but the final design depends on structural requirements · available space · glass limitations · fixing methods.

**"Can curved glass be made?"** — yes, curved glass can be manufactured for bespoke staircases, but it requires specialist production and accurate templates.

**"Why does a custom staircase take longer?"** — a bespoke staircase is manufactured specifically for one property. Design approval · precision manufacturing · quality checks take more time than standard kits.

**"Can the factory make glass without measuring my house?"** — a professional manufacturer normally requires accurate site dimensions because every staircase opening is different.
