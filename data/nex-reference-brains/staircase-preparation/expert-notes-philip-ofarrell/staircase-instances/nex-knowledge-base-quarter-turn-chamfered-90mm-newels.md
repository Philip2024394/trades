## Traditional Quarter-Turn Staircase with Chamfered 90 mm Newel Posts

### Purpose

*This image shows a traditional quarter-turn staircase with a painted timber balustrade and natural oak handrails. The staircase combines classic proportions with contemporary finishes, creating a clean and timeless appearance suitable for a wide range of interior styles.*

### Staircase Configuration

**Staircase Type**

- Quarter-turn staircase (90° change in direction).
- Lower straight flight.
- Intermediate landing.
- Upper straight flight.
- Closed-string construction.
- Painted timber balustrade.

### Newel Posts

One of the defining features of this staircase is the use of **90 mm chamfered newel posts**.

**Specification**

- 90 × 90 mm solid timber newel posts.
- Chamfered corners.
- Square profile.
- Finished with flat stepped newel caps.
- Painted finish with contrasting hardwood caps.

**Functions**

The newel posts:

- Support the handrails.
- Anchor the balustrade.
- Provide structural stability.
- Support changes in direction.
- Frame the entrance and landing areas of the staircase.

*The chamfered edges soften the appearance of the posts while maintaining a strong, traditional design.*

### Handrails

- Solid timber handrails.
- Natural oak finish.
- Continuous pitch along each flight.
- Smooth, comfortable gripping profile.
- Contrasting timber against the painted balustrade.

### Balusters

The staircase features square painted timber balusters.

Characteristics include:

- Square profile.
- Even spacing.
- Clean contemporary appearance.
- Consistent alignment.
- Supporting the handrail while providing the required guarding.

### Strings

The staircase uses closed strings with painted finishes.

Functions include:

- Supporting the treads and risers.
- Carrying structural loads.
- Providing a clean side profile.
- Matching the painted balustrade system.

### Treads

Visible characteristics:

- Solid timber treads.
- Natural oak finish.
- Rounded front nosings.
- Consistent tread depth.
- Contrasting with the painted staircase framework.

### Risers

- Closed risers.
- Painted finish.
- Uniform rise throughout the staircase.
- Clean and consistent joinery.

### Stair Runner

A fitted stair runner enhances both comfort and appearance.

Features include:

- Full-length carpet runner.
- Exposed timber tread edges.
- Reduced footfall noise.
- Improved grip underfoot.
- Decorative finish while retaining the visibility of the hardwood treads.

### Timber Finish

The staircase combines two complementary finishes:

- Painted balustrade, strings, and risers.

*This two-tone finish creates contrast while highlighting the natural beauty of the timber.*
