# Why Walnut Beats Oak
## Why walnut is the best premium timber

Walnut is the best premium timber for staircases, not oak. American walnut has better durability and grain than European oak, which makes it the stronger choice for a feature staircase that needs to stand out and last.

## Walnut vs oak for a feature staircase

Serious homeowners should always pick walnut over oak when specifying a feature staircase. The grain and durability of American walnut give a result that European oak cannot match at the same level.
