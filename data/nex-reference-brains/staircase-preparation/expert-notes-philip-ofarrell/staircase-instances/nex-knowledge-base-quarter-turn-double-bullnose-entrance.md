# NEX Knowledge Base

## Quarter-Turn Staircase with Double Bullnose Entrance Steps

### Purpose

*This image shows a traditional timber quarter-turn staircase installed within a modern residential entrance hall. It demonstrates how staircase design combines structural engineering with architectural styling to create an attractive focal point.*

### Staircase Configuration

**Staircase Type**

- Quarter-turn staircase (90° change in direction).
- Lower straight flight leading to a quarter landing.
- Upper flight continues to the next floor.
- Closed-string construction.
- Timber balustrade throughout.

### Entrance Steps

One of the defining design features is the **double bullnose entrance**.

**Bottom Entrance Step**

- Full-width curved bullnose tread.
- Rounded front nosing.
- Creates an elegant and welcoming first step.
- Provides a smooth transition from the floor to the staircase.

**Second Step**

- Smaller bullnose tread.
- Continues the curved entrance detail.
- Gradually transitions into the standard staircase width.

*Double bullnose entrance steps are often selected as a premium design feature because they create a softer, more refined appearance than standard square entrance treads.*

### Strings

The staircase includes:

- **Wall string** supporting the staircase along the wall.
- **Outer string** supporting the treads, risers, and balustrade.

*The strings form the primary structural members of the staircase, carrying the loads of the treads and risers while maintaining the alignment of the flight.*

### Treads

Visible characteristics:

- Solid timber construction.
- Rounded front nosings.
- Consistent tread depth.
- Natural hardwood finish.
- Continuous timber grain.

### Risers

- Closed risers.
- Matching timber finish.
- Uniform rise throughout the staircase.
- Clean, precise joinery.

### Newel Posts

Several newel posts are visible.

Their functions include:

- Supporting the handrail.
- Anchoring the balustrade.
- Providing structural stability.
- Supporting changes in direction.
- Defining the entrance to the staircase.

*Each newel is finished with a simple square cap, creating a timeless and understated appearance.*

### Handrail

- Continuous timber handrail.
- Mounted above the balusters.
- Follows the pitch of the staircase.
- Consistent profile throughout the flight.

### Balusters

- Square timber balusters.
- Evenly spaced.
- Simple, clean profile.
- Support the handrail while providing the required guarding.

### Timber Finish

The staircase appears to feature:

- Natural hardwood.
- Clear lacquer or hardwax oil finish.
- Satin sheen.
- Visible timber grain.

### Interior Integration

Features include:

- Matching timber internal doors.

- Neutral wall colours.
- Modern pendant and wall lighting.
- Contemporary furnishings.

Visible premium features include:
