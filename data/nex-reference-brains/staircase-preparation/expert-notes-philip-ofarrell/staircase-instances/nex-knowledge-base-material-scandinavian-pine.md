# Scandinavian Pine (Redwood)

**Material ID:** MAT-005
**Common names:** Pine · Scandinavian Pine · European Redwood · Pinus sylvestris
**Category:** timber softwood
**Origin:** Northern Europe (Scandinavia, Baltic states, Russia)

## Description

Scandinavian Pine is the UK default painted-staircase timber — economical, stable, works brilliantly under paint. For a natural clear-finished staircase pine is softer than the hardwoods (marks more easily) but for painted cottage/coastal/Scandi looks it's genuinely the best choice: takes primer + paint smoother than any hardwood.

## Appearance

- **Colour:** Pale yellow to light amber, darkens to warm honey with age
- **Grain:** Straight, often with prominent knots
- **Texture:** Fine to medium
- **Variation:** High — knot patterns vary widely; grade determines how much character is visible

## Physical properties

- **Durability rating:** 3/5
- **Hardness (Janka):** 380 lbf / 1690 N
- **Density:** 510 kg/m³
- **Machining rating:** 5/5
