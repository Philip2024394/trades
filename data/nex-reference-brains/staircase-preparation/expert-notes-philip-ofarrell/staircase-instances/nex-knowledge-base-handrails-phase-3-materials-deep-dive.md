# Handrails · Phase 3 · Materials Deep Dive

## Oak · Walnut · Metal · Brass · Stainless Steel · Glass Systems & Finishes

## 1. Choosing the Correct Handrail Material

A handrail is touched every day, so material choice affects comfort · appearance · durability · maintenance · cost · relationship with the staircase design.

**A premium staircase does not choose a handrail separately.** It considers the complete system: newels · balusters · treads · risers · strings · flooring · interior architecture.

## 2. Timber Handrails · The Traditional Premium Choice

Timber remains one of the most popular handrail materials because it provides natural warmth · comfortable grip · craftsmanship · easy customisation.

Common species: oak · walnut · ash · maple · mahogany · pine.

## 3. Oak Handrails

Strong · durable · attractive grain · works with modern and traditional designs.

Used with oak stairs · glass balustrades · stainless steel details · shaker staircases.

**Finishes** — Natural Oil (shows grain · warmth · natural character) · Clear Lacquer (protection · lower maintenance) · Stained Oak (darker luxury appearance · matching with flooring).

## 4. Walnut Handrails

Luxury timber. Dark colour · rich grain · premium appearance. Designer homes · feature staircases · luxury interiors.

Consideration: higher cost · matching timber availability.

## 5. Ash Handrails

Light colour · clean grain · modern appearance. Scandinavian designs · minimalist interiors.

## 6. Maple Handrails

Pale colour · smooth grain · contemporary appearance. Modern staircases · bright interiors.

## 7. Painted Timber Handrails

Common combinations: painted newels + timber handrail · painted balusters + oak handrail. Design flexibility · easier colour matching.

## 8. Stainless Steel Handrails

Common in modern staircase design. Used with glass panels · metal balustrades · floating staircases.

**304 Stainless Steel** — normal indoor environments. Clean appearance · good corrosion resistance.

**316 Stainless Steel** — extra corrosion resistance needed. Coastal homes · humid environments · swimming pool areas.

## 9. Stainless Steel Finishes

**Brushed Satin** — most popular. Modern · subtle · low reflection.

**Polished** — mirror-like · luxury appearance.

**Black Powder Coated** — architectural · contemporary.

## 10. Brass Handrails

Luxury statement. Hotels · premium homes · feature staircases. Warm metallic colour · timeless appearance.

**Brass Finishes** — polished · brushed · aged · antique.

## 11. Bronze Handrails

Rich colour · handcrafted appearance · luxury character. Bespoke interiors · heritage-style projects.

## 12. Aluminium Handrails

Lightweight construction · corrosion resistance · modern appearance. Commercial · exterior systems.

## 13. Glass Staircase Handrails

Glass systems create openness · light · modern architecture. Combined with oak top rail · stainless steel top rail · brass details.

## 14. Glass Types Used With Staircases

**Clear Toughened Glass** — most common. Transparency · clean appearance.

**Low Iron Glass** — premium installations. Clearer appearance · reduced green tint.

**Laminated Glass** — additional safety performance required.

## 15. Luxury Material Combinations

**Oak + Glass** — warm + modern. Contemporary homes.

**Oak + Stainless Steel** — industrial luxury.

**Brass + Walnut** — high-end classic.

**Black Steel + Oak** — modern architectural.

## 16. Outdoor and Coastal Considerations

Consider salt air · moisture · temperature changes.

Coastal: prefer 316 stainless steel · suitable timber protection · marine-grade finishes.

## 17. Handrail Maintenance

**Timber** — cleaning · occasional refinishing.

**Stainless Steel** — cleaning to remove marks · correct products.

**Brass** — polishing or accepting natural patina.

**Glass** — regular cleaning · checking fittings.

## 18. Manufacturer Decision Process

Location · interior style · budget · staircase structure · maintenance expectations · long-term appearance.

## 19. Nex Recommendation Logic

Customer: *"What material should my handrail be?"* — Nex should not answer with one material. It should ask:

- What style?
- Indoor/outdoor?
- Existing staircase?
- Other materials already selected?
- Maintenance preference?

## 20. Premium Staircase Rule

**The best handrail material is not always the most expensive.** It is the material that creates the correct balance between touch · durability · architecture · craftsmanship.
