# Newel Posts · Phase 18.8.6 · Custom Newel Supplier Connection

## From Nex Design Idea to Manufacturer Brief

## 1. Turning Inspiration Into a Real Manufacturing Request

Many customers start with *"I want this staircase"* or *"Can someone make this newel post from the image?"*

**The problem is that a supplier cannot manufacture from inspiration alone.** A professional manufacturer needs clear design information · dimensions · material choice · quantity · location · installation details.

**Nex's role is to bridge the gap between: Customer idea → Professional specification → Supplier enquiry.**

## 2. Why Nex Should Prepare the Customer First

A supplier receives many enquiries.

**Weak enquiry:** *"Can you make this?"* — the supplier does not know size · material · quantity · project stage · location · budget.

**Strong enquiry:** *"I require four solid oak square newel posts approximately 100mm × 100mm for a residential staircase renovation in Ireland. I have attached a reference image and require matching handrail details."* — this shows the customer is serious.

## 3. Stage 1 · Understand The Customer

**Location** — country · region · installation area. Important because materials differ · terminology differs · suppliers differ.

**Project Type** — new staircase · renovation · replacement part · custom feature staircase.

**Customer Role** — homeowner · builder · architect · staircase installer · interior designer.

## 4. Stage 2 · Understand The Newel Requirement

**Style** — modern square · traditional turned · shaker · luxury feature · floating staircase.

**Material** — Timber (oak · walnut · ash · painted timber) · Metal (stainless steel · brass · bronze · powder-coated steel) · Mixed (oak + glass · metal + timber · metal + stone).

**Dimensions** — approximate height · width/depth · quantity.

Example: Square oak · 90mm × 90mm · 1100mm approx · 4 posts.

## 5. Stage 3 · Image Analysis

If the customer uploads an image, Nex extracts:

**Visible Design** — square or turned · cap style · base detail · decorative elements.

**Materials** — likely timber · metal finishes · glass type · colour.

**Manufacturing Difficulty:**

- **Standard** — can likely be supplied from catalogue
- **Modified** — requires adaptation
- **Bespoke** — requires specialist manufacturing

## 6. Stage 4 · Create Supplier Brief

Example:

```
CUSTOM NEWEL POST REQUEST

Customer Location:  United States
Project:            Residential staircase renovation
Component:          Custom newel posts
Quantity:           4
Preferred Material: Solid European oak
Style:              Modern square architectural
Approx Size:        100mm x 100mm
Finish:             Natural oil
Reference:          Customer supplied image
Additional Requirements: Matching handrail and balustrade
```

## 7. Stage 5 · Supplier Matching

Nex should not simply find any supplier. It should match capability.

**Timber Specialist** — oak newels · turned profiles · carved designs · traditional joinery.

**Metal Fabricator** — stainless posts · brass features · architectural details.

**Staircase Manufacturer** — complete staircase systems · matching components · installation.

**CNC Specialist** — complex profiles · repeat production · detailed designs.

## 8. International Customer Example

Customer: USA. Question: *"Can I buy this UK-style oak newel?"*

Nex should NOT immediately connect. First: *"I can help prepare the exact details so the supplier understands what you need. Are you looking for a complete staircase, replacement parts, or custom-made newels?"*

Then collect dimensions · material · quantity · location.

## 9. Why This Creates Better Supplier Relationships

Suppliers prefer **prepared customers**. A good enquiry contains:

- ✅ clear requirements
- ✅ realistic expectations
- ✅ correct terminology
- ✅ project information

This reduces wasted emails · incorrect quotations · misunderstandings.
