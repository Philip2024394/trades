# NEX Premium Mono-String Open-Riser Staircase with Cable Balustrade

*This spec is classified as a modern open-riser mono-string staircase with a contemporary steel and timber cable balustrade system. Although it resembles a floating staircase, the treads are structurally supported by a central steel spine string.*

*Three image variants represent this design language (see `image_variants` in frontmatter). One authored specification · not three separate designs.*

## Staircase Classification

- Straight flight staircase.
- Modern contemporary design.
- Residential feature staircase.
- Open riser staircase.
- Mono-string (single central spine beam) construction.
- Feature staircase for open-plan living.
- Premium architectural finish.

## Stair Structure

- Single central steel spine string supports all treads.
- Steel spine is fabricated from welded box-section steel.
- Powder-coated matte black finish.
- Structural steel provides the primary load-bearing support.
- Treads are cantilevered from the central spine on both sides.
- No side strings.

## Stair Layout

- Straight flight.
- Constant pitch.
- Uniform tread spacing.
- Open risers throughout.
- Quarter-turn landing at the upper floor (only partially visible).
- Feature semicircular starting step.

## Starting Step

Unlike a standard staircase, this design begins with:

- Large semicircular feature step.
- Curved front edge.
- Full oak construction.
- Acts as a welcoming architectural feature.
- Wider than the remaining staircase.

## Treads

- Solid European oak.
- Thick floating appearance (approximately 50–60 mm).
- Square-edge profile.
- Closed front edge.
- Uniform tread depth.
- Satin clear lacquer finish.
- No visible brackets or fixings.
- Fixed directly to the central steel spine.

## Risers

- Completely open.
- No timber risers.
- Light passes through the staircase.
- Creates a floating visual effect.
- Modern minimalist appearance.

## Spine String

- Single fabricated steel box section.
- Matte black powder-coated finish.
- Positioned centrally beneath the treads.
- Concealed fixing system.
- Continuous from bottom to landing.

## Balustrade System

Modern cable balustrade.

**Components include:**

- Square steel balustrade posts.
- Matte black powder-coated finish.
- Evenly spaced structural posts.
- Stainless steel horizontal tension cables.
- Multiple parallel cable runs.
- Clean minimalist appearance.

The cable system provides safety while maintaining maximum visibility through the staircase.

## Handrail

- Solid oak handrail.
- Continuous along the full staircase.
- Rectangular profile with eased corners.
- Natural oak finish matching the treads.
- Fixed to the steel balustrade posts.
- Continues onto the upper landing.

## Newel Posts

Contemporary square steel newel posts.

**Features:**

- Fabricated steel construction.
- Powder-coated black.
- Large square profile.
- Timber cap in matching oak.
- Structural fixing into floor.
- Matching upper landing post.

## Feature Newel Post

**Characteristics:**

- Hollow rectangular steel section.
- Integrated warm white LED lighting.
- Vertical recessed opening.
- Decorative planter built into the recess.
- Acts as both structural support and architectural focal point.

- **Handrail:** Continuous solid oak.
- **Newels:** Square steel posts with oak caps.

## Overall Description

This spec describes a premium modern mono-string staircase where a single black powder-coated steel spine supports thick floating solid oak treads with open risers. The balustrade consists of square steel posts, horizontal stainless steel tension cables, and a continuous oak handrail. A distinctive semicircular oak starting step leads to a feature illuminated steel newel post with an integrated planter, creating a strong architectural focal point. Hidden tread lighting and recessed wall lights enhance the floating appearance, making this an ideal example of a high-end contemporary residential staircase.
