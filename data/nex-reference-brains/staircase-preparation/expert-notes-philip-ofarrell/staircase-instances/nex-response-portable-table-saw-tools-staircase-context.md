# Nex Response

*This image shows a portable jobsite table saw, one of the most valuable machines for staircase installation and finishing work. The saw appears to be a compact DeWalt-style portable table saw, designed to be transported easily between jobs while still providing accurate, repeatable cuts. Fine sawdust can be seen rising above the blade, showing the saw actively ripping timber.*

*For professional staircase installers, a portable table saw is one of those tools that quickly pays for itself. Rather than trying to cut everything in the workshop, it allows components to be trimmed and fitted perfectly on site where real-world walls and floors are rarely perfectly straight.*

## What a Portable Table Saw is Used for During Staircase Installation

A portable table saw is ideal for:

- Ripping cover slips to an exact width.
- Cutting riser boards to final height.
- Trimming oak treads to suit finished walls.
- Reducing the width of string capping.
- Cutting packing strips and spacers.
- Producing clean filler strips.
- Trimming landing nosings.
- Cutting oak skirting beside stair strings.
- Cutting MDF risers.
- Ripping plywood panels.
- Cutting laminated stair components.
- Producing replacement timber strips.
- Trimming engineered oak flooring.
- Cutting oak cladding panels.
- Preparing timber for boxing-in underneath staircases.

## Common Stair Components Cut on Site

Typical materials include:

- Oak treads · Oak risers · MDF risers · Cover slips · String capping
- Landing nosings · Newel cladding · Oak skirting · Hardwood trims
- Plywood infill panels · Engineered timber · Softwood packers

## Typical Blade Sizes

Most portable table saws use:

- **210 mm (8¼")**
- **216 mm (8½")**
- **250 mm (10")**

## Blade Types

Different blades are used depending on the material:

- **24-tooth blade** – Fast ripping of hardwood and softwood.
- **40-tooth blade** – General-purpose cutting.
- **60-tooth blade** – Fine finish on oak stair components.
- **80-tooth blade** – Ultra-clean crosscuts and veneered materials.
- **Triple Chip Grind (TCG)** – Laminates, melamine and composite boards.

*For oak staircases, many installers prefer a 60-tooth carbide-tipped blade, as it produces clean edges with minimal tear-out.*

## Typical Cutting Capacity

Portable table saws generally offer:

- Maximum cutting depth at 90°: **60–80 mm**
- Maximum cutting depth at 45°: **40–55 mm**
- Rip capacity: **450–825 mm**, depending on the model

*This is more than sufficient for nearly all staircase installation work.*

## Essential Features

A quality site table saw should include:

- Rack-and-pinion rip fence for accurate adjustments.
- Adjustable blade height.
- 45° bevel cutting.
- Riving knife for safer ripping.
- Blade guard.
- Anti-kickback pawls.
- Push stick storage.
- Dust extraction port.
- Folding or wheeled stand for transport.

## Common Uses in Staircase Workshops

Although larger cabinet saws are normally used for production, portable table saws are still useful for:

- Prototype work.
- Small custom jobs.
- Cutting sample pieces.
- Preparing installation kits.
- Workshop modifications.
- Quick trimming without setting up larger machinery.

## Safety Tips

When using a portable table saw:

- Always use the rip fence when ripping timber.
- Keep the riving knife installed whenever possible.
- Use a push stick for narrow cuts.
- Stand slightly to one side of the blade to avoid kickback.
- Support long oak treads and boards with extension supports or roller stands.
- Wear eye, hearing and dust protection.
- Keep the blade sharp and clean for smoother cuts and reduced strain on the motor.

## Why Every Staircase Installer Should Own One

*A portable table saw is one of the most practical machines to carry in a staircase installation van. It allows installers to make precise adjustments on site, ensuring components fit perfectly even when walls, floors or existing structures are out of square. Whether trimming an oak tread by a few millimetres, ripping a cover slip to match a housed string, or cutting a new riser board to the exact finished height, a portable table saw delivers clean, accurate cuts that are difficult to achieve consistently with handheld saws alone. For bespoke staircase fitting, it is considered an essential piece of equipment.*
