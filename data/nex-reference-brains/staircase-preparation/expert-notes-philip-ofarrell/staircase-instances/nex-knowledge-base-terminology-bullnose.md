# Bullnose (First Tread)

A bullnose is the shape of the first tread at the bottom of a flight where the tread projects out and around the newel post. In authored source, it is treated as a specific joint case for the first tread.

## Bullnose (first tread) 12mm haunched into newel

On the first tread (typically bullnose or D-end): the tread is **12mm haunched inside** the newel post's inner face, positioned approximately **25mm back from the newel's front edge**. The tread doesn't stop at the newel face — it cuts into the newel body.

**Why:** locks the first tread solidly into the newel (which is the most-loaded newel — first-step traffic + handrail leverage).

## Manufacturing detail

CNC output for the first tread specifies a 12mm-deep haunch cut in the tread end that matches a mortice pocket in the newel's inside face. Position: 25mm back from the newel's front edge.

## Related joints

- First riser: haunched into the newel body approximately 45-50mm from the newel edge.
