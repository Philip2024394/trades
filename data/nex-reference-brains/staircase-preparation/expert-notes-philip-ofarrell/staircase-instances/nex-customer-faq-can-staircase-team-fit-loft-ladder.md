## Customer Question

> ***Can the staircase installation team fit my loft ladder while they're on site?***

*Possibly, but you shouldn't assume it's part of the staircase installation.*

*Many staircase installation teams are highly skilled joiners capable of fitting a loft ladder. However, whether they can do so depends on the staircase company's services, the installation programme, and the agreement made before work begins.*

## Who Installs the Staircase?

There are generally two approaches used by staircase manufacturers:

### In-House Installation Teams

*Many manufacturers employ their own installation crews who specialise in fitting the company's staircases. These installers are trained to work to the manufacturer's standards and represent the company directly.*

### Approved Subcontract Installers

*Some manufacturers use experienced subcontract staircase fitters who regularly install their products. These installers are selected because they have a proven track record, understand the manufacturer's systems and are trusted to maintain the company's quality standards.*

*Using subcontract installers is common throughout the construction industry and does not necessarily indicate lower quality. What matters is that they are experienced and approved by the staircase manufacturer.*

## Should You Ask?

**Yes.**

Before assuming additional work can be carried out, ask the staircase manufacturer:

- Is the installation completed by your own employees or approved subcontractors?
- Do you offer loft ladder installation as an additional service?
- Can it be quoted and scheduled alongside the staircase installation?
- Will fitting a loft ladder affect the installation programme or warranty?

*Having this conversation before installation day avoids misunderstandings and ensures everyone knows what work has been agreed.*

## Why a Specialist Loft Ladder Company Is Often the Best Choice

Although many joiners have the skills to fit a loft ladder, specialist loft ladder companies usually provide the most comprehensive service because they:

- Supply products designed for the application.
- Recommend the correct ladder based on ceiling height, opening size and usage.
- Assess structural requirements before installation.
- Make any necessary alterations to the loft hatch or surrounding framework.
- Ensure the ladder operates safely and smoothly.
- Understand insulation, draught-proofing and loft access regulations.
- Provide product-specific installation experience and after-sales support.

*A company that both supplies and installs loft ladders is often best placed to ensure the product is fitted correctly and performs as intended.*

## NEX Summary

*A staircase installation team may be able to fit a loft ladder, but this is not usually included as part of a standard staircase installation unless agreed in advance. Some staircase manufacturers use in-house installers, while others use approved subcontract staircase fitters with proven experience and quality standards. It's always advisable to ask the staircase manufacturer whether loft ladder installation is a service they provide and who will be carrying out the work on site. For the best results, many homeowners choose a specialist loft ladder company that both supplies and installs the product, as they have the dedicated knowledge and experience to select the right ladder, make any necessary structural adjustments and ensure it is installed safely and correctly.*
