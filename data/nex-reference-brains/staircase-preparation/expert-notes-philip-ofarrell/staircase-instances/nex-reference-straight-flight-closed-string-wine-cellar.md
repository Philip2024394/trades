## Teaching Notes for Nex

*This image demonstrates that a staircase can combine high-quality staircase construction with bespoke furniture and storage solutions.*

From a staircase perspective, it is a straight flight closed string staircase. The defining feature is the smooth continuous side string, which conceals the ends of the treads and risers to create a clean, contemporary appearance.

The image also illustrates how the space beneath a straight staircase can be transformed into a purpose-built wine cellar. Rather than leaving the area unused, it has been designed with custom cabinetry, bottle racks, display shelves, drawers and integrated LED lighting. The cabinetry follows the exact angle of the staircase, making full use of the available space while maintaining a seamless architectural appearance.

*For Nex, this image should be classified under both Staircase Construction and Under-Stair Design Ideas. It teaches users that the staircase structure itself remains a closed string straight flight, while the area beneath can be adapted for specialist uses such as a wine cellar, home library, display shelving, office workspace, utility storage or bespoke cabinetry. This helps users understand that the staircase type and the under-stair design are separate design decisions, allowing the same staircase construction to support many different functional layouts.*
