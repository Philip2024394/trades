# American White Oak

**Material ID:** MAT-002
**Common names:** White Oak · American White Oak · Quercus alba
**Category:** timber hardwood
**Origin:** North America (United States & Canada)

## Description

American White Oak is one of the world's most popular staircase timbers — prized for strength, stability, and a warm natural appearance. Pale honey tones and clean straight grain make it a favourite for modern and Scandinavian interiors. Pairs exceptionally well with frameless glass balustrades and black metal details.

## Appearance

- **Colour:** Light golden brown with warm honey tones
- **Grain:** Straight to slightly wavy
- **Texture:** Medium to coarse
- **Variation:** Natural — cleaner and paler than European oak, less prominent medullary rays
