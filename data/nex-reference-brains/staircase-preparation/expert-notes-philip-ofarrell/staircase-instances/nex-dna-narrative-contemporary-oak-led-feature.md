# NEX Premium Staircase Description

**Product Name:** **NEX Premium – Contemporary Straight Flight Oak Staircase with LED Feature Lighting**

## Staircase Type

- Straight Flight Staircase (Single Flight)
- Closed String Staircase
- Closed Riser Design
- Contemporary / Premium Residential Staircase [source: dabstairs.com][1]

## Design Style

- Modern minimalist
- Premium architectural finish
- Clean linear design
- Integrated feature lighting
- Luxury timber and stainless steel combination

## Materials

- American Oak (treads, risers, strings, handrails and newel post)
- Brushed Stainless Steel round balusters (spindles)
- Black decorative inset panel to the front newel post with brushed stainless steel surround
- Warm white recessed LED lighting beneath the wall handrail
- Recessed LED strip lighting integrated into each stair nosing
- Black feature plinth/base trim at the bottom of the staircase

## Features

- Premium solid oak construction
- Full closed risers for a seamless appearance
- Rounded starting step with curved bullnose corner
- Contemporary square newel post with decorative black feature insert
- Continuous oak handrail
- Slim brushed stainless steel balusters
- Hidden LED stair tread illumination
- LED wall-mounted handrail lighting creating a floating light effect
- Precision joinery with concealed fixings
- Luxury architectural finish

## Overall Look

*A high-end contemporary straight flight staircase designed for luxury homes. The combination of warm American oak, brushed stainless steel balusters, integrated LED lighting, and a statement feature newel post creates a premium modern aesthetic while maintaining a timeless timber appearance. The staircase emphasizes clean lines, high-quality craftsmanship, and subtle architectural lighting for both safety and visual impact.* [source: dabstairs.com][1]
