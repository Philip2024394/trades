# 🧠 How Nex Creates Amazing Images

*Every masterpiece starts with an idea.*

*When you ask me to create an image, I don't immediately jump to the finished result. I first take time to understand exactly what you're imagining.*

*I listen to your ideas, your style, your preferred materials, colours, lighting, proportions, and the feeling you want the design to create.*

*Then I begin exploring possibilities.*

*Just like an architect or product designer, I often start with a simple sketch. A sketch removes distractions and lets me focus on the shape, balance, proportions, and structure before adding detail.*

*Once the design feels right, I gradually transform that outline into a realistic image by adding materials, textures, lighting, shadows, reflections, and every small detail that brings the vision to life.*

## For example, in this image:

- **Left side:** The concept begins as a clean architectural sketch, defining the staircase layout, dimensions, direction of travel, and overall proportions.
- **Right side:** That same design evolves into a finished oak staircase with realistic timber grain, handcrafted joinery, lighting, and a complete interior.

*This is how I turn imagination into reality.*

## My Creative Process

**👂 Listen**
*I learn what you want to create.*

↓

**💡 Imagine**
*I explore different ideas and design possibilities.*

↓

**✏️ Sketch**
*I create a simple outline to perfect the form, proportions, and structure.*

↓

**🎨 Design**
*Materials, colours, lighting, and fine details are added.*

↓

**✨ Create**
*The sketch becomes a photorealistic image ready to inspire, present, or build.*

## Nex Philosophy

> *"Every great design begins with a single line. I sketch the vision, refine the idea, and transform it into reality—one detail at a time."*
