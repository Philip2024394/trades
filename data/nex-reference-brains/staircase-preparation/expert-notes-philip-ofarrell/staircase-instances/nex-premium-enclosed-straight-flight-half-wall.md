# NEX Premium · Enclosed Straight Flight with Half Wall (7 Levels Applied to Image)

*This specification demonstrates the 7 Levels of Understanding applied to a single reference image. It is not primarily a description of a staircase — it is a template for how NEX should THINK when looking at a staircase image.*

## WHAT AM I LOOKING AT?

This is an enclosed straight flight timber staircase installed between two structural walls. The staircase rises continuously in one direction without any turns or landings.

The structural strings are concealed from view beneath and beside the treads giving the appearance that the staircase is emerging directly from the surrounding walls.

The staircase has been designed to appear visually simple, although the structural construction remains hidden.

## WHAT CAN I SEE?

- Straight flight staircase
- Enclosed between two walls
- Oak finished treads and risers
- Concealed string construction
- Left side half wall with integrated oak capping acting as the handrail
- Right side wall mounted oak handrail
- Stainless steel brushed feature plates fitted to the risers
- No visible newel posts
- No visible balustrades
- Modern minimalist design
- Large generous tread proportions
- Contemporary architectural appearance

## WHAT CAN I NOT SEE?

*NEX must understand that not all knowledge is visible within an image. She must NEVER invent information. She must instead say:*

- The supporting strings are not visible
- The fixing method cannot be confirmed from the image
- The staircase may be fully manufactured in timber or may utilise concealed structural supports
- The underside construction is not visible
- The material thickness of the treads cannot be confirmed
- The installation method cannot be confirmed
- Building regulation compliance cannot be determined from the image alone

**Truthfulness pattern (Rule A applied to images):**

- Never: *"This staircase uses two concealed oak strings."*
- Always: *"The staircase appears to utilise concealed structural support, however the supporting construction cannot be confirmed from the visible image."*

## HOW DOES IT WORK?

The staircase functions as a traditional straight flight staircase despite appearing minimalist.

The structural loads are transferred through the concealed supporting construction beneath the treads into the surrounding building structure.

Although no strings are visible, the staircase will still require suitable structural support.

The handrails provide user support during ascent and descent while maintaining the minimalist architectural appearance.

## WHY WAS IT DESIGNED THIS WAY?

A designer would typically choose this style to:

- Maximise the feeling of space
- Create clean architectural lines
- Remove visual clutter
- Produce a contemporary appearance
- Conceal the traditional staircase construction
- Create a luxury minimalist finish
- Allow the timber to become the primary visual feature

## HOW IS IT MANUFACTURED?

NEX must understand the journey, not just remember answers.

### Before installation

Timber → Machining → Strings manufactured → Treads manufactured → Risers manufactured → Dry assembly → Finished → Packed → Delivered

### After delivery

Floor preparation → String installation → Staircase assembly → Structural fixing → Handrail installation → Feature inserts fitted → Decoration → Final inspection
