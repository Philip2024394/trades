# European Oak

**Material ID:** MAT-001
**Common names:** Oak · European Oak · English Oak · Quercus robur · Quercus petraea
**Category:** timber hardwood
**Origin:** Europe (UK, France, Germany, Poland)

## Description

European Oak is the UK default premium staircase timber — warm, characterful, wildly versatile. Works in a Georgian townhouse or a modern new-build without fighting either. Ages beautifully; a well-made European oak staircase reads as heirloom joinery after 30 years. Pairs equally with turned traditional detail or slim contemporary glass.

## Appearance

- **Colour:** Warm golden-brown with prominent medullary rays
- **Grain:** Straight, sometimes wavy or figured on quarter-sawn boards
- **Texture:** Medium to coarse, open pores
- **Variation:** Notable — every board slightly different; darker heartwood streaks common

## Physical properties

- **Durability rating:** 5/5
- **Hardness (Janka):** 1290 lbf / 5740 N
- **Density:** 750 kg/m³
- **Machining rating:** 4/5
- **Cost band:** £££
