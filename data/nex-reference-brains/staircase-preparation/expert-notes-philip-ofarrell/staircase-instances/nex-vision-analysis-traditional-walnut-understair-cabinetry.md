- Console table

- Warm recessed ceiling lighting
- Ambient table lamp

## Overall Quality

*This is a premium bespoke staircase, combining traditional craftsmanship with practical storage. Every visible timber component—from the staircase to the cabinetry and surrounding doors—is colour matched in walnut, creating a cohesive, elegant interior. The under-stair cabinets are fully integrated into the stair structure, making the storage appear as a natural extension of the staircase rather than an addition.*
