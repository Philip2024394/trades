## Customer Question

> ***Can my site carpenter install my new staircase instead of the staircase company's installers?***

*In many cases, yes. Experienced site carpenters install staircases regularly and are often more than capable of fitting a staircase correctly.*

*However, before deciding who should carry out the installation, it's important to understand how this may affect responsibility, support and warranty.*

## Why Many Customers Choose the Manufacturer's Installation Service

Many staircase companies offer a complete service that includes:

- Initial site survey.
- Accurate site measurements.
- Design and technical drawings.
- Manufacture.
- Delivery.
- Professional installation.
- Final inspection and adjustments.

*Using one company from start to finish means there is a single point of responsibility throughout the project.*

*If any issues arise during installation, the company that measured, manufactured and fitted the staircase is generally in the best position to resolve them.*

## Can a Site Carpenter Install It?

**Absolutely.**

A competent site carpenter will often have the skills and experience to install:

- Straight staircases.
- Quarter-turn staircases.
- Half-turn staircases.
- Cut-string staircases.
- Closed-string staircases.
- Timber balustrades.
- Handrails and newel posts.

*On many building sites, staircases are successfully installed by site carpenters every day.*

## Things to Consider

If you choose not to use the staircase manufacturer's installation service, you should ask:

- Does the manufacturer provide installation guidance?
- Will technical support still be available if required?
- Does self-installation or third-party installation affect the warranty?
- Who is responsible if the staircase is damaged during fitting?
- Who pays if alterations are required because of incorrect installation?

*Some manufacturers continue to support customers regardless of who installs the staircase, while others may limit their responsibility to the supplied product only. This isn't universal, so it's important to check the company's terms before making a decision.*

## When the Manufacturer's Installers Can Be an Advantage

Manufacturer-approved installers are usually familiar with:

- The company's construction methods.
- Assembly sequence.
- Hidden fixings.
- Product-specific details.
- Recommended installation techniques.
- Final adjustments and finishing.

*Because they work with the same staircase designs regularly, they can often identify and resolve issues more quickly.*

## NEX Summary

*Many experienced site carpenters have the skills to install a bespoke staircase successfully, and on many projects this is a practical option. However, when ordering a new staircase, it's worth considering a manufacturer that offers a complete service from site survey and design through to manufacture and installation. Using the same company throughout the project provides a single point of responsibility and can simplify communication if adjustments are needed. If you choose to use your own carpenter, ask the staircase manufacturer how this affects technical support, warranty and after-sales service. Understanding these arrangements before installation begins can help avoid misunderstandings and ensure everyone knows who is responsible should any issues arise.*
