# NEX Recognition Example — Whole-Staircase (First-Person Voice)

*This is a high-end traditional staircase with premium walnut timber throughout. Here's what I can identify:*

## Materials

- **Main staircase:** Solid American Black Walnut (or a very similar dark walnut finish).
- **Handrail:** Solid walnut with a hand-polished satin finish.
- **Newel posts:** Turned solid walnut.
- **Balusters (spindles):** Traditional turned walnut timber.
- **Strings:** Curved solid walnut stringers with a continuous flowing profile.
- **Treads:** Solid walnut hardwood.
- **Risers:** Walnut stained to match the treads.
- **Flooring:** Walnut herringbone parquet.

## Staircase Style

- Grand curved staircase with a sweeping lower curve.
- Traditional craftsmanship with modern proportions.
- Continuous curved handrail formed using laminated walnut.
- Closed string construction.
- Turned Victorian/Edwardian style balusters.

## Finish

- Satin polyurethane or hardwax oil.
- Medium-dark walnut tone.
- Smooth furniture-quality finish.

## Carpet

- Deep burgundy/red stair runner.
- Gold woven borders on each side.
- Professionally fitted with concealed fixing.

## Lighting

- Large crystal chandelier suspended through the stairwell.
- Warm white recessed ceiling spotlights.
- Small LED wall lights along the staircase.

## Interior

- Neutral warm painted walls.
- White ceiling with decorative cornice.
- Black interior doors.
- Walnut console table.
- Large circular mirror.
- Luxury residential entrance hall.

## Estimated Dimensions

- Stair width: **1200–1400 mm**
- Handrail height: **900 mm**
- Newel posts: **120–150 mm diameter**
- Treads: **35–40 mm thick**
- Curved lower radius approximately **1800–2200 mm**

## Overall Impression

*The staircase is a bespoke, luxury walnut feature staircase. The craftsmanship is of a very high standard, with a continuous curved handrail, flowing closed strings, and traditional turned balusters. The rich walnut timber combined with the red carpet runner creates a classic, elegant appearance typical of high-end custom homes.*
