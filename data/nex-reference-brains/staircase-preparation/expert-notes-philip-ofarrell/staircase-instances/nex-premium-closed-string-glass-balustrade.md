- Toughened laminated safety glass.
- Full-height panels.

- Black powder-coated steel glass clamps.
- Vertical steel support posts.
- Mechanical clamp fixing system.
- Even spacing between posts.
- Designed to securely retain the glass panels.

- Continuous from the bottom newel to the upper landing.
- Rectangular profile with eased edges.

- Black cap.

## Lighting

Integrated architectural LED lighting throughout.

### Tread Lighting

- Recessed LED lights installed into the side of each riser.
- Warm white illumination.
- Highlights every individual step.

### String Lighting

- Continuous concealed LED strip beneath the handrail along the glass balustrade.
- Produces an even wash of light along the staircase.

### Wall Lighting

- Small recessed square LED wall lights installed adjacent to the staircase.
- Consistent spacing.
- Complements the tread lighting.

## Overall Description

This spec describes a premium straight-flight closed-string timber staircase that combines natural oak treads, white painted risers and strings, and a frameless structural glass balustrade. The glass is supported by black powder-coated steel posts and clamps, with a continuous solid oak handrail running the full length of the staircase and onto the landing. Large square oak newel posts with black caps provide structural support at the bottom and top of the flight. Integrated warm white LED tread lights, a concealed LED strip beneath the handrail, and recessed wall lights create a sophisticated, well-lit staircase that balances traditional joinery with modern architectural detailing.
