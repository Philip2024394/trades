# Newel Posts · Phase 18.3.4 · Glass Newel Systems · Dimensions, Sizes, Heights and Structural Details

## 1. Glass Newel Post Dimensions

Unlike timber newels, glass staircase posts are often specified by structural requirement · glass thickness · handrail design · visual style · fixing method.

**There is no single universal size.** The correct dimension depends on the system.

## 2. Stainless Steel Glass Newel Sizes

### Square Stainless Newels

Typical profiles:
- 40mm × 40mm
- 50mm × 50mm
- 60mm × 60mm

Used for modern residential staircases · commercial interiors · glass clamp systems.

### Round Stainless Newels

Typical diameters:
- 42.4mm
- 48.3mm
- 50mm

Used for softer modern appearance · curved staircases · marine-style designs.

### Heavy-Duty Architectural Posts

Typical:
- 60mm × 60mm
- 80mm × 80mm

Used for large glass panels · commercial applications · feature staircases.

## 3. Timber Newel With Glass Sizes

A very common premium residential combination.

### Square Oak Newel

**Standard: 75mm × 75mm** — lighter staircase designs · smaller spaces.

**Premium Residential: 90mm × 90mm** — oak staircases · glass balustrades · contemporary designs.

**Statement Luxury: 120mm × 120mm+** — grand entrances · large hallways · traditional luxury staircases.

## 4. Newel Height Above Floor

The visible height depends on handrail height requirement · staircase design · building regulations · customer preference.

**Bottom Newel** — usually extends above the first tread · supports handrail start · creates the staircase entrance point. Common visible height: approximately **900mm–1100mm above finished floor level**.

**Landing Newel** — usually **1000mm–1100mm above finished floor level**. Used to terminate the handrail · secure glass panels · complete balustrade protection.

## 5. Glass Height

Glass panels normally follow the balustrade requirement. Common residential appearance: approximately **900mm–1100mm high**.

The final height depends on local regulations · staircase location · residential/commercial use.

## 6. Glass Thickness Selection

**10mm Toughened Glass** — smaller panels · lighter applications.

**12mm Toughened Glass** — common premium residential choice. Glass balustrades · timber/glass combinations.

**13.5mm Laminated Glass** — premium safety option. Luxury staircases · higher specification projects.

**15mm–21.5mm Laminated Glass** — large panels · commercial projects · demanding architectural applications.

## 7. Fixing Glass to Newels · Component Chain

The fixing system is critical. A beautiful glass staircase can fail if clamps are undersized · fixings are incorrect · the structure behind is weak.

**Glass Clamp System Components:**

1. Newel post
2. Clamp bracket
3. Rubber gasket
4. Glass panel
5. Mechanical fixing

The gasket protects glass edges · against metal contact · vibration damage.

## 8. Typical Clamp Positions

A professional installation considers:

**Bottom Clamp Position** — controls glass stability · alignment · visual appearance.

**Intermediate Clamp Positions** — used depending on glass height · panel width · loading requirements.

**Top Connection** — may include handrail fixing · glass channel · stainless cap system.

## 9. Handrail Connections in Glass Systems

**Timber Handrail** — popular combinations: oak · walnut · painted timber. Creates warm touch point · luxury appearance.

**Stainless Handrail** — used for modern interiors · commercial buildings. Durable · minimal maintenance.

**Glass Supported Handrail** — advanced system: handrail visually floats · glass carries the visual structure. Requires accurate engineering · specialist fittings.

## 10. Structural Considerations

A glass staircase is not only decorative. The system must resist people leaning on the balustrade · movement loads · handrail forces · vibration · daily use.

**Newel Base Fixing** can be bolted into steel · fixed into structural timber · chemically anchored into concrete · connected to stair structure.

**Floor Strength** — the fixing point must transfer loads into concrete slab · structural timber · steel framework. Not just plasterboard · decorative flooring · weak coverings.

**Wall Conditions** — for wall-supported systems: wall construction · fixing depth · hidden steel support if required.

## 11. Luxury Design Rule

The best glass staircase designs balance:

- **Structure** — the staircase must feel solid
- **Transparency** — the glass should create openness
- **Materials** — the newel, handrail and staircase materials should work together

Example · **Premium modern home**: oak treads + oak 90×90mm newels + 12mm clear glass + brushed stainless clamps + oak handrail. Result: warm + modern + architectural.
