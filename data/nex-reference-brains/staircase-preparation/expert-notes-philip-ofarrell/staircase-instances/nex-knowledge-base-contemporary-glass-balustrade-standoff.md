### Glass Safety

*The balustrade should be manufactured using toughened safety glass, laminated safety glass, or a toughened laminated safety glass assembly, depending on the design requirements and applicable building regulations.*

**Key Characteristics**

- Designed to withstand significant impact loads.
- If toughened glass breaks, it fractures into small blunt fragments rather than dangerous sharp shards.
- Laminated glass contains an interlayer that helps retain broken pieces if the glass is damaged, maintaining the barrier until replacement.
- Highly resistant to everyday impacts encountered in residential use.
- Suitable for use as a protective guarding system when correctly specified and installed.

> **NEX Note:** *The term "shatterproof" should be avoided in technical documentation. No glass is completely unbreakable. The correct terminology is toughened safety glass, laminated safety glass, or toughened laminated safety glass, which are engineered to break in a controlled and significantly safer manner than ordinary glass.*
