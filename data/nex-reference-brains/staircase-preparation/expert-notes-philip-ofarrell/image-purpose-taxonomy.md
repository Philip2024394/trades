# Image Purpose Taxonomy

## Why this taxonomy exists

**Philip 2026-07-31 (verbatim):**

> *"It is important because it separates: 'Can NEX show this?' from 'Can NEX learn from this?'"*

The staircase-brain-membership-criteria answers whether an image should CONTRIBUTE TO STAIRCASE UNDERSTANDING (learning). This taxonomy answers a different question: WHAT IS THIS IMAGE FOR? (purpose).

The two questions are structurally independent. An image may be:

- **Highly valuable for display** (a beautiful premium staircase render on a marketing page) BUT
- **Zero-value for training** (the render teaches customer-desire, not staircase construction)

The reverse is also true:

- **Weak visual quality** (a scratched photograph of a workshop joint detail) BUT
- **Highly valuable for training** (the joint teaches an authentic construction relationship)

The failure mode this taxonomy prevents: silently admitting marketing-asset images into the recognition-training pathway because they LOOK premium.

---

## The five categories (Philip 2026-07-31 · Decision 5)

### 1 · `recognition_training`

**Purpose:** contributes to what NEX can identify · describe · reason about visually.

**Examples of qualifying images:**

- Component close-ups showing construction relationships
- Junction detail photographs (tread-to-string · handrail-to-newel · baluster-to-tread)
- Complete-staircase reference photographs where the whole system is coherently visible
- Comparison images demonstrating a specific design distinction (cut-string vs closed-string)

**Enters staircase_brain intelligence pathway:** YES (subject to 5-gate criteria + ADR-0033 quality threshold)

### 2 · `reference_only`

**Purpose:** staircase-adjacent or staircase-related content that does not train recognition.

**Examples of qualifying images:**

- Workshop context photographs (equipment · storage · organization)
- Manufacturing process moments (planing · sanding · assembly stages)
- Installation-scene photographs (site fitting · adjustment moments)
- Historical or heritage staircase photographs archived for reference

**Enters staircase_brain intelligence pathway:** NO (may be reachable for reference lookups · but does not train recognition · does not contribute to relationship graph)

### 3 · `customer_inspiration`

**Purpose:** to help customers imagine what is possible · to spark design conversations.

**Examples of qualifying images:**

- Premium showcase photographs of finished staircases in styled interiors
- Architectural photographs emphasizing atmosphere · lighting · space
- Portfolio hero images from completed projects
- Aspirational imagery for design discovery

**Enters staircase_brain intelligence pathway:** NO (high aesthetic value · low recognition-training value · exactly the failure mode the governing discipline sentence protects against)

### 4 · `article_illustration`

**Purpose:** to illustrate a written article · guide · or educational piece.

**Examples of qualifying images:**

- Images accompanying Philip O'Farrell's authored articles (workshop organisation · installation topics · manufacturing walkthroughs)
- Header images on knowledge-base entries
- Contextual imagery for how-to content

**Enters staircase_brain intelligence pathway:** NO (the article carries the knowledge · the image carries visual accompaniment · these are different evidence roles)

### 5 · `marketing_asset`

**Purpose:** to support brand · marketing · business surfaces.

**Examples of qualifying images:**

- Hero banners (news · contact · about · social media pages)
- Category thumbnails
- Advertising imagery
- Business-page decoration

**Enters staircase_brain intelligence pathway:** NO (subject_domain=staircase tagging on these images is over-inclusive · they belong to a marketing-asset library · not a knowledge brain)

## Application procedure

For each image being tagged:

1. **Ask the guiding-separation question first:** *"Is this image PRIMARILY for showing (display) · for learning (training) · or does it require verification (evidence check)?"* (Round 1 Check 1 refinement · verify became a third question)
2. **Assign one primary `image_purpose` from the five categories.** (An image has ONE primary purpose · secondary purposes may exist but are noted separately.)
3. **If `image_purpose == recognition_training`:** proceed to the 5-gate staircase-brain-membership criteria (that document decides brain admission).
4. **If `image_purpose ∈ {reference_only · customer_inspiration · article_illustration · marketing_asset}`:** the image does NOT enter the staircase_brain intelligence pathway · but remains in the manifest under its correct purpose classification.

**No image is deleted based on this taxonomy.** All images remain in the manifest. Only the intelligence-admission pathway is gated.

## The five-level evidence hierarchy (Philip 2026-07-31 · Round 2 refinement · authoritative)

**Philip verbatim:** *"I don't think we're building an image taxonomy any more, we're beginning to discover the NEX epistemology (how NEX knows something is true)."*

**The five evidence levels · in order:**

### LEVEL 1 · VISUALLY VERIFIED

*"I can physically see this."*

### LEVEL 2 · AUTHOR PROVIDED KNOWLEDGE

*"The author has supplied this knowledge."*

### LEVEL 3 · HIGH CONFIDENCE INFERENCE

*"I cannot physically see this component but the relationship is strongly supported by the available evidence."*

**Philip's critical insight:** *"Inference is neither Known nor Unknown. It lives between the two."*

### LEVEL 4 · UNKNOWN

*"The available evidence is insufficient."*

### LEVEL 5 · REQUEST MORE EVIDENCE

*"Additional evidence is required."*

**Worked example (Philip verbatim):**

```
VISUALLY VERIFIED         → open risers · timber treads · glass balustrade
AUTHOR PROVIDED           → concealed metal support plates · bevelled glass edges
HIGH CONFIDENCE INFERENCE → staircase designed to maximise openness · glass minimises visual obstruction · staircase acts as architectural feature
UNKNOWN                   → timber species · regulations · dimensions
REQUEST MORE EVIDENCE     → another image · dimensions · manufacturing specifications
```

**All five levels are equally valid states of knowledge. None is a failure state.**

## Qualitative labels over numeric confidence scores (Philip 2026-07-31 · Round 2 discipline)

**Philip verbatim:** *"I don't believe NEX should have a confidence score any more. I actually prefer VISUALLY VERIFIED / AUTHOR PROVIDED / HIGH CONFIDENCE INFERENCE / PARTIALLY VERIFIED / UNKNOWN / INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE."*

**Rationale (verbatim):** *"Users don't actually care whether NEX is 97% confident or 98% confident. They care about: How do you know this?"*

**BAD:** *"98% confident."*
**GOOD:** *"VISUALLY VERIFIED."*
**BETTER:** *"VISUALLY VERIFIED — I can physically see: open risers · glass balustrade · timber treads."*

**We're teaching NEX to explain itself.**

**Effect on Vision Scan Master Score sections:** future Vision Scan model outputs may replace numeric-percentage master scores with qualitative labels (EXCEPTIONAL / HIGH / MODERATE / LOW plus per-dimension evidence-tier labels). Existing Vision Scans #01, #02, #03 retain their authored numeric scores as historical evidence of the transition · but the qualitative discipline governs new authoring going forward.

## The Unknown governance sentence (Philip 2026-07-31 · Round 1 Check 4 · pre-round-2 refinement)

> **"Unknown is an acceptable and preferred answer when sufficient evidence is unavailable."**

**Philip verbatim:** *"I am finding this sentence appearing repeatedly throughout the project. NEX should never be punished for saying 'I don't know' or 'I require more evidence.'"*

**Governing examples (Philip verbatim):**

**GOOD:**

> This appears to be dark timber.
> UNKNOWN: species unknown · dimensions unknown · manufacturer unknown

**BAD:**

> This is a walnut staircase manufactured in Europe.

**GOOD:**

> Image contains: person carrying timber · workshop environment.
> NO staircase evidence detected.

**BAD:**

> Article title mentions staircases therefore staircase intelligence.

**Applied within this taxonomy:** any image whose purpose cannot be honestly determined from available metadata should be marked with `image_purpose: unknown` · not force-fit into one of the five known purposes. Unknown is a valid classification outcome · not a failure state.

## The bigger discipline this taxonomy protects

**Philip 2026-07-31 (verbatim closing observation on Batch #01):**

> *"A staircase image is not automatically staircase knowledge."*

This taxonomy operationalises that observation. Being tagged `subject_domain=staircase` says the image is ABOUT staircases. Being assigned `image_purpose=recognition_training` says the image is FOR training NEX. Those are different claims. Only the second claim admits an image to the staircase_brain intelligence pathway.

**Batch #01 pilot confirmed empirically:**

- 8 of 19 records (42%) failed Gate 1 because they were marketing/canteen/general assets · `image_purpose` should have been `marketing_asset` from the start
- 5 of 19 records (26%) were article illustrations · `image_purpose` should have been `article_illustration`
- 2 of 19 records were reference-only (workshop scene · loft ladder) · `image_purpose` should have been `reference_only` or (for loft ladder) reclassified
- 0 of 19 records passed as clean `recognition_training` candidates

If this taxonomy had existed at image-ingest time, the recognition-pathway audit would have produced dramatically cleaner results.
