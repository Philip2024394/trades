# Straight Staircase (Cut String)

## Name

Straight Staircase (Cut String)

## Also Known As

* Straight Cut String Staircase
* Open String Staircase
* Cut Carriage Staircase
* Sawtooth String Staircase
* Exposed String Staircase

---

## Definition

A straight cut string staircase is a staircase that travels in a single uninterrupted direction, with one or both side strings cut to follow the profile of the treads and risers. This exposes the ends of the steps, creating a more decorative and visually open appearance than a closed string staircase.

---

## Characteristics

* Single uninterrupted flight.
* No turns, winders or intermediate landings unless incorporated into the overall staircase design.
* One or both strings are cut to the profile of the treads.
* The ends of the treads and risers remain visible.
* Often considered a more decorative staircase construction.
* Suitable for both traditional and contemporary interiors.
* Can incorporate decorative brackets, scrolls or feature detailing beneath the treads.
* Compatible with both closed and open riser designs where appropriate.

---

## Advantages

* Elegant and decorative appearance.
* Exposed tread ends highlight the craftsmanship of the staircase.
* Creates a lighter and more open visual effect.
* Allows decorative cut string brackets or scroll details.
* Ideal for feature staircases.
* Complements traditional, heritage and luxury interiors.
* Works well with premium hardwoods.
* Compatible with a wide variety of balustrade styles.
* Individual staircase components become part of the overall design rather than being concealed.

---

## Disadvantages

* More complex to manufacture than a closed string staircase.
* Requires greater machining accuracy and joinery precision.
* Decorative details increase manufacturing time.
* Exposed components may require more finishing and maintenance.
* Typically more expensive than a comparable closed string staircase due to increased craftsmanship.

## Space Requirements

A straight cut string staircase requires similar floor space to a straight closed string staircase. It is most suitable where the staircase is intended to be a visible architectural feature rather than concealed within enclosed walls.

---

## Design Considerations

A designer will normally consider:

* Overall architectural style.
* Visibility of the staircase.
* Decorative detailing beneath each tread.
* Baluster spacing and style.
* Handrail profile.
* Newel post design.
* Timber species.
* Timber grain continuity.
* Stair width.
* Lighting.
* Flooring transitions.
* Whether both strings or only one string will be cut.
* Consistency of decorative brackets or scrollwork.
* The staircase's appearance from multiple viewing angles.

A cut string staircase is often designed to become a focal point within the property.

---

## Installation Considerations

Professional installation normally includes:

* Confirming structural openings.
* Checking floor levels and wall alignment.
* Protecting decorative string details during handling.
* Installing newel posts securely.
* Ensuring each tread and riser is fully seated before fixing.
* Checking the alignment of exposed tread ends.
* Maintaining consistent decorative spacing.
* Preventing damage to finished hardwood surfaces.
* Completing balustrade installation with careful alignment to preserve the decorative appearance.

## Common Misconceptions

* A cut string staircase is not structurally weaker than a closed string staircase when correctly designed and manufactured.
* Decorative brackets are not structural supports; they are primarily aesthetic features.
* Cut string staircases are not limited to traditional homes and can be designed for contemporary interiors.
* Exposed tread ends do not automatically require more maintenance than enclosed staircases.
* Hardwood construction is not mandatory; many cut string staircases combine painted structural components with exposed hardwood features.
* A cut string staircase is not simply a closed string staircase with decorative cuts; it is a distinct construction style requiring different joinery and detailing.

---

## Summary

A straight cut string staircase combines traditional joinery with decorative craftsmanship. By exposing the tread profile and allowing feature detailing beneath each step, it creates a staircase that is both functional and visually distinctive. Suitable for a wide range of architectural styles, it is commonly chosen where the staircase is intended to become an important design feature within the home or building.
