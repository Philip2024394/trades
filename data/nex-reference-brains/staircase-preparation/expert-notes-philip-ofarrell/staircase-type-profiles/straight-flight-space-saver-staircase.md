# Straight Flight Space Saver Staircase

## Name

Straight Flight Space Saver Staircase

## Also Known As

* Space Saver Staircase
* Alternating Tread Staircase
* Paddle Staircase
* Paddle Tread Staircase
* Alternate Step Staircase
* Alternating Step Staircase
* Compact Staircase
* Loft Staircase

---

## Definition

A straight flight space saver staircase is a compact staircase designed to reduce the amount of floor space required by using specially shaped alternating treads. Each tread is formed to accommodate one foot at a time, allowing the staircase to achieve a steeper angle than a conventional staircase while remaining comfortable to use when climbed correctly.

---

## Characteristics

* Single uninterrupted straight flight.
* Alternating left and right tread design.
* Requires users to begin with the correct foot.
* Significantly steeper than a conventional staircase.
* Designed primarily where space is limited.
* Compact footprint.
* Can be manufactured with open or closed risers depending on the design.
* Usually fitted with a continuous handrail.
* Designed for occasional rather than frequent use.

---

## Advantages

* Requires considerably less floor space than a conventional staircase.
* Ideal where a standard staircase cannot be accommodated.
* Comfortable to use once the alternating tread pattern is understood.
* Can provide permanent access where only a loft ladder would otherwise fit.
* Available in traditional or contemporary styles.
* Can be manufactured from a variety of timber species.
* Attractive alternative to retractable loft ladders.

---

## Disadvantages

* Not suitable for every application.
* Users must become familiar with the alternating tread pattern.
* Less convenient for carrying large items.
* Not normally intended as the principal staircase in a home.
* Unsuitable for some users due to the steeper angle and tread arrangement.
* Requires careful consideration before specifying.

## Space Requirements

A straight flight space saver staircase is specifically designed for locations where a conventional staircase would occupy too much space. Its compact footprint allows access within smaller floor areas while maintaining a permanent staircase solution.

---

## Design Considerations

A designer will normally consider:

* Available floor space.
* Floor-to-floor height.
* Frequency of use.
* Intended users.
* Handrail position.
* Headroom.
* Lighting.
* Direction of travel.
* Timber selection.
* Stair width.
* Overall appearance.
* Integration with the surrounding interior.
* Ease of use for the intended occupants.

Because the staircase operates differently from a traditional staircase, the user experience is an important part of the design process.

## Installation Considerations

Professional installation normally includes:

* Confirming structural support.
* Checking floor-to-floor dimensions.
* Ensuring the staircase remains square during installation.
* Secure fixing of newel posts where fitted.
* Accurate positioning of alternating treads.
* Correct installation of the handrail.
* Verifying that all fixings are fully tightened.
* Protecting finished timber surfaces during installation.
* Completing all finishing work before use.

## Common Misconceptions

* A space saver staircase is not simply a steeper standard staircase; the alternating tread design is fundamental to how it functions.
* Every space saver staircase does not have identical tread shapes; designs vary between manufacturers.
* Space saver staircases are not intended to replace conventional staircases in every situation.
* Although compact, they still require careful design, accurate manufacture and professional installation.
* A smaller footprint does not mean lower quality; many are manufactured using premium hardwoods and high-quality joinery.

---

## Summary

A straight flight space saver staircase is a compact staircase solution designed for locations where available floor space is limited. By using alternating treads, it provides a practical means of access while occupying significantly less floor area than a conventional staircase. It is commonly used for loft conversions, mezzanines and similar spaces where a permanent staircase is preferred but space is restricted.
