# Straight Staircase (Closed String)

## Name

Straight Staircase (Closed String)

## Also Known As

* Straight Flight Staircase
* Single Flight Staircase
* Straight Run Staircase
* Closed String Staircase
* Housed String Staircase (traditional joinery)

---

## Definition

A straight closed string staircase is a staircase that travels continuously in one direction without changing course. The treads and risers are housed into solid side strings, hiding the ends of the steps and creating a clean, traditional appearance.

---

## Characteristics

* Single uninterrupted flight.
* No turns, winders or intermediate landings unless required by the building design.
* Treads and risers are housed into the side strings.
* Step ends are concealed by the strings.
* Strong traditional construction.
* Suitable for both painted and fully timber staircases.
* Available with closed or open risers depending on design requirements.
* Can be manufactured as a fully assembled staircase or as a kit for on-site assembly.

---

## Advantages

* Strong and durable construction.
* Clean, timeless appearance.
* Suitable for both traditional and contemporary interiors.
* Easier to manufacture accurately than many turning staircases.
* Efficient use of straight spaces.
* Can be finished in painted timber, hardwood or mixed-material designs.
* Well suited to carpet, timber or luxury flooring finishes.
* Simple layout makes surveying and installation straightforward.
* Compatible with a wide range of handrail and balustrade designs.
* Long service life when correctly manufactured and maintained.

---

## Disadvantages

* Requires a long uninterrupted floor space.
* Cannot change direction without introducing a landing or redesign.
* May occupy more linear floor area than turning staircases.
* Less suitable where available space is restricted.
* Large uninterrupted flights may require additional structural consideration in certain building designs.

---

## Typical Applications

* New-build homes.
* Property renovations.
* Loft conversions where sufficient length is available.
* Traditional houses.
* Contemporary homes.
* Apartments.
* Commercial offices.
* Retail premises.
* Educational buildings.
* Public buildings.

---

## Space Requirements

A straight staircase is best suited to properties with sufficient uninterrupted floor length. It works particularly well where a direct route between floors is available without needing to turn around corners or obstacles.

---

## Design Considerations

A designer will normally consider:

* Available floor length.
* Floor-to-floor height.
* Visual proportions.
* Width of the staircase.
* Handrail design.
* Newel post positions.
* Balustrade style.
* Timber species.
* Painted versus exposed timber.
* Lighting.
* Flooring transitions.
* Headroom.
* Overall architectural style of the property.

Good design should ensure the staircase looks balanced from every viewing angle while complementing the surrounding interior.

---

## Installation Considerations

Professional installation normally includes:

* Verifying floor levels.
* Checking wall positions.
* Confirming the opening dimensions.
* Ensuring the staircase is square before fixing.
* Installing newel posts securely.
* Correctly fitting housed treads and risers.
* Gluing and securing wedges where applicable.
* Preventing movement before final fixing.
* Protecting finished timber during installation.
* Completing balustrade installation after the main staircase is secured where appropriate.

## Common Misconceptions

* A straight staircase is not necessarily the cheapest staircase.
* Closed string does not mean poor quality or basic construction.
* Hardwood is not always the best choice for every component.
* Painted staircases are not always manufactured entirely from softwood.
* A larger staircase is not automatically stronger.
* Timber movement is a natural characteristic and should not automatically be considered a defect.
* Glass balustrades can be incorporated into traditional staircase designs.
* A straight staircase can be highly customised despite its simple layout.
* Appearance depends on the complete design, not only the timber species.
* Professional surveying and installation remain important even for a simple straight staircase.

---

## Summary

A straight closed string staircase is one of the most versatile and widely used staircase designs. Its simple geometry, concealed joinery and adaptable construction make it suitable for both traditional and contemporary properties. It offers excellent structural strength, flexible design options and compatibility with a wide range of materials, balustrade systems and finishes, making it a practical solution for residential and commercial applications alike.
