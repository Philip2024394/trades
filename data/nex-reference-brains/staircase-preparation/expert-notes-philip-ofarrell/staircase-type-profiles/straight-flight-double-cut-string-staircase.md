# Straight Flight Double Cut String Staircase

## Name

Straight Flight Double Cut String Staircase

## Also Known As

* Double Cut String Staircase
* Double Open String Staircase
* Twin Cut String Staircase
* Double Exposed String Staircase
* Open String Staircase (Both Sides)

---

## Definition

A straight flight double cut string staircase is a staircase that travels in one continuous direction with both side strings cut to follow the profile of the treads and risers. Unlike a standard cut string staircase, both sides of the staircase expose the ends of the treads, creating a fully symmetrical and decorative appearance that showcases the craftsmanship from every viewing angle.

---

## Characteristics

* Single uninterrupted straight flight.
* Both left and right strings are cut to the tread profile.
* Exposed tread ends on both sides.
* Highly decorative construction.
* Symmetrical appearance.
* Ideal as a feature staircase.
* Compatible with closed or open riser designs.
* Suitable for central hallways and open-plan interiors where both sides of the staircase remain visible.
* Frequently manufactured using premium hardwoods or painted and hardwood combinations.

---

## Advantages

* Creates a striking architectural feature.
* Decorative detailing is visible from both sides.
* Provides excellent visual balance and symmetry.
* Ideal for luxury homes and prestige developments.
* Enhances the appearance of premium timber treads.
* Compatible with glass, timber or metal balustrades.
* Offers extensive opportunities for bespoke detailing.
* Adds value through craftsmanship and visual appeal.

---

## Disadvantages

* More complex to manufacture than a single cut string staircase.
* Requires precise machining on both strings.
* Increased manufacturing and finishing time.
* Higher material and labour costs.
* Decorative components require additional care during transportation and installation.

## Space Requirements

A straight flight double cut string staircase requires a long uninterrupted floor area similar to any straight staircase. It performs best where both sides of the staircase are visible and can be appreciated as part of the interior design.

---

## Design Considerations

A designer will normally consider:

* Visibility from both sides.
* Symmetry of the staircase.
* Decorative cut string brackets or scrolls.
* Handrail profile.
* Balustrade design.
* Newel post style.
* Timber grain matching.
* Tread thickness.
* Feature starting steps.
* Lighting integration.
* Flooring transitions.
* Overall architectural style.
* Whether the staircase is intended to be the focal point of the room.

Because both strings remain visible, careful attention should be given to achieving consistent detailing and high-quality finishing throughout.

---

## Installation Considerations

Professional installation normally includes:

* Confirming the staircase opening dimensions.
* Checking floor levels and wall alignment.
* Protecting both decorative strings during handling.
* Ensuring the staircase remains square before final fixing.
* Installing newel posts securely.
* Aligning exposed tread ends on both sides.
* Protecting finished hardwood surfaces throughout installation.
* Completing balustrade installation with accurate alignment to maintain symmetry.
* Preventing damage to decorative brackets or feature details.

## Common Misconceptions

* A double cut string staircase is not structurally weaker than a closed string staircase when correctly designed and manufactured.
* Decorative brackets beneath the treads are generally aesthetic features rather than structural supports.
* Double cut string staircases are not limited to traditional interiors and can be designed for contemporary homes.
* Exposed tread ends require quality finishing but do not necessarily require significantly more maintenance.
* Both strings do not have to be manufactured entirely from hardwood; painted and hardwood combinations are common.
* A double cut string staircase is designed to showcase craftsmanship and visual symmetry rather than simply exposing both sides of the staircase.

---

## Summary

A straight flight double cut string staircase is a premium staircase construction that exposes the tread profile on both sides, creating a symmetrical and highly decorative appearance. Often selected for luxury homes and feature entrances, it combines traditional joinery with bespoke craftsmanship, allowing the staircase to become a central architectural feature while remaining practical, durable and adaptable to a wide variety of materials, balustrade systems and interior styles.
