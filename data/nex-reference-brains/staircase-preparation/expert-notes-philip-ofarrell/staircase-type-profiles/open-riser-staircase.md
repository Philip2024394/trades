# Open Riser Staircase

## Name

Open Riser Staircase

## Also Known As

* Open Tread Staircase
* Open Staircase
* Floating Riser Staircase
* Open Flight Staircase
* Contemporary Open Staircase

---

## Definition

An open riser staircase is a staircase constructed without solid risers between the treads, leaving an open space between each step. This design creates a lighter, more spacious appearance while allowing light to pass through the staircase.

---

## Characteristics

* No solid risers between the treads.
* Creates an open, airy appearance.
* Allows natural and artificial light to pass through the staircase.
* Often used in contemporary and modern architecture.
* Can be built using closed strings, cut strings or floating structural systems.
* Suitable for timber, glass and mixed-material designs.
* Can incorporate feature lighting within the treads or strings.
* Available in straight, quarter-turn, half-turn and curved configurations.

---

## Advantages

* Creates a bright and spacious feel.
* Allows light to travel through the staircase.
* Makes smaller rooms appear larger.
* Modern and contemporary appearance.
* Highlights the beauty of timber treads.
* Works exceptionally well with glass balustrades.
* Can become an architectural feature.
* Reduces the visual weight of the staircase.
* Compatible with a wide range of modern interior designs.

---

## Disadvantages

* May not be suitable for every household depending on the occupants.
* Some people may feel less comfortable using open riser staircases due to the visible gaps.
* Cleaning beneath and between treads may require additional care.
* Requires careful design and manufacture to achieve both appearance and structural performance.
* May have specific building regulation requirements depending on the country or jurisdiction.

## Space Requirements

An open riser staircase generally occupies a similar floor area to an equivalent closed riser staircase. It is particularly effective in properties where maximising the feeling of light and space is an important design objective.

## Installation Considerations

Professional installation normally includes:

* Verifying structural support points.
* Ensuring the staircase remains square throughout installation.
* Protecting exposed tread edges during handling.
* Accurate positioning of balustrades and handrails.
* Checking tread alignment and spacing.
* Installing glass panels where specified.
* Preventing movement before final fixing.
* Protecting finished timber surfaces throughout installation.
* Completing lighting installation where incorporated into the design.

## Common Misconceptions

* An open riser staircase is not automatically a floating staircase.
* Open risers do not mean the staircase is structurally weaker.
* Glass balustrades are not required; timber or metal balustrades can also be used.
* Open riser staircases are not limited to luxury homes.
* Modern appearance does not prevent the use of traditional timber materials.
* Open riser staircases still require careful structural design and professional installation.
* A more open appearance does not necessarily reduce the staircase's strength or durability.

---

## Summary

An open riser staircase is a modern staircase design that omits the solid risers between treads to create an open, light-filled appearance. It is widely used in contemporary architecture because it enhances the feeling of space while showcasing high-quality materials and craftsmanship. With appropriate design, construction and installation, an open riser staircase can provide both architectural impact and long-term functionality in residential and commercial environments.

---

## Additional Design Considerations

When designing an open riser staircase, consideration should be given to the intended users of the staircase, including young children, older adults and pets. For family homes, designers often choose smaller, carefully proportioned openings between treads to reduce the visual gap while maintaining the desired open appearance. Where appropriate, additional design features such as horizontal stainless steel support bars, glass infill panels or other balustrade systems may be incorporated to create a more enclosed appearance and reduce the likelihood of children attempting to pass through the staircase. The final design should always balance aesthetics, usability and the applicable requirements for the project location.

## Additional Common Misconceptions

* Open riser staircases are not inherently unsuitable for family homes. The overall design, balustrade arrangement and the size of the openings between treads all influence how the staircase functions within a home.
* Every open riser staircase does not have the same gap between treads; the appearance can be adapted to suit the design intent.
* Stainless steel bars, glass panels and other architectural features are design options that can enhance the appearance while creating a more enclosed visual effect.
