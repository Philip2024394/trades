# Staircase Brain Membership Criteria

*Governance document · draft pending expert confirmation · not Layer 1 evidence until Philip approves.*

*This document answers one question for every candidate image:*

> **"Should this image contribute to staircase understanding?"**

*It does NOT answer the question "Is this a staircase image?" — those are different questions. A staircase photograph can exist without being useful to a staircase recognition brain. This document exists to prevent the failure mode where subject-tagging is silently promoted to brain-connection.*

---

## Governing discipline sentence (Philip 2026-07-31 · review refinement)

> **"High visual quality does not equal staircase intelligence value."**

**Rationale:** a premium architectural photograph may be excellent for inspiration, customer browsing, or visual search — yet still fail as staircase component recognition, construction knowledge, or technical reference.

**Effect on this criteria:** applied throughout — particularly at Gate 4 (Evidence Quality). Beauty is not admission. Aesthetic impact is not evidence weight. Marketability is not intelligence value.

**Composes with:** the guiding question above (visual quality = *"Is this a staircase image?"* answer · intelligence value = *"Should this image contribute to staircase understanding?"* answer). The two are structurally different tests.

**The audit finding (Philip 2026-07-31):**

This document is the "define what belongs" step.

## The 5 gates (Philip 2026-07-31 · verbatim structure)

### Gate 1 · SUBJECT RELEVANCE

**Question:** Is staircase the primary visual subject?

- **YES** → continue to Gate 2
- **NO** → different domain · route to appropriate brain OR leave unclassified

**Discipline:** the image must be ABOUT the staircase, not merely CONTAIN a staircase in the background. A hallway photograph that happens to include a staircase visible in the corner may fail Gate 1 even though a staircase is present. A close-up of a staircase component in isolation passes Gate 1.

**Rule A applied:** primary-subject-ness is an observation call · not a fact the pixels prove alone. When uncertain, the named-expert reviewer records the reasoning in a per-image review note (not silent).

---

### Gate 2 · STAIRCASE EVIDENCE

**Question:** Does the image contain visible staircase information?

**Examples of qualifying evidence (Philip 2026-07-31):**

- Whole staircase
- Stair geometry
- Stair parts (tread · riser · string · newel · handrail · baluster · nosing · balustrade panel · glass clamp · newel cap · newel base · etc.)
- Junctions (tread-to-string · handrail-to-newel · baluster-to-tread · baluster-to-handrail · newel-to-floor · staircase-to-landing · staircase-to-wall · etc.)
- Materials (visible timber species suggestion · metal appearance · glass · painted finish · stone · composite)
- Construction relationships (cantilever · closed-string · cut-string · mono-string · box-string · concealed structure · exposed structure)

**Discipline:** *"visible information"* is intentionally broader than *"whole visible staircase."* A close-up of a single tread joint qualifies if it teaches something about staircase construction. Rule B applies: what qualifies as evidence is decided by the named expert · not auto-derived.

---

### Gate 3 · RECOGNITION VALUE

**Question:** Does the image teach something NEX needs to recognise?

**Examples of recognition value (Philip 2026-07-31):**

- Staircase type (straight flight · quarter turn · half turn · L-shaped · U-shaped · curved · spiral · space-saver · alternating tread · etc.)
- Component relationship (how balusters connect to handrail · how strings support treads · how newels terminate handrails · etc.)
- Design language (traditional · contemporary · industrial-modern minimalist · edge-defined architectural · contained circulation · traditional-craft with modern-material accent · classic joinery · etc.)
- Installation detail (fixing method visible · concealed vs exposed structure · wedging · joinery · brackets · concealment discipline)
- Material relationship (oak+glass · oak+cable · oak+steel · painted+timber · timber+steel · etc.)

**Discipline (Philip 2026-07-31 verbatim):** *"A staircase photograph can exist without being useful to a staircase recognition brain."* Gate 3 is where THAT distinction is applied. Duplicate views of the same instance may be dropped if they teach nothing new. New angles of an already-authored subject may fail Gate 3 if the recognition value is redundant.

**Composes with candidate #12:** image count ≠ knowledge count. Three visually-different photographs of the same design may equal one recognition unit · not three.

---

### Gate 4 · EVIDENCE QUALITY

**Question:** Does the image meet ADR-0033 threshold?

**ADR-0033 thresholds (loaded in CLAUDE.md · authoritative):**

- **≥70** → clean save · intelligence candidate · enters matcher/brains/cards
- **50-69** → draft only · filtered from intelligence reads · admin can promote
- **<50** → SAVE FAILED · admin must resolve before save is allowed
- **`primary_brain=null`** → SAVE FAILED regardless of score

**Discipline:** ADR-0033 is not this document's discretion · it is a shipped rule. This gate is a HARD threshold check. An image passing Gates 1-3 but failing Gate 4 does NOT enter staircase brain intelligence · it may enter as reference-only content or wait for quality lift.

**Governing discipline sentence applied at this gate:** *"High visual quality does not equal staircase intelligence value."* (Philip 2026-07-31 · review refinement). An image may LOOK premium (rendered · lit · composed) yet score below ADR-0033's ≥70 threshold when NEX's intelligence layer measures its evidence content. Score is not aesthetic. Aesthetic is not admission.

**Retrospective application (Philip 2026-07-31 audit):** 0 of the 94 currently-connected rows meet ≥70. Applying Gate 4 retrospectively means those 94 rows are recognized as **draft-quality intelligence at best** — they should not be promoted to full intelligence-active status without quality lift.

---

### Gate 5 · DOMAIN BOUNDARY

**Question:** Is staircase the correct primary brain?

- **YES** → assign `primary_brain=staircase_brain` (subject to Gate 4 quality)
- **NO** → route to a different brain (timber_brain · garden_staircase_brain · interior_brain · materials_brain · etc.)

**Discipline (Philip 2026-07-31 verbatim scope constraint):** *"we require only staircase images and stairparts · not swimming pools · not timber sheds · only staircase images."* This gate enforces the scope filter. An image that passes Gates 1-4 for staircase relevance might STILL belong in a different brain if the primary utility is timber-species knowledge (timber_brain) or garden-context (garden_staircase_brain) rather than staircase construction/recognition.

**Note on existing manifest brain population:** 145 rows currently `primary_brain=staircase_brain` · 5 rows `timber_brain` · 5 rows `garden_staircase_brain` · 885 rows unclassified. Gate 5 governs new assignments and reassignments · does NOT auto-move existing rows.

## Application procedure (per candidate image)

For each image being reviewed for staircase_brain membership:

1. **Named expert opens the image** (Philip or approved reviewer under Rule B refined form).
2. **Gate 1 · Subject Relevance:** YES / NO. If NO → stop · route elsewhere.
3. **Gate 2 · Staircase Evidence:** enumerate visible qualifying evidence categories. If none → stop.
4. **Gate 3 · Recognition Value:** enumerate what NEX would learn from this image. If nothing new → stop.
5. **Gate 4 · Evidence Quality:** check ADR-0033 score. If <50 → do not admit. If 50-69 → draft-only. If ≥70 → intelligence candidate.
6. **Gate 5 · Domain Boundary:** confirm staircase_brain is the CORRECT primary brain (not another brain).
7. **Record decision:** per-image review note recording all 5 gate outcomes · reviewer name · date · reasoning.
8. **Apply classification:** only if ALL 5 gates pass affirmatively. Otherwise route or leave unclassified.

**No silent admissions. No batch skips. No gate reordering.**
