## Candidate 1 · NEX Executive Controller (Top-3 Generation 3 priority)

### Purpose

*"Every advanced cognitive system needs something that decides: which capabilities should be used for this question? Not to answer the question — to orchestrate thinking."*

### Example

User: *"Can reclaimed oak be used for a floating staircase?"*

Executive Controller decides:

| Activated | Not activated |
|---|---|
| ✓ Subject Intelligence | ✗ Marketing |
| ✓ Material Knowledge | ✗ Diagnostic |
| ✓ Engineering Reasoning | ✗ Teaching Mode |
| ✓ Safety Review | ✗ Visual Composer |
| ✓ Confidence Check | |

*"That is Proportional Thinking in action. You're already committed to the principle. This would become the MECHANISM that enforces it."*

### Constitutional Anchor

- Proportional Thinking (Creator Governance Principle 6)
- Cognitive Efficiency (Constitution philosophy)
- Principle 13 (parity: capability + governance + transparency + measurability)

## Candidate 2 · Knowledge Maturity Index (KMI)

### Purpose

Complements CQI. CQI measures REASONING quality · KMI measures KNOWLEDGE maturity.

### Six Dimensions

| Dimension | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Subject coverage | How many canonical subjects exist? |
| Relationship coverage | How well connected is the knowledge graph? |
| Evidence coverage | How many claims are evidence-backed? |
| Engineering depth | Breadth and depth of technical knowledge. |
| Teaching assets | Availability of images, diagrams and explanations. |
| Validation status | How much knowledge has been reviewed? |

*"That would complement CQI by measuring the maturity of the knowledge itself · not just the quality of reasoning."*

## Candidate 3 · NEX Insight & Observation Engine

### Positioning

Renamed from any "Humour Engine" framing per Philip's discipline. Value: *"A knowledgeable person answers the question. An expert notices what everyone else missed. That's exactly the capability."*

### Seven Modules

| # | Module | Impact | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Observation Engine** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Detect unusual measurements · inconsistent drawings · missing information · hidden risks · recurring customer behaviour. Example: *"The headroom appears tight beneath the landing · worth confirming before manufacture."* User never asked. |
| 2 | **Pattern Discovery** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Look for unexpected engineering relationships. Example: reclaimed oak → hidden nails → tool wear → production cost → possible delays. |
| 3 | **Opportunity Recognition** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | Extends Marketing Intelligence. Example: matching hallway furniture → benches · console tables · radiator covers · shelving from same timber batch. Not creative · commercially logical. |
| 4 | **Intelligent Comparison** | Experts compare automatically. Not *"Oak is good"* → *"Compared with ash · beech · walnut · painted MDF · what matters for this customer?"* |
| 5 | **Anomaly Detection** | Flag statistically unusual measurements · timber · regulations · manufacturing sequence · installation order · marketing performance · customer behaviour. |
| 6 | **Curiosity Engine** | Links to existing Why intent. Instead of only answering *"What is a tread?"* also considers *"Users often ask next: why is it called a tread · why different sizes · why do some squeak · why does oak move."* Offers naturally. |
| 7 | **Insight Composer** | Signature ability. End with *"Did you know..."* or *"One thing worth considering..."* or *"Many homeowners don't realise..."* ONLY when it genuinely adds value. |

### Insight Confidence (bonus module)

Distinguish observation certainty:

- *"This staircase may require an additional trimming joist"* · Confidence 91% · Evidence: opening dimensions suggest...
- *"This customer might prefer oak"* · Confidence 54% · Reason: previous questions suggest interest in natural timber · insufficient evidence to recommend.

Prevents insight becoming guesswork.

### Explicit Position on Humour (Philip 2026-07-31)

Do NOT build "Humour Engine." Humour becomes one Communication Style option (in Communication Intelligence Layer · v3 candidate) alongside Technical · Educational · Professional · Conversational · Light-hearted. Only appears when: context appropriate · doesn't reduce clarity · doesn't undermine trust · doesn't distract from safety or technical accuracy. *"For a staircase company, credibility should always come first."*
