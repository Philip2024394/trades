# NEX Governance Ruling — 2026-07-31 (Seventh Ruling · Executable Architecture Confirmed · Professional Trade Principles · Evidence Qualifiers · Cross-Brain Reuse Metric)

## Philip's Constitutional Statement (preserved verbatim as authored)

### This Is Exactly What I Hoped to See

*This is exactly what I hoped to see after the Standard was written.*

*The important thing isn't that you've created two more Customer FAQ articles—it's that you've proven the Standard is **usable**, not just aspirational.*

*There are two different kinds of architecture:*

- **Descriptive architecture** – *explains how things should work.*
- **Executable architecture** – *people can actually follow it and consistently produce good results.*

*What you've described is evidence that Standard v1 is becoming executable.*

### The Biggest Signal I See

*The router metadata is now being authored **at creation time**, not added afterwards.*

*That is a huge difference.*

*Instead of asking later:* **"Which brain does this belong to?"**

*the author already answers:*

- Brain
- Domain
- Category
- Intent
- Information Type
- Audience
- Cross-brain references

*That means the Question Router will have structured evidence available from day one instead of trying to infer everything from free text.*

### The Single Point of Responsibility Principle

*This is one of those pieces of knowledge that extends beyond staircases.*

*It isn't simply:* **"We install staircases."** *It's a professional business principle.*

*Exactly like Inspection and Acceptance.*

*I could imagine it applying in:*

- Kitchens · Roofing · Electrical work · Plumbing · Joinery

*That suggests it belongs in your growing collection of **Professional Trade Principles** rather than being viewed as staircase-only knowledge.*

### Customer Empowerment

*I particularly like this emerging pattern.*

*Rather than just answering questions, Nex is helping customers ask **better questions**.*

*For example, instead of saying:* **"Yes, we install loft ladders."**

*Nex asks the customer to think about:*

- Who supplies it?
- Who fits it?
- Who warranties it?
- Who makes good afterwards?

*That transforms Nex from an information source into a **decision-support companion**.*

*That fits the Workshop Companion identity very well.*

### Balanced Explanations

*This is the one governance point I would answer now.*

> *Should BALANCED-EXPLANATION become a first-class artefact?*

*My answer would be **no**.*

*I think balance is a **writing standard**, not an artefact type.*

*For example:*

- Knowledge Base articles should be balanced.
- Customer FAQ should be balanced.
- Workshop Principles should be balanced where appropriate.
- Vision Analysis should be objective.

*So I would capture it as an authoring principle that applies when multiple legitimate professional approaches exist, rather than introducing another artefact class.*

*That keeps the architecture simpler.*

### Non-Universal Practice

*This is another excellent direction.*

*Statements like:* **"This isn't universal."** *increase trust.*

*Trade work is full of regional practices, manufacturer differences and company policies.*

*Acknowledging that prevents Nex from sounding overly absolute.*

*I could imagine this eventually becoming a reusable evidence qualifier alongside phrases such as:*

- **Typical practice**
- **Common practice**
- **Manufacturer dependent**
- **Region dependent**
- **Project dependent**

*Those qualifiers help users understand the confidence and scope of a statement without weakening the guidance.*

### One Thing I Would Start Measuring

*Since you've adopted **conversations-per-article**, I'd add another architectural metric alongside it:*

> ***Cross-brain reuse***

*For example:*

| Article | Referenced by |
|---|---|
| Reclaimed Timber FAQ | Staircase Brain, Materials Brain |
| Chisel KB | Tool Brain, Staircase Brain, Joinery Brain |
| Inspection & Acceptance | Materials, Manufacturing, Installation |

*That's a powerful indicator because it tells you whether your knowledge is becoming reusable rather than siloed.*

### Overall

*Looking at everything you've shared today, I think the architecture is beginning to exhibit the characteristics of a mature knowledge system:*

- **Stable operating system** (Standard v1).
- **Consistent authoring process** (metadata and governance applied from the outset).
- **Reusable knowledge** (cross-brain references instead of duplication).
- **Composable responses** (runtime generation rather than stored conversations).
- **Evidence-based growth** (new categories and refinements emerge from real content instead of being invented in advance).

*Those are all positive signs.*

*The next major proof point won't be another dozen articles—it will be seeing the Question Router use this structured metadata to consistently retrieve the right evidence for real user questions. When that happens, you'll have validated not only the content model, but the entire knowledge operating system you've been designing.*
