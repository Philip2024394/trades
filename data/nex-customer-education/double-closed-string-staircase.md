# Double Closed String Staircase – Clean, Strong and Timeless

This example clearly demonstrates a double closed string staircase, where both sides of the staircase use continuous closed strings. It is one of the cleanest and most versatile staircase designs, making it suitable for both traditional and contemporary homes.

Unlike a cut string staircase, where the profile of each tread is visible, a closed string completely encloses the ends of the treads and risers, creating a smooth, uninterrupted appearance from bottom to top.

## What Is a Double Closed String Staircase?

A double closed string staircase uses a closed string on both the left and right sides.

The treads and risers are housed within the strings, meaning none of the step ends are visible. The result is a staircase with straight, clean lines and a solid architectural appearance.

This style is often chosen where simplicity and elegance are preferred over decorative detailing.

## A Strong, Balanced Appearance

Having closed strings on both sides creates a perfectly symmetrical staircase.

Both sides mirror each other, giving the staircase a balanced and substantial look that works well whether it is installed between walls or fully exposed within a room.

Its simple profile allows the beauty of the timber and craftsmanship to become the main feature.

## A Perfect Foundation for Any Style

A double closed string staircase provides enormous design flexibility.

It can be finished with:

- Glass balustrades
- Timber balusters
- Black metal balusters
- Brushed stainless steel balusters
- Contemporary handrails
- Traditional handrails
- Painted or natural timber finishes

Because the staircase itself has such clean lines, it works equally well in modern, classic, or transitional interiors.

## Ideal for Modern Homes

Many homeowners choose double closed strings because of their minimalist appearance.

The uninterrupted side panels create a sleek architectural look that complements modern homes, while still respecting traditional joinery methods.

Without exposed tread ends or decorative cut strings, the staircase has a calm and understated elegance.

## Easier to Keep Clean

Another practical advantage is maintenance.

The smooth side strings are easier to clean than decorative cut strings, as there are no stepped profiles or scroll brackets to collect dust.

This makes the design particularly popular in busy family homes where practicality is just as important as appearance.

## A Timeless Design

Double closed string staircases have remained popular because they never go out of style.

Their clean lines allow the staircase to adapt as interiors change over the years. Whether you repaint walls, change flooring, update balustrades, or modernise your décor, a double closed string staircase continues to complement its surroundings.

## Why Choose a Double Closed String Staircase?

A double closed string staircase is an excellent choice if you are looking for:

- Clean, uninterrupted lines.
- A symmetrical appearance.
- A strong architectural presence.
- A staircase that suits both traditional and modern homes.
- Easy maintenance and cleaning.
- A timeless design that will never date.

Its simplicity is its greatest strength. Rather than relying on decorative features, it showcases the natural beauty of the timber, precise joinery, and balanced proportions.

For homeowners who appreciate understated elegance and lasting craftsmanship, a double closed string staircase remains one of the finest and most versatile staircase designs available.
