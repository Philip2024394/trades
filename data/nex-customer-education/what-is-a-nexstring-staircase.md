# What is a NexString™ Staircase?

## Definition

NexString™ is the NEX brand name for a staircase where the outer string forms a continuous architectural face that encloses the ends of the treads and risers, creating a clean, uninterrupted side profile.

While the construction method is commonly referred to in the staircase industry as a closed string, NEX uses NexString™ as its customer-facing design language.

## Recognition

A staircase is identified as a NexString by these characteristics:

- Continuous outer string.
- Tread ends are concealed inside the string.
- Clean uninterrupted diagonal line.
- Premium furniture-style appearance.
- Suitable for traditional and contemporary homes.
- Can use timber, glass or metal balustrades.
- Shape varies to suit each project while the construction principle remains the same.

## Design Flexibility

A NexString staircase is not one fixed design.

It may include:

- Straight or shaped lower steps.
- Curved or radiused bullnose steps.
- Wall-side shaped treads.
- Feature lighting.
- Modern or traditional newel posts.
- Square, metal, glass or turned balusters.

These are aesthetic choices built on the same NexString construction system.
