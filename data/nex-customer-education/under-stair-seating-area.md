# Create a Relaxing Seating Area Under Your Staircase

The space beneath a staircase doesn't have to be hidden away or used only for storage. With thoughtful design, it can become one of the most inviting and practical areas in a home.

NEX design intelligence loves transforming under-stair spaces into comfortable seating areas that add both style and functionality. Whether the goal is a quiet place to relax, read a book, or simply enjoy a morning coffee, a bespoke seating area can make the most of every inch of a home.

## Turn Empty Space into Somewhere to Sit

Instead of leaving the area unused, imagine a beautifully built-in bench that follows the shape of the staircase.

With carefully chosen materials and finishes, the space can become a natural extension of the interior while creating a welcoming feature for family and guests.

## Designed to Match the Staircase

A bespoke seating area can be crafted using the same materials and finishes as the staircase, creating a seamless, integrated appearance.

Popular choices include:

- Oak seating with matching staircase details
- Rich walnut for a warm, luxurious feel
- Mahogany for timeless elegance
- Painted finishes for a contemporary look
- Black matte cabinetry with timber accents
- Brushed stainless steel details for modern homes

The result is a feature that looks as though it has always been part of the home.

## Add Comfort and Practicality

A seating area can be customised with features that suit any lifestyle, including:

- Upholstered seat cushions
- Hidden storage beneath the bench
- Open shelving for books
- Decorative display niches
- LED feature lighting
- USB charging points
- Side tables or floating shelves
- Coat hooks for entrance hall seating

By combining comfort with clever storage, the space becomes both beautiful and practical.

## The Perfect Spot for Everyday Living

An under-stair seating area is ideal for many uses:

- Reading a favourite book
- Enjoying a morning coffee
- A quiet place to relax
- Putting on or removing shoes
- Waiting for family before heading out
- A cosy corner for children to read
- A welcoming feature in the entrance hall

It's a simple addition that can quickly become one of the most frequently used spaces in a home.

## Complete the Look

The atmosphere can be enhanced with thoughtful finishing touches such as:

- Soft cushions and throws
- Warm LED lighting
- Indoor plants
- Framed artwork
- Decorative wall panelling
- Stylish shelving
- Feature wallpaper
- Natural wood finishes

These details help transform the area into a comfortable retreat while complementing the overall staircase design.

## The NEX Approach

A staircase should do more than connect two floors — it should inspire the spaces around it.

A bespoke seating area beneath the staircase is an excellent way to make the most of unused space while creating a feature that adds warmth, character, and everyday practicality to a home.

Sometimes, the best seat in the house isn't in the living room — it's the beautifully designed space beneath the staircase, where thoughtful craftsmanship meets comfortable living.
