# Why Every Staircase Is Designed Around Its Space

There is no standard staircase.

Every staircase is designed to fit the space available.

Unlike furniture, a staircase is part of the structure of the home. The available floor area, ceiling height, walls, doors and landing positions determine the final geometry.

## Space determines design

Depending on the project, the staircase may require:

- Curved wall-side treads.
- Shaped corners.
- Tapered treads.
- Bullnose starting steps.
- Offset newel positions.
- Unequal string lengths.

These are intentional design responses, not defects.

## Every project is unique

No two homes are identical.

A staircase should be designed to fit the available space while maintaining comfort, safety and visual balance.

The goal is not to force a staircase into a predefined shape — the goal is to create the best staircase for that particular home.
