# Create a Home Office Under Your Staircase

As more people work from home, every square metre of living space becomes valuable. One of the smartest ways to maximise a home is by transforming the unused area beneath the staircase into a compact home office.

NEX design intelligence treats under-stair spaces as an opportunity rather than wasted space. With thoughtful design, this often-overlooked area can become a stylish, practical workspace that blends seamlessly with the staircase and the rest of the home.

## Turn Unused Space into a Productive Workspace

The space beneath a staircase is often perfect for a home office because it naturally creates a quiet, defined area without needing a separate room.

A bespoke under-stair office can provide everything needed, including:

- A built-in desk
- Floating shelves
- Drawer storage
- Filing cabinets
- Cable management
- Charging stations
- A comfortable workspace
- Decorative lighting

Designed correctly, it can feel like a purpose-built office rather than an afterthought.

## A Bespoke Design That Matches the Staircase

An under-stair office does not have to look like standard furniture.

It can be custom-built to follow the shape of the staircase, creating a clean, integrated appearance.

Materials that complement the staircase include:

- Natural oak
- Walnut
- Mahogany
- Painted finishes
- Black matte cabinetry
- Glass shelving
- Brushed stainless steel details

The result is a workspace that feels like part of the home's architecture.

## Small Space, Big Functionality

Even a compact area can include impressive features:

- Hidden printer storage
- Soft-close drawers
- Pull-out keyboard shelves
- Adjustable shelving
- LED task lighting
- Built-in power sockets and USB charging
- Display shelves for books or awards
- Decorative wall panelling

Every inch can be designed to work efficiently.

## Keep the Home Organised

One of the biggest benefits of an under-stair office is reducing clutter elsewhere in the home.

Instead of paperwork spreading across the dining table or kitchen worktop, everything has its own dedicated place.

This creates a cleaner, more organised living environment while making it easier to switch between work and home life.

## Designed for Today — and Tomorrow

A bespoke under-stair office is a flexible investment.

As needs change, the same space can easily become:

- A study area for children
- A craft station
- A homework desk
- A reading corner
- A charging station for the whole family
- Additional storage
- A display area

Its purpose can evolve without major alterations.

## The NEX Approach

A staircase should do more than connect two floors — it should help the home work smarter.

A beautifully designed staircase combined with a bespoke under-stair office creates a practical and elegant feature that adds everyday value. By making use of space that might otherwise remain empty, a workspace can be functional, stylish, and perfectly integrated into the home.

Sometimes, the most productive room in the house isn't a room at all — it's the clever space waiting beneath the staircase.
