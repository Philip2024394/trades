# Modern Straight-Flight Cut-String Staircase with Stainless Steel Balusters

This staircase demonstrates how traditional timber joinery can be combined with modern materials to create a timeless design. The staircase is a straight-flight cut-string staircase, where each tread profile is visible from the side because the string follows the shape of every tread and riser.

The staircase features a rich hardwood construction with a clear varnished finish, complemented by brushed stainless steel square balusters. The combination of warm timber and metal creates a clean contemporary appearance while maintaining the strength and durability expected from a traditional staircase.

## Design Features

- Straight-flight staircase with no turns or landings.
- Cut-string construction, exposing the profile of each tread.
- Hardwood treads, risers and strings finished in a deep mahogany or walnut stain.
- Stainless steel square balusters giving the staircase a modern architectural appearance.
- Matching timber handrail with continuous clean lines.
- Large square hardwood newel post with a brushed stainless steel base sleeve.
- Minimal detailing allowing the natural timber grain to become the focal point.
- Recessed LED wall lighting enhancing safety and highlighting the staircase at night.
- White walls provide contrast, allowing the timber staircase to stand out as the feature within the hallway.

## The Newel Post

The newel post is the structural anchor of the staircase. It supports the handrail, secures the balustrade and provides rigidity to the entire stair system.

This design uses a large square contemporary newel post, giving the staircase a bold and substantial appearance. The brushed stainless steel sleeve at the base protects the timber while adding a modern industrial detail.

Like traditional staircases, the newel size is largely a design decision:

- **90 mm newel posts** create a lighter, more minimalist appearance.
- **120 mm newel posts** create a stronger architectural statement and suit larger homes with higher ceilings.

Both sizes are structurally functional when designed correctly. The choice depends on the desired visual impact rather than strength alone.

## Metal Balusters

The stainless steel balusters provide several advantages:

- Contemporary appearance.
- Low maintenance.
- Resistant to corrosion.
- Slim profile allows more light through the staircase.
- Makes hallways appear larger and more open.
- Pairs exceptionally well with hardwoods such as oak, walnut, mahogany and ash.

The square profile shown here gives a crisp modern aesthetic compared with traditional turned timber spindles.

## Cut String Construction

Unlike a closed-string staircase, where the tread ends are hidden inside the string, a cut-string staircase exposes every tread and riser.

Advantages include:

- Highlights the craftsmanship of the staircase.
- Creates visual depth and shadow lines.
- Allows decorative tread nosings to be appreciated.
- Offers a more bespoke appearance.
- Particularly suited to contemporary and transitional interiors.

## Contemporary Style

This staircase illustrates how modern staircase design relies on simplicity rather than excessive ornamentation.

Key characteristics include:

- Large square newel posts.
- Slim stainless steel balusters.
- Straight uninterrupted handrail.
- Rich natural timber.
- Minimal mouldings.
- Clean geometric lines.
- Open, bright hallway with concealed lighting.

The overall result is elegant, functional, and timeless, making it suitable for modern homes, luxury developments, and architect-designed interiors.
