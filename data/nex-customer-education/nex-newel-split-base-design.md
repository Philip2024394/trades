# The Nex Newel™ Split Base Design

## Definition

The Nex Newel™ Split Base Design is a NEX feature where the lower base of the newel post is visually separated from the upper post by precision spacers, stainless steel plates and concealed dowel construction.

## Characteristics

- Independent lower plinth.
- Floating visual separation.
- Stainless steel separator plates.
- Concealed dowel connection.
- Structural strength with a lightweight appearance.
- Can be produced in oak, walnut, ash or painted finishes.
- Suitable for contemporary staircases.

## Purpose

The split creates a premium furniture-inspired detail that makes the newel appear engineered rather than carved from a single block, giving the staircase a distinctive NEX identity.
