# How Long Will a Garden Staircase Last?

A well-built garden staircase made from quality decking materials can last for many years, but its lifespan depends on the timber, climate, maintenance, and how it is constructed.

Here are some typical lifespans:

| Material | Expected Lifespan* |
|---|---|
| Pressure-treated softwood | 15–25 years |
| Cedar or larch | 15–30 years |
| Durable hardwoods | 25–40+ years |
| Composite decking stairs | 25–30+ years |

*These are typical estimates. Actual lifespan depends on installation quality, climate, exposure, and maintenance.*

## What Makes a Staircase Last Longer?

The timber itself is only part of the story. A staircase built correctly can last much longer than one built with poor detailing.

Important factors include:

- Good drainage so water doesn't collect.
- Stainless steel or galvanised fixings.
- Pressure-treated structural timber.
- Good airflow around the staircase.
- Regular cleaning.
- Replacing damaged boards before they affect the structure.

## Climate Makes a Difference

In a normal climate with typical seasons:

- Rain will wet the timber.
- Sunshine will dry it.
- Winter will cause expansion and contraction.
- Frost may affect exposed surfaces.

A staircase designed for outdoor use is intended to cope with these seasonal changes.

The biggest enemy is constant dampness, not simply rain.

## Which Parts Wear Out First?

Usually, the decking boards or stair treads show wear before the main structure.

This isn't necessarily a problem.

Many staircases are designed so the walking boards can be replaced while the main framework remains in good condition.

This can significantly reduce future repair costs.

## Should I Maintain It?

Yes.

Simple maintenance can add many years to the life of your staircase.

Once or twice a year:

- Sweep away leaves.
- Wash off algae and moss.
- Check handrails are secure.
- Inspect screws and bolts.
- Look for damaged boards.
- Recoat or oil the timber if recommended for your chosen finish.

## Can It Last 30 Years?

Yes.

A professionally built staircase using quality materials, proper fixings, and regular maintenance can remain safe and attractive for 30 years or more. Some hardwood outdoor staircases have lasted much longer.

## Think Long Term

When planning your staircase, don't just consider the initial cost.

Ask questions such as:

- Can individual treads be replaced?
- Are replacement decking boards readily available?
- Are the fixings corrosion-resistant?
- Is the timber suitable for ground contact where necessary?
- Will the staircase be easy to maintain?

Choosing a well-designed staircase may cost a little more initially, but it can provide decades of reliable service while enhancing your garden and adding value to your home.
