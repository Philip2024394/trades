# Drawer Storage Staircases – Hidden Storage in Every Step

Modern staircases don't just have to connect one floor to another — they can also provide valuable storage that would otherwise be wasted.

One of the smartest solutions is to use the riser section of the staircase to create hidden pull-out drawers. These discreet storage compartments make excellent use of the space beneath each step while maintaining the clean appearance of the staircase.

## Making Every Step Work Harder

Each riser can be transformed into a shallow drawer, providing storage for everyday items such as:

- Shoes
- Slippers
- Children's toys
- Dog leads
- Gloves and hats
- Cleaning cloths
- Tools
- Keys and household essentials

Because the drawers are built into the staircase, they remain almost invisible until needed.

## "Won't the Drawers Open While Walking?"

This is one of the first questions people ask.

The answer is no, when the drawers are designed correctly.

Each drawer is installed with a slight downward angle towards the back of the staircase. This means gravity naturally encourages the drawer to remain closed rather than move forward.

In addition, each drawer is secured using four concealed magnetic catches, providing a strong holding force that keeps it firmly shut during normal use.

The combination of the rearward angle and multiple magnetic locks means the drawers stay securely closed, even with the everyday vibrations created by people walking up and down the staircase.

## Thicker Treads for Added Strength

One important design consideration when incorporating storage drawers into a staircase is the thickness of the treads.

A standard staircase tread is often around 35 mm thick, depending on the timber species and staircase design. However, when drawers are built into the risers, the tread above normally becomes thicker to provide additional strength and to accommodate the drawer mechanism.

For example:

- **Walnut**, being a dense hardwood, can often achieve the required strength with a slightly thinner tread than some other timbers.
- **Mahogany** may require a little more thickness depending on the design and span.
- **Pine and many softwoods** generally require a thicker tread to provide the same rigidity and long-term performance.

A typical comparison might be:

- Standard painted softwood staircase: 35 mm tread
- Hardwood walnut staircase with storage: 40–45 mm tread
- Softwood staircase with storage: 45–55 mm tread

These figures are examples only, as the final dimensions depend on the staircase design, timber species, span, loading requirements, and the drawer system being used.

The increased tread thickness is often seen as an advantage, as it gives the staircase a more substantial, premium appearance while improving structural performance.

## Easy to Open When Needed

Although the drawers remain firmly closed during normal use, they are still easy to access.

Simply pull the drawer forward using the concealed finger pull or integrated handle, and it glides smoothly out on quality drawer runners.

When pushed closed, the magnets automatically engage, locking the drawer neatly back into position.

## A Perfect Solution for Smaller Homes

Hidden riser drawers are particularly useful where storage space is limited.

They are ideal for:

- Hallways
- Townhouses
- Apartments
- Family homes
- Loft conversions
- Under-stair storage projects

Instead of leaving the staircase to perform only one function, it becomes part of the home's storage solution.

## Designed to Blend In

The beauty of riser drawers is that they don't interrupt the appearance of the staircase.

When closed, they look just like ordinary risers, preserving the clean lines of the staircase while concealing practical storage behind them.

The drawer fronts can be finished to match the staircase perfectly, whether in:

- Oak
- Walnut
- Mahogany
- Pine
- Painted finishes
- Modern matte colours

The result is storage that is almost impossible to notice.

## Clever Design Meets Everyday Practicality

A staircase is one of the largest pieces of furniture in many homes, so it makes sense to use every available space.

Hidden riser drawers are an excellent example of thoughtful design — combining clever engineering with everyday practicality. By using a slight rearward angle, concealed drawer runners, stronger tread construction, and multiple magnetic catches, the drawers remain secure during normal use while still being quick and convenient to access.

It's a simple idea that transforms unused space into valuable storage without compromising the beauty, strength, or functionality of the staircase.
