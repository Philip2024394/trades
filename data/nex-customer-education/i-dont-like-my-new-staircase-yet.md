# I Don't Like My New Staircase — Even Though It's Exactly What I Chose

If you've recently had your new staircase installed and your first thought is:

> *"I'm not happy with it."*
>
> or
>
> *"It doesn't look like I imagined."*

Please don't worry — you are far from alone.

This happens far more often than most people realise.

At NEX, we've seen many customers experience this feeling, especially within the first few hours or days after installation. It doesn't mean you've made the wrong choice, and it certainly doesn't mean your staircase cannot become exactly what you envisioned.

## Why Does This Happen?

When you choose a staircase, you're usually looking at beautiful finished photographs, professionally lit showrooms, or digital designs where every surrounding detail has already been completed.

Then installation day arrives. The old staircase disappears. The new one is fitted. The installers leave.

For the first time, you're standing alone looking at something completely different from what your home has had for years. Your mind immediately starts comparing reality with the picture you created in your imagination.

**This is completely normal.**

Our brains are naturally designed to analyse change. When something significant changes inside our home — especially something as large as a staircase — it can temporarily feel unfamiliar, even if it's exactly what we chose. Psychologists often refer to this as an *adjustment period*. Your mind simply needs time to replace the image of the old staircase with the new one.

## Remember — Your Staircase Isn't Finished Yet

One of the biggest misconceptions is believing that the installation is the finished product. In reality, it's often only the beginning.

Many staircases completely transform once the finishing touches are added. These can include:

- Painting surrounding walls
- Decorating the hallway
- Installing new flooring
- Adding carpets or runners
- Staining or painting the timber
- Changing handrails or balustrades
- Glass or metal upgrades
- LED lighting
- New skirting boards
- Feature lighting
- Wall artwork and décor

A staircase is part of the whole entrance to your home — not a standalone piece of furniture.

## Timber Is One of the Most Adaptable Materials

One of the greatest advantages of a timber staircase is its flexibility. Your staircase can evolve with your style over time. For example:

- Timber can be painted
- Timber can be stained darker or lighter
- Natural wood can be clear lacquered
- Steps can be carpeted
- Runners can be added
- Balustrades can be upgraded
- Glass panels can replace spindles
- Stainless steel can be introduced
- Existing treads can be refaced
- Complete design features can be modernised without replacing the whole staircase

There are usually many options available before considering replacing anything.

## Give Yourself Time

The first impression isn't always the lasting impression.

Many homeowners tell us that after a few weeks they couldn't imagine their home without their new staircase. As the rest of the room comes together, the staircase naturally becomes the feature they originally hoped for.

Sometimes the only thing that needed to change wasn't the staircase — it was giving yourself time to see it as part of your home.

## We're Here to Help

If you're feeling unsure about your staircase, don't keep that feeling to yourself. Tell us:

- What type of staircase you chose
- Which part you're unhappy with
- What you expected it to look like
- Whether you're considering paint, stain, carpet, or another finish
- Photos of your current staircase, if possible

The more information you can provide, the better we can help. At NEX, we can share visual examples of similar staircases finished in different ways, helping you see possibilities you may not have considered. Often, seeing the same staircase with different finishes is all it takes to reveal its true potential.

## Every Staircase Has Potential

There is no single staircase that cannot be enhanced through thoughtful finishing and design.

With the right combination of colours, materials, lighting, and finishing touches, almost every staircase can become a beautiful focal point within the home.

Your staircase isn't just what was installed today.

It's what it becomes over the coming weeks as your vision comes together.

Take a breath, give yourself time, and let us help you explore the possibilities. Together, we can help turn uncertainty into a staircase you'll be proud to come home to every day.
