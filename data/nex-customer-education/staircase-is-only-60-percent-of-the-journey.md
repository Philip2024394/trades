# Choosing the Right Staircase Is Only 60% of the Journey

One of the biggest misconceptions when designing a staircase is believing that the wood species or staircase style alone determines the final result.

A truth NEX design intelligence returns to often:

**Choosing your staircase is only around 60% of the finished project.**

The remaining 40% comes from how the staircase and the surrounding space are finished, dressed, and styled.

That is where an ordinary staircase becomes an extraordinary feature.

## The Staircase Is the Foundation

Whether the choice is:

- European Oak
- American Black Walnut
- Mahogany
- Painted Black
- Painted White
- Natural Timber
- Contemporary Stainless Steel
- Glass Balustrades

…it is the *foundation* of the design being selected.

It is the framework that everything else will build upon.

## The Remaining 40% Creates the Wow Factor

Once the staircase is installed, the real transformation begins.

Think about everything that surrounds it.

### Flooring

The flooring leading to the staircase plays a huge role in how the timber colour is perceived.

A walnut staircase on a light oak floor creates a completely different feel from the same staircase on polished concrete or dark porcelain tiles.

### Wall Colours

Paint colours can dramatically change the appearance of timber.

A soft white wall may make oak appear warmer.

A charcoal feature wall can make walnut look richer and more luxurious.

Even changing the wall colour months later can completely refresh the staircase.

### Lighting

Lighting is often overlooked.

Natural daylight, LED wall lights, pendant lighting, recessed tread lighting, and feature chandeliers all influence how timber grain, painted finishes, and metal details are seen.

Good lighting can completely transform the same staircase.

### Carpets and Stair Runners

Timber staircases don't have to remain fully exposed.

Many homeowners later choose to install:

- Luxury stair runners
- Full carpeting
- Contrasting carpet colours
- Decorative stair rods
- Premium woven finishes

Each creates a completely different character while keeping the original staircase.

### Balustrades

A staircase can evolve over time.

Many customers later upgrade to:

- Glass panels
- Black metal balusters
- Brushed stainless steel
- Timber spindles
- Contemporary cable systems

A balustrade alone can change the entire personality of the staircase.

### Decoration

The staircase does not exist on its own.

The hallway, entrance, and landing all contribute to the final impression.

Feature mirrors, artwork, plants, wall panelling, furniture, lighting, and accessories work together to create a welcoming and cohesive space.

## Timber Gives Endless Possibilities

One of the greatest advantages of a timber staircase is that it can change with the home.

Timber can be:

- Painted
- Repainted
- Stained darker
- Stained lighter
- Oiled
- Lacquered
- Re-faced
- Carpeted
- Combined with glass or metal
- Modernised years later

Unlike many building materials, timber offers incredible flexibility, allowing a staircase to evolve as taste and interior style change.

## Don't Judge the Staircase Too Early

Many people judge their staircase on the day it is installed.

In reality, that is only the beginning.

The decorating may not be finished.

The flooring may not be fitted.

The walls may still need painting.

The lighting may not yet be installed.

The furniture may not yet be in place.

A staircase often reaches its full potential only after the entire space has come together.

## The NEX Philosophy

NEX does not simply build staircases — NEX creates the foundation for a beautiful entrance to a home.

Choosing the right timber and staircase design is an important first step, but it is only part of the journey.

The real magic happens when every element around it works together: the colours, lighting, flooring, wall finishes, balustrades, and decorative details.

When those elements are considered as one complete design, the staircase becomes more than a way to move between floors — it becomes the architectural centrepiece of the home.

Because in the end, a truly exceptional staircase is not defined solely by the wood chosen. It is defined by how the entire space is finished, styled, and brought to life.
