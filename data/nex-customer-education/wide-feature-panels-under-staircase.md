# Wide Feature Panels Under Your Staircase – A Beautiful Alternative to Standard Storage

The area beneath a staircase doesn't always have to be filled with cupboards or left open. One of the most stylish and contemporary ideas is to install wide feature panels that transform the space into an architectural design element.

NEX design intelligence favours under-stair spaces that are both practical and visually appealing. Wide panels offer a clean, modern finish while allowing unique design features to be introduced that make a staircase truly stand out.

## Add Character with Circular Display Windows

A particularly eye-catching option is to incorporate small circular display windows into the feature panels.

These recessed openings create depth and visual interest while providing the perfect place to display treasured items.

They can be used to showcase:

- Books
- Family photographs
- Small ornaments
- Sculptures
- Indoor plants
- Collectables
- Decorative lighting
- Seasonal decorations

The circular shapes soften the straight lines of the staircase and create an elegant feature that immediately draws attention.

## A Modern Architectural Look

Wide wall panels provide a seamless appearance beneath the staircase, concealing any structural framework while creating a clean and sophisticated finish.

Panels can be produced in a variety of materials, including:

- Oak
- Walnut
- Mahogany
- Painted finishes
- Black matte panels
- White contemporary finishes
- Wood veneer
- Textured decorative panels

Matching the panels to the staircase creates a coordinated design that feels built into the home rather than added afterwards.

## Add Lighting for Extra Impact

To enhance the circular display windows, consider adding soft LED lighting inside each recess.

This creates a warm glow in the evening and highlights books, ornaments, or artwork, turning the under-stair area into a striking feature wall.

## A Design That Reflects Personality

Unlike standard under-stair storage, feature panels give the opportunity to personalise a home.

Whether the preference is a minimalist look with just a few carefully chosen ornaments or a display filled with treasured books and decorative pieces, the design can be tailored to suit any style.

## The NEX Approach

Every staircase is an opportunity to create something unique. Wide feature panels with circular display windows are an excellent way to transform unused space into a beautiful focal point that combines practicality with modern design.

Sometimes it's the smallest details that make the biggest impression. By adding bespoke display niches beneath a staircase, an ordinary area becomes a feature that adds warmth, personality, and lasting character to a home.
