# Staircase Side Panels — Geometric Walnut with Hidden Storage Door

This staircase demonstrates how side panels can transform the space beneath a staircase from an empty void into a beautiful architectural feature. Rather than leaving the underside open, the designer has created a series of carefully proportioned timber panels that complement the staircase and surrounding interior.

## Staircase Side Panel Design

The side panels beneath a staircase serve both a practical and decorative purpose. Structurally, they conceal the framework beneath the stairs and can provide access to useful storage. Visually, they create a clean, finished appearance that makes the staircase feel like a built-in piece of furniture rather than a standalone structure.

In this design, the staircase is enclosed with solid walnut side panels, perfectly matched to the staircase, newel posts, balusters, handrail and flooring. The consistent timber finish creates a seamless appearance throughout the hallway.

## Geometric Panel Layout

One of the most attractive features is the use of multiple panel orientations.

- Vertical tongue-and-groove style boards at the front create height.
- Horizontal panels across the lower section add width and balance.
- Angled panels follow the pitch of the staircase.
- The different directions naturally divide the large area into smaller, well-proportioned sections.

This geometric layout gives the staircase visual interest while maintaining a clean and uncluttered appearance.

## Hidden Storage Door

The central panel has been designed as a concealed access door.

Instead of installing a visible cupboard beneath the stairs, the door is integrated into the panel design so it almost disappears. This creates valuable storage for household items while preserving the elegant appearance of the staircase.

The hidden storage can be used for:

- Cleaning equipment
- Vacuum cleaners
- Shoes
- Coats
- Children's toys
- Seasonal decorations
- General household storage

## Matching Materials

A successful staircase design relies on consistency.

In this example, the following components all share the same walnut finish:

- Handrail
- Newel posts
- Balusters
- String
- Side panels
- Storage door
- Flooring
- Internal doors

Using one continuous timber species allows the staircase to become the focal point of the hallway without appearing busy.

## Architectural Balance

The side panels also help balance the visual weight of the staircase.

Without them, the underside would appear hollow and unfinished. The enclosed design gives the staircase a stronger architectural presence, making it resemble bespoke cabinetry rather than a simple flight of stairs.

The large square newel post at the bottom anchors the composition, while the panel layout directs the eye naturally along the line of the staircase.

## Modern Yet Traditional

Although the staircase uses traditional materials and craftsmanship, the panel design feels contemporary because it avoids excessive mouldings or ornamentation.

The clean lines make it suitable for:

- Modern homes
- Contemporary farmhouse interiors
- Luxury family homes
- Traditional properties with updated interiors
- High-end bespoke staircase projects

## Advantages of Staircase Side Panels

Well-designed side panels offer several benefits:

- Hide the structural framework beneath the stairs.
- Create useful under-stair storage.
- Improve the appearance of the hallway.
- Increase the feeling of quality and craftsmanship.
- Match surrounding doors, flooring and joinery.
- Turn unused space into practical storage.
- Make the staircase appear as bespoke fitted furniture.

This style of side panel demonstrates that the area beneath a staircase should not be overlooked. With thoughtful panel design, hidden storage, and matching timber finishes, the side of the staircase becomes an important design feature that enhances both the functionality and elegance of the home.
