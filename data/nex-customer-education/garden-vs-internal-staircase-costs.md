# Garden Staircase vs Internal Staircase — Cost Comparison

In most cases, garden staircases are less expensive than internal house staircases, but it depends on the design and materials.

Here's a general comparison:

| Staircase Type | Typical Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Simple timber garden staircase | ⭐ Lowest | Basic construction using pressure-treated timber and decking boards. |
| Composite garden staircase | Medium | Composite decking costs more but requires less maintenance. |
| Standard internal softwood staircase | Medium | Requires precision joinery, building regulation compliance, and interior-quality finishes. |
| Oak or hardwood internal staircase | High | Premium timber, detailed craftsmanship, and higher-quality finishing. |
| Bespoke feature staircase (glass, floating, steel, curved) | ⭐ Highest | Complex engineering, custom fabrication, and specialist installation. |

## Why Are Garden Staircases Usually Cheaper?

Garden staircases are generally built using structural construction methods rather than fine furniture-grade joinery.

They often use:

- Pressure-treated timber.
- Standard decking boards.
- Exterior-grade screws and brackets.
- Simple handrails and posts.

The focus is on strength, weather resistance, and practicality rather than decorative detail.

## Why Are Internal Staircases More Expensive?

An indoor staircase is often one of the most prominent features of a home and usually requires:

- High-quality joinery.
- Furniture-grade timber.
- Precise manufacturing.
- Decorative balustrades.
- Finished handrails.
- Staining, painting, or lacquering.
- Compliance with building regulations for internal stairs.

Many internal staircases are custom-made to fit the exact dimensions of the property, which also increases the cost.

## When Can a Garden Staircase Cost More?

A garden staircase can become more expensive if it includes features such as:

- Hardwood construction.
- Composite decking.
- Glass balustrades.
- Stainless steel railings.
- Curved or multi-level designs.
- Integrated LED lighting.
- Large landings.
- Extensive landscaping.

In these cases, an outdoor staircase may cost as much as — or even more than — a basic internal staircase.

## Which Offers Better Value?

If you're simply looking for access between different garden levels, a pressure-treated timber garden staircase is usually the most economical option.

If you're investing in the appearance and value of your home, an internal staircase often becomes a long-term feature that can significantly influence the look and feel of your interior.

Both are worthwhile investments, but they are built for different purposes: garden staircases prioritise durability and weather resistance, while internal staircases prioritise craftsmanship, comfort, and interior design.
