# Can Woodworm Spread in a Staircase?

Yes, woodworm can spread in a staircase, but it depends on whether the infestation is active and what condition the timber is in.

A staircase can be a good place for woodworm because it is made from solid timber, often with hidden areas such as:

- Underneath steps.
- Inside stringers (side boards).
- Behind risers.
- Around newel posts.
- Inside handrails and balusters.

## How Woodworm Spreads in a Staircase

**Adult beetles emerge from existing timber.**

They leave small round holes (often 1–2 mm for the common furniture beetle). They fly and can lay eggs on nearby untreated wood.

**Larvae move inside the wood.**

The "worm" stage stays hidden inside the timber. It does not crawl around the house like a normal worm.

**Nearby timber can become affected.**

If conditions are suitable, they may attack:

- Other staircase parts.
- Wooden flooring.
- Beams.
- Furniture.

## Will It Destroy Your Staircase Quickly?

Usually no.

Woodworm is a slow process. A staircase may take years before serious weakening occurs. The biggest risk is when:

- The timber is old.
- The wood has moisture.
- There is an active infestation for many years.

Hardwoods such as oak, walnut, mahogany, and teak are generally more resistant than softwoods like pine, but they are not immune.

## Signs Your Staircase May Still Have Active Woodworm

Check for:

- ✅ Fresh pale powder (frass) under holes.
- ✅ New holes appearing each year.
- ✅ Timber becoming soft when pressed with a screwdriver.
- ✅ Crumbling edges around holes.
- ✅ Small beetles appearing indoors (usually spring or summer).

## Signs It May Be Old or Inactive

- ✅ Dark holes with no fresh dust.
- ✅ Holes filled with dirt, polish, or old finish.
- ✅ No new activity for several years.

## What You Can Do Now

**Do not just fill the holes yet.**

Filling hides the evidence and makes checking activity harder.

**Vacuum any dust below the staircase.**

Then monitor for new powder.

**Check moisture.**

Woodworm loves damp timber. A dry staircase is much less attractive.

**Treat if active.**

A professional woodworm treatment can penetrate the timber surface. Severely damaged pieces may need replacement.

## A Note on New Hardwood Staircases

If you have small holes in a new teak / walnut / mahogany staircase, it is worth checking carefully because sometimes the holes are from old timber before the staircase was made, not a new infestation.

A close photo of the holes and any wood dust underneath, combined with the questions in the "What to Do If You See Woodworm Holes" article, is usually enough for a specialist to judge whether the activity looks recent or historical.
