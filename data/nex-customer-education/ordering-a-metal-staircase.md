# Ordering a Metal Staircase – The 18-Point Information Checklist

Ordering a metal staircase requires accurate information to ensure the staircase is manufactured correctly, complies with building regulations, and fits perfectly within the available space. Whether the staircase is for a residential property, commercial building, or industrial project, the following information should be provided before manufacturing begins.

## 1. Staircase Type

Select the required staircase layout.

Examples include:

- Straight Staircase
- Quarter Turn Staircase
- Dog Leg Staircase
- Half Turn Staircase
- Winder Staircase
- Double Winder Staircase
- Open Well Staircase
- Spiral Staircase
- Helical Staircase
- Floating Staircase
- Cantilever Staircase

## 2. Floor-to-Floor Height

Provide the exact finished floor level to finished floor level.

This measurement determines:

- Number of risers.
- Individual rise height.
- Stair pitch.
- Compliance with building regulations.

## 3. Stair Width

Specify the finished staircase width.

Common sizes include:

- 800 mm
- 900 mm
- 1000 mm
- 1100 mm
- 1200 mm
- Custom widths

## 4. Available Space

Provide the installation space.

Measurements required:

- Overall length.
- Overall width.
- Ceiling height.
- Headroom.
- Wall positions.
- Door locations.
- Windows.
- Structural beams.

A floor plan is recommended.

## 5. Direction of Travel

Indicate:

- Left Hand Turn.
- Right Hand Turn.

Also specify whether the staircase travels:

- Up from left to right.
- Up from right to left.

## 6. Structural Design

Choose the staircase construction.

Examples:

- Mono Stringer.
- Twin Stringer.
- Box Stringer.
- Folded Plate.
- Central Spine.
- Side Plate.
- Cantilever.
- Steel Frame with Timber Treads.

## 7. Material

Select the metal type.

Common options:

- Mild Steel.
- Stainless Steel 304.
- Stainless Steel 316.
- Galvanised Steel.
- Powder-Coated Steel.
- Structural Box Section.
- Laser Cut Steel Plate.

## 8. Finish

Available finishes include:

- Powder Coated.
- Primed.
- Galvanised.
- Zinc Coated.
- Satin Stainless.
- Brushed Stainless.
- Mirror Polish.
- Raw Steel.
- Custom RAL Colour.

## 9. Treads

Choose the tread material.

Examples:

- Oak.
- American White Oak.
- American Black Walnut.
- Ash.
- Beech.
- Glass.
- Steel Plate.
- Stone.
- Porcelain.
- Composite.
- Open Steel Grating.

Specify:

- Thickness.
- Finish.
- Colour.
- Anti-slip requirements.

## 10. Balustrade

Select the balustrade system.

Options include:

- Glass Panels.
- Frameless Glass.
- Stainless Steel Posts.
- Steel Balusters.
- Timber Balusters.
- Horizontal Bars.
- Vertical Bars.
- Cable System.
- No Balustrade.

## 11. Handrail

Choose:

- Oak.
- Walnut.
- Ash.
- Stainless Steel.
- Mild Steel.
- Powder Coated Steel.
- Glass Mounted Handrail.

## 12. Installation Method

Specify:

- Bolted together on site.
- Fully welded.
- Modular sections.
- Flat packed.
- Crane lift required.

## 13. Building Regulations

Confirm compliance with local building regulations.

Typical considerations include:

- Maximum rise.
- Minimum going.
- Stair pitch.
- Headroom.
- Balustrade height.
- Gap spacing.
- Handrail requirements.
- Loading requirements.

## 14. Installation Location

State whether the staircase is:

- Internal.
- External.
- Residential.
- Commercial.
- Industrial.
- Public Building.
- Fire Escape.

## 15. Drawings

Provide:

- Floor plans.
- Elevations.
- Section drawings.
- Structural drawings (if available).
- Photographs of the installation area.
- BIM or CAD files (if available).

## 16. Manufacturing Information

The manufacturer may require:

- Fabrication tolerances.
- Weld finish specification.
- Connection details.
- Fixing positions.
- Base plate sizes.
- Anchor bolt locations.
- Load calculations.
- Engineering approval.

## 17. Delivery Information

Provide:

- Delivery address.
- Site access restrictions.
- Vehicle size limitations.
- Crane availability.
- Forklift availability.
- Preferred delivery date.

## 18. Optional Extras

Additional options may include:

- LED tread lighting.
- Glass floor panels.
- Hidden fixings.
- Acoustic isolation pads.
- Fire-rated coatings.
- Anti-slip inserts.
- Feature newel posts.
- Custom laser-cut designs.
- Timber cladding to steel stringers.
