# Closed String Open Riser Staircases – A Modern Design That Opens Up Your Home

A closed string open riser staircase combines the structural strength of a traditional closed-string staircase with the clean, contemporary appearance of open risers. It has become an increasingly popular choice in modern homes because it creates a lighter, more spacious feel while maintaining the elegance of timber construction.

Unlike a fully enclosed staircase, the space between each tread is left open, allowing light and views to pass through the staircase.

## What Is a Closed String Open Riser Staircase?

In this design, the treads are housed inside solid side strings, creating the appearance of a traditional staircase when viewed from the side.

However, instead of fitting solid risers between each tread, the gaps are left open. This produces a modern floating effect while retaining the strength and clean lines of a closed-string construction.

The result is a staircase that feels lighter without compromising on craftsmanship.

## A Great Choice for Darker Hallways

One of the biggest advantages of an open riser staircase is the amount of natural light it allows through.

Traditional closed risers can create a visual barrier that blocks daylight from reaching areas beneath or behind the staircase.

By removing the risers, light can pass through each step, helping to:

- Brighten entrance halls.
- Make narrow hallways feel larger.
- Improve the feeling of openness.
- Allow natural daylight to travel between rooms.
- Create a more contemporary living space.

This simple design choice can completely change the atmosphere of a home.

## Safety Always Comes First

While open risers create a beautiful modern appearance, they are not suitable for every staircase.

One important consideration is headroom beneath the staircase.

If people, particularly young children, are able to walk underneath the staircase, the open risers must be carefully assessed to ensure the design complies with the relevant building regulations for your location. Safety requirements vary by country, but they are intended to reduce the risk of children passing through openings or striking their heads beneath the flight.

For this reason, the lower part of the staircase often remains enclosed until the underside is above normal head height. From that point onwards, the staircase can transition into open risers, creating the desired visual effect while maintaining a safer design.

Always check your local building regulations or consult a qualified staircase designer before choosing an open-riser design.

## A Modern Architectural Feature

Open riser staircases work particularly well in:

- Contemporary homes
- Open-plan living spaces
- Hallways with limited natural light
- Modern extensions
- Minimalist interiors
- Timber and glass staircase designs

They pair beautifully with timber, glass balustrades, stainless steel, and black metal details.

## Design Flexibility

A closed string open riser staircase can be manufactured in a wide range of finishes, including:

- European Oak
- American Black Walnut
- Mahogany
- Pine
- Painted timber
- Black matte finishes
- Glass balustrades
- Stainless steel balusters
- Contemporary timber handrails

This allows the staircase to complement both traditional and modern interiors.

## A Staircase That Feels Light and Spacious

An open riser staircase is about more than appearance. It changes how a space feels by allowing light, views, and openness to flow naturally through the home.

When carefully designed with safety, proportion, and the surrounding architecture in mind, a closed string open riser staircase becomes both a functional feature and a striking piece of modern design that can make even compact hallways feel brighter, larger, and more welcoming.
