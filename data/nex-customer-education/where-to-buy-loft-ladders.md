# Where to Buy Loft Ladders

Loft ladders are a standard building supply. When a homeowner asks where to buy one, there are two straightforward routes:

- **NEX Centre** — the online marketplace for staircase and staircase-related products across the NEX network.
- **Local building merchants** — every UK builders' merchant stocks loft ladders as a shelf line, usually on pallets in the trade area of the shop.

## What a Building Merchant Typically Stocks

- Aluminium concertina loft ladders (the most common domestic model).
- Timber sliding loft ladders (traditional, quieter, heavier).
- Fixed timber loft stairs (a step up from a ladder — closer to a small staircase).
- Fire-rated hatches to pair with the ladder.
- Handrails and safety-rail add-ons.

Stock varies by branch and by season — always call ahead if a specific model is needed on the day.

## When a Loft Ladder Is the Right Choice vs a Full Staircase

A loft ladder suits a loft that is used **occasionally** — seasonal storage, minor access, a hobby space visited weekly rather than daily. A loft that becomes a **habitable room** — bedroom, home office, family space — usually requires a proper compliant fixed staircase per UK Building Regulations Approved Document K, not a loft ladder.

## What NEX Design Intelligence Recommends

For occasional loft access, a stock loft ladder from a local builders' merchant is usually the fastest and most economical solution. For anything closer to daily use, or where the loft will be converted into a room, NEX design intelligence recommends specifying a fixed staircase from the outset — the loft ladder rarely satisfies long-term needs and often has to be replaced anyway.

Where uncertainty exists about which category the loft falls into, NEX can help match the answer to the intended use before any purchase is made.
