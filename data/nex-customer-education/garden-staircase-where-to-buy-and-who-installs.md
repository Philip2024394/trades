# Where to Buy Garden Staircase Materials and Who Installs Them

Yes, there are many businesses that can supply the materials, but most garden centres do not build or install garden staircases themselves. Installation is usually carried out by specialist tradespeople.

Typically, you'll find three options:

## 1. Landscaping Companies ⭐ (Most Popular)

Professional landscapers regularly build:

- Garden staircases
- Decking
- Raised patios
- Balustrades
- Timber retaining walls
- Garden structures

They can usually design, supply, and install the complete project.

## 2. Decking Specialists

Many decking companies also build:

- Timber garden stairs
- Composite garden stairs
- Handrails and balustrades
- Multi-level decking
- Garden landings

If you're adding or replacing decking, these specialists are often the best choice because they can ensure the staircase matches the decking perfectly.

## 3. Timber and Builders' Merchants

These businesses generally supply the materials but don't always install them.

They stock items such as:

- Structural timber
- Decking boards
- Stair stringers
- Handrails
- Balusters
- Posts
- Stainless steel fixings
- Concrete and post supports

Many can also recommend local installers.

## Can Garden Centres Help?

Some larger garden centres sell decking materials, sleepers, fencing, and outdoor timber products. A few may also offer installation services through approved contractors, but this is less common than using a landscaping or decking company.

Garden centres are generally a good place for inspiration and to purchase complementary items such as:

- Plants
- Planters
- Outdoor lighting
- Decorative stone
- Garden furniture
- Pergolas

The structural staircase itself is more often supplied through specialist timber merchants or decking suppliers.

## Should I Buy the Materials Myself?

You have two choices:

### Option 1 – Installer Supplies Everything

Advantages:

- One company is responsible for the materials and workmanship.
- Materials are ordered in the correct quantities.
- Any issues are usually easier to resolve.

### Option 2 – You Purchase the Materials

Advantages:

- You choose the exact products and finishes.
- You may be able to compare prices from different suppliers.

Before buying, it's a good idea to ask the installer whether they are happy to work with customer-supplied materials. Some prefer to use products they know and trust, while others are happy to install materials you've selected.

## Questions to Ask Before Ordering

Before you buy anything, ask:

- Is the timber suitable for outdoor structural use?
- Are the decking boards slip resistant?
- What type of fixings should be used?
- Does the timber need sealing or treatment after cutting?
- Is the handrail system designed for outdoor use?
- What maintenance is recommended?
- Does the supplier provide a warranty on the materials?

## The Best Approach

For most homeowners, the simplest route is to choose a landscaping or decking specialist who can design, supply, and install the entire staircase. This helps ensure that the structure, handrails, decking, and fixings all work together and are suitable for outdoor use.

If you enjoy planning your own project, visiting garden centres and timber merchants can be an excellent way to compare materials and gather ideas before speaking with an installer.
