## Executive Summary

**US-vs-UK divergences (highest impact):** US says **stringer**, UK says **string**. US **railing / guardrail** covers what UK calls **balustrade**. US uses **baluster / spindle / picket** interchangeably (regional), UK uses **spindle** most commonly. US **skirt board** ≈ UK **wall string** but is explicitly non-structural finish trim. US **cut stringer** ≈ UK **cut string**, but US also uses **open stringer** and **sawtooth stringer** for the same thing. **Cable railing** is a huge US-specific product category with essentially no UK equivalent volume.

**US-vs-Canada divergences:** Terminology ~95% aligned. Canada's NBC uses **run** where US-IRC uses **tread depth**. Canada separates **guard** (fall protection) from **handrail** (graspable) more strictly in code language. Canada retains British spelling **"balustrade"** more frequently. Canadian dimensions are metric-first vs US imperial-first.

**IBC 2024 anchor terms (all four must appear in taxonomy):**
- **Curved stairway** (IBC 1011.9) — inside radius ≥ 2× minimum stair width
- **Spiral stairway** (IBC 1011.10) — central column; 6-3/4" min tread at 12" from narrow edge
- **Alternating-tread device** (IBC 1011.14) — 5" tread depth min, 20 ft max rise, handrails both sides
- **Ship's ladder** (IBC 1011.15) — 5" tread depth min, restricted egress use

- **Cut stringer / open stringer / sawtooth stringer** — UK "cut string / open string"
- **Wall stringer** — UK "wall string"

### Layer F · Stringer / Edge
Housed stringer · Cut stringer · Open stringer · Wall stringer · **Skirt board / skirtboard** (US: finish trim, NON-structural — critical distinction) · Exposed tread end / return end / mitered return · Fascia stringer.

### Layer G · Balustrade / Guard / Railing
- Whole assembly: **Railing / Guardrail** (US universal) · **Guard** (Canada preferred) · **Balustrade** (high-end US residential)
- Vertical: **Baluster** (technical), **Spindle** (informal), **Picket** (Southern US regional; primary meaning = fence)
- Materials: Iron/steel · Glass panel · Cable infill (huge US category) · Wood
- Top rail · Base rail / shoe rail · Newel post

### Layer H · Handrail
- Wall-mounted · Balustrade-mounted · Continuous · Bending
- Fittings: **Volute** · **Turnout** · **Easing** (up/over) · **Gooseneck** · **Rosette** · **Return** (ADA)
- **ADA-compliant** (ADA 505: 34–38" height, 1.25–2" grip)
- **IRC Type I** (circular 1.25–2" OR perimeter 4–6.25", max 2.25" cross-section)
- **IRC Type II** (non-circular perimeter >6.25" with finger recess)

### Layer I · Newel Posts
Starting newel (bottom) · Angle newel (turn) · Landing newel · Balcony newel · Box newel · Turned newel · **Post-to-post** vs **Over-the-post** systems · Newel cap · Finial.

### Layer J · Landing
Intermediate · Quarter (90°) · Half (180°, switchback) · Top · Bottom · Floor landing · Split landing.

### Layer K · Specialist (IBC anchors)
Spiral stairway (1011.10) · Curved stairway (1011.9) · Alternating-tread device (1011.14) · Ship's ladder (1011.15) · Monumental (industry, not code) · Floating (marketing) · Cantilever · Sculptural · Exterior/deck (IRC R318.7).

## Ambiguity Flags (9)

1. **"Curved" vs "circular" stair** — IBC uses "curved" (1011.9); industry uses both. needs_review — prefer "curved" as anchor.
2. **"Dog-leg"** — US usage varies by region; UK consistently 180°. needs_review per region.
3. **"Floating stair"** — Marketing term with 3+ possible constructions (mono-stringer, dual-stringer with concealed structure, true cantilever). Not code-defined.
4. **"Skirt board" vs "stringer board"** — Some US sources call the wall-side finish trim "stringer board," confusing with structural stringer.
5. **"Baluster" vs "spindle" vs "picket"** — Interchangeable in most US; picket is Southern US regionalism.
6. **"Railing" vs "balustrade"** — Both used in US; balustrade more traditional / high-end. Canada uses balustrade more.
7. **NBC "run" vs IRC "tread depth"** — Same measurement, different word.
8. **"Mono stringer" vs "central stringer" vs "spine stair"** — All refer to single central beam; usage varies.
9. **IRC section renumbering** — IRC 2024 reorganised Chapter 3, renumbering stairways from R311.7 to R318.7.
