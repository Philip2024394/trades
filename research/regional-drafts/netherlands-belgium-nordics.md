## Executive Summary

Six countries, seven vocabularies (Belgium = Dutch/Flemish + French). Key findings:

- **Nordic spiral/helical is a genuine false-friend minefield.** In **Danish** and **Norwegian**, `spindeltrappe/spindeltrapp` (central pole) and `vindeltrappe/vindeltrapp` (open well, two curved stringers) are **distinct types**. In **Swedish**, `spindeltrappa` and `vindeltrappa` are treated as **synonyms of `spiraltrappa`** (Swedish Academy dictionary confirms). Do NOT map DK/NO `vindeltrappe` to SE `vindeltrappa` as equivalents — the SE term is a general synonym, the DK/NO term is a specific structural type. Norwegian `spiraltrapp` is an **umbrella term** covering both `spindeltrapp` and `vindeltrapp` (Store norske leksikon, authoritative).
- **Netherlands has parallel spiral confusion.** `Spiltrap` = central pole. `Wenteltrap` = open well, curved inner/outer stringer. `Spiraaltrap` = general curved-form stair, often used loosely.
- **Belgium — bilingual coverage is real.** Manufacturers (EE Trappen, Trappen Verschaeve, Bobex) publish both NL/Flemish and FR terminology in parallel. NBN B 03-004:2017 anchors safety/geometry.
- **NL vs BE Dutch has small but real deltas.** BE Dutch prefers `kwartdraaitrap` (with the -draai- infix); NL Dutch uses `kwart-trap` / `kwartslag trap`. Both current; BE more consistent about the compound form.
- **Finland is a distinct language family** (Uralic, not Germanic). Terminology is compact and descriptive.

## Nordic Spiral / Helical / Winder Distinction Map

**Single highest-risk area for machine translation. Handle explicitly.**

| Concept | SE | NO | DK | FI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Umbrella "spiral-form stair" | spiraltrappa (= vindel-, spindel-) | spiraltrapp (umbrella per SNL) | (none; specify spindel- OR vindel-) | kierreporras |
| Spiral WITH central load-bearing pole | (all three used; specify "med spindel") | spindeltrapp (distinct) | spindeltrappe (distinct) | kierreporras keskitolpalla |
| Spiral WITH open central well, curved stringers | (as above; specify "utan spindel") | vindeltrapp (distinct) | vindeltrappe (distinct) | kierreporras avoin |
| Sweeping curved (not corkscrew) | svängd / böjd trappa | buet trapp | buet trappe | kaareva / pyöreä porras |
| L-shape (90°) | vinkeltrappa / L-trappa | kvartsvingt trapp | kvartsvingstrappe | L-porras |
| U-shape (180°) | U-trappa | halvsvingt trapp | halvsvingstrappe | U-porras |

**Critical false friends:**
- **SE vindeltrappa ≠ DK vindeltrappe** structurally. SE = general spiral synonym. DK = specifically excludes central pole.
- **NO spiraltrapp** is an umbrella per SNL. Do NOT translate directly to DK `spindel-` or `vindel-`.
- **DE Spindeltreppe** semantically matches DK `spindeltrappe` (central pole) — safer bridge than SE `spindeltrappa`.
- **NL wenteltrap** semantically matches DK `vindeltrappe` (open well). NL `spiltrap` matches DK `spindeltrappe` and NO `spindeltrapp`.
- **FR hélicoïdal vs colimaçon** — same open-well vs central-pole distinction; inconsistent usage even in professional French sources.

## Ambiguity Flags (9)

1. **NL vlindertrap** — retail usage, no consistent authoritative definition. Treat as marketing.
2. **SE sneslittrappa** — no authoritative source. Suggest excluding until source verifies.
3. **SE spiral synonyms** — SE Academy dictionary treats spiraltrappa / vindeltrappa / spindeltrappa as synonyms. Use qualifiers ("med spindel" / "utan spindel") to disambiguate structural type.
4. **NL spiraaltrap vs wenteltrap vs spiltrap** — colloquially interchangeable; professionally distinct. Preserve distinction.
5. **FR hélicoïdal vs colimaçon** — professionally distinct; casually interchangeable. Store both.
6. **NO svingtrapp** = umbrella turning stair; do NOT treat as specific geometry.
7. **Nordic floating-stair vocabulary** overlaps between structural (cantilever) and visual (floating) meanings.
8. **FI stringer terminology** less strongly codified than Germanic languages. Medium confidence.
9. **NL boomtrap** — under-attested as specific type; treat as informal.
