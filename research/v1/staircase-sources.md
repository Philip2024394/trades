# Staircase Research · Sources Bibliography

**Version:** v1 · 2026-08-17
**Rule:** every claim in the taxonomy is anchored to a source tier. No claim without a citation. Every source is grouped by country and by tier.
**Tiers:**
- **Tier 1 — Building codes & regulatory standards** (highest authority)
- **Tier 2 — Industry standards bodies & trade associations**
- **Tier 3 — Educational / textbook / academic sources**
- **Tier 4 — Established manufacturers & specialists**
- **Tier 5 — General industry publications & dictionaries**

---

## UK

### Tier 1 — Regulatory
- **Approved Document K** — Protection from falling, collision and impact (2013 edition, with 2024 amendments), Ministry of Housing / DLUHC.
- **Building Regulations 2010 · Schedule 1 · Part K.**
- **BS 5395-1:2010** — Stairs. Code of practice for the design of stairs with straight flights and winders.
- **BS 5395-2:1984** — Stairs. Code of practice for the design of helical and spiral stairs.
- **BS 5395-3:1985** — Stairs. Code of practice for the design of industrial type stairs, permanent ladders and walkways.
- **BS 6180:2011** — Barriers in and about buildings. Code of practice.
- **BS EN 14076:2013** — Timber stairs. Terminology.

### Tier 2 — Industry
- **BWF Stair Scheme (British Woodworking Federation)** — Design Guides, member technical bulletins.
- **British Standards Institution (BSI) publications.**
- **CIRIA Guides** — commercial stair design.

### Tier 4 — Established manufacturers (reference for trade vocabulary use)
- Stairbox, JELD-WEN, Cheshire Mouldings, Richard Burbidge, George Quinn Stair Parts, Bespoke Stair Solutions, TK Stairs.

### Tier 5 — Trade publications
- Professional Builder magazine, Housebuilder & Developer.

## Provenance & integrity notes

- **UK-centric literature was NOT treated as authoritative baseline.** UK sources (BS, AD K) were treated as one country's inputs alongside the other 17.
- **False-friend traps documented in each regional draft** were cross-checked against at least 2 sources per pair before entering the compatibility file.
- **Where a single authoritative source dominates a country** (e.g., DIN 18065 for DE, RT 88-10129 for FI), it is cited by section/clause where possible.
- **Manufacturer sites (Tier 4)** are used as evidence of trade vocabulary in use — never as authority for definitions.
- **Wikipedia and general dictionaries (Tier 5)** are used only as corroborative sources, never sole citation.
- Every UNKNOWN / weak-evidence / needs_review flag is listed in `research-gaps.md`.
