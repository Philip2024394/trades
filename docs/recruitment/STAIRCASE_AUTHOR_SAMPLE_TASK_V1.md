# Staircase Author Sample Authoring Task — Stage 3

**Draft V1 · 2026-07-24**
**Purpose:** the actual task issued to Stage-3 shortlisted candidates. £300 paid on submission regardless of hire outcome. Tests real authoring ability against the Brain schema — the strongest single filter in the process.

---

## Overview

Each shortlisted candidate is offered a choice of 3 sample topics. They pick one, deliver within 10 working days. £300 paid on receipt regardless of decision. Nex retains the work for evaluation only — the applicant retains full IP unless subsequently hired (in which case the work-for-hire terms activate on signing).

## Ground rules given to the candidate

1. **Word count:** 800 – 1,200 words
2. **Format:** a Google Doc or Word file · plain prose with sub-headings · no images required (but permitted if they'd help)
3. **Sources:** cite every non-obvious claim (regulations, standards, industry data). Personal experience should be labelled as such ("in my experience over X years…")
4. **Voice:** how you would explain this to a competent joiner who hasn't specialised in staircases — direct, working-level, no marketing language
5. **Attribution:** put your name and credentials at the top. Nex will not publish this work — it is for evaluation only.
6. **Time budget:** we expect this to take a competent author 4-6 hours. If it takes significantly more, that's a signal about pace we should discuss before hire.

## Topic choice (candidate picks one of three)

### Option A — "How to first-fit a cut-string staircase in a domestic property"
Cover: measuring the opening · specifying rise + going within Approved Doc K limits · marking out the strings · assembly sequence · fitting to trimmer · winder positioning if applicable · when to install balustrades vs after decorating · common site-fitting mistakes and how to avoid them.

### Option B — "Choosing between softwood, hardwood and engineered timber for a domestic staircase"
Cover: performance comparison (durability, dimensional stability, cost tier) · regulatory implications (fire, structural loading) · finish compatibility · matching to intended use (heavy family use vs occasional access to loft) · long-term maintenance considerations · when NOT to use each. **No £ figures** — describe cost as "tier" (entry, mid, premium) so the piece isn't tied to a moment in time.

### Option C — "Understanding Approved Document K for domestic staircases"
Cover: which stair categories apply to which situations · rise/going/pitch limits · winder rules · alternating tread (space-saver) restrictions · headroom and landings · guarding (balustrade) requirements including the 100mm sphere rule · handrail heights · how the private/utility/general access categories differ · what changes if the property is not a single dwelling.

## Evaluation criteria (share with candidate on assignment)

Every submission will be evaluated on the same 6 axes as the portfolio (see PORTFOLIO_RUBRIC_V1). Two Nex reviewers score independently.

Additional Stage-3 criteria:
- **Regulatory currency** — cites the current Approved Doc version, not superseded editions
- **Structure suitability** — text can be broken into discrete verifiable claims (this is a proxy for how the AI extraction pipeline will handle their work)
- **Honesty about uncertainty** — where they don't know, they say so. Applicants who present opinions as facts are a poor fit for the Brain.
- **Voice fit** — does it sound like a real UK joiner talking, or a generic content mill?

## Reviewer note-taking template

For each submission, both reviewers note:
- 3 things this candidate does better than a generalist
- 3 things this candidate does worse than we'd expect from a master
- 1 sentence: would you personally trust this person to be the named authority on every staircase answer Nex gives for the next 2 years? Why / why not?

## What we're actually testing at this stage

- **Real authoring ability** — the CV said 15 years, the interview said the right words, this shows whether they can actually structure knowledge for capture into the Brain
- **Judgement under a bounded task** — 800-1200 words is small; how they choose what to include and what to leave out is diagnostic
- **Working relationship signal** — do they ask thoughtful questions before starting? Do they deliver on time? Do they invoice cleanly? All of these predict the 2-year working relationship

## Post-submission

- Both reviewers score within 5 working days
- Combined score + notes go to CEO
- Candidate feedback given within 2 weeks — successful candidates advance to Stage 4 (contract); unsuccessful candidates get respectful written feedback (their reputation carries, so does ours)

## Retention of sample tasks

- Successful candidates: work rights transfer to Nex on contract signing under work-for-hire terms
- Unsuccessful candidates: Nex deletes all copies within 30 days · confirms deletion in writing to applicant
