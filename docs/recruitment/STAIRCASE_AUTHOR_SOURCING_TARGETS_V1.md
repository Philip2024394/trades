# Staircase Author Sourcing Targets — UK-specific

**Draft V1 · 2026-07-24**
**Purpose:** the concrete list of UK organisations, publications and channels to contact when posting the Staircase Author role. Every entry below is a real body; verify current contact addresses before sending (personnel change).

---

## Tier 1 — Trade bodies most likely to yield qualified candidates

| Body | Why | Website | Suggested channel |
|------|-----|---------|-------------------|
| **British Woodworking Federation (BWF)** | Represents joinery manufacturers including stair specialists. Members work at V1-Author qualification level daily. | bwf.org.uk | Contact membership team → request forwarding to Stair Scheme members. BWF Stair Scheme certifies stair manufacturers to a quality standard. |
| **Institute of Carpenters (IOC)** | Chartered body for carpenters and joiners. Fellows and Members hold recognised expertise levels. | instituteofcarpenters.com | Contact secretariat → offer to send job posting to Fellows and Master Members. Consider paid advert in members' newsletter. |
| **Guild of Master Craftsmen** | Vets and lists master-grade tradespeople across UK. Search filter for joinery/stair-related trades. | guildmc.com | Contact head office. Their trade magazine (Craftsman) accepts recruitment adverts. |
| **Federation of Master Builders (FMB)** | Broader construction body but member directory searchable by trade. FMB Members work to Master standards. | fmb.org.uk | Post to FMB Opportunities board. Contact regional offices for stair-specialist member referrals. |
| **CITB (Construction Industry Training Board)** | Sets training standards. Instructors and moderators are typically senior tradespeople. | citb.co.uk | Contact awarding body relations to reach Level 3 Bench Joinery internal verifiers and instructors. |

## Tier 4 — Specialist adjacent sources

- **BM TRADA** (trada.co.uk) — technical timber body. Their members and consultants include senior joinery professionals who write technical notes. Contact their publications team.
- **The Wood Awards** (woodawards.com) — annual UK design awards for wood construction. Past finalists and judges include masterful stair designers. Publish list is public.
- **Heritage Craft Association** (heritagecrafts.org.uk) — critically endangered crafts register includes traditional stair techniques (helical timber stairs, kite-winder crafts). Their contact list for traditional-craft practitioners.
- **National Historic Ships UK** — refers historic ship joinery specialists (transferable to heritage stair work).
- **STA (Structural Timber Association)** (structuraltimber.co.uk) — engineered timber stair projects; adjacent expertise.

## Tier 5 — Personal referral routes

- **Merchant advisory panel** (per MERCHANT_ADVISORY_PANEL_CHARTER_V1) — every panel merchant is asked "who's the best staircase expert you've worked with?"
- **Existing Nex network** — check if any current merchant contacts have staircase specialists in their supplier chain
- **LinkedIn targeted outreach** — search for "bench joinery instructor UK", "master stair maker", "senior joiner FMB". Approach individually with the posting.
- **Ex-competition finalists** from The Wood Awards / Institute of Carpenters awards over last 10 years.

## Do NOT source from

- Generalist job boards (Indeed, LinkedIn Jobs paid postings, Reed) — wrong audience, will produce generalist applicants
- Recruitment agencies — this role is too specialised and long-term for typical agency briefs
- Sales-side of any staircase manufacturer — conflict of interest (they'd bias content toward their employer)
- Retirees more than 5 years out of practice — regulation currency will be stale
