# Founding Staircase Author — Nex

**Public-facing job posting** · draft V1 · 2026-07-24
**Intended channels:** BWF newsletter · Institute of Carpenters bulletin · FMB opportunities board · trade colleges (senior instructor networks) · targeted LinkedIn

## The role in one line
Become the recognised expert voice behind Nex Staircases — an AI knowledge platform for the UK trades, launching 2026.

## What you'll do
Nex is a UK-built platform that helps homeowners, contractors and merchants get honest, expert answers about their trade. Every trade has its own **Trade Brain** — a structured knowledge base authored by a single named master tradesperson.

You will author the **staircase Trade Brain**: six modules covering craft, regulations, materials, workflow, defects and pricing model. Your name and credentials appear on every answer Nex gives about staircases. You are the expert — Nex is your voice at scale.

## What we're actually asking of you
- Draft ~120 hours of authored content across 6 modules over 6 weeks
- Use our purpose-built editor (Author Studio) — you paste knowledge in your own words, review AI-extracted facts, accept or reject each one. No coding.
- Meet weekly with the product lead and a merchant advisory panel
- After V1: quarterly reviews (~4 hours per quarter) to keep content current with regulation changes and merchant feedback
- Sign a work-for-hire IP agreement with attribution rights preserved

## Who we're looking for
**Non-negotiable:**
- Minimum 15 years hands-on staircase work (design · build · install · repair) — not just supervisory
- Verified certification (Institute of Carpenters, City & Guilds Level 3 Bench Joinery, or equivalent)
- Working knowledge of current Approved Doc K (Protection from falling) and BS 5395 stair standards
- Written or trained others in your craft (apprentice mentoring counts)
- Willing to have your name and credentials publicly attached to every Brain answer

**Highly desirable:**
- Experience across bespoke joinery + volume stair manufacturing OR domestic + commercial
- Worked in more than one UK region (regional variance strengthens the Brain)
- Comfort with digital tools sufficient to use a GUI editor (Nex builds the tool — you don't need to be a programmer)
- Continuing professional development record

**Not for you if:**
- You've been out of hands-on practice for more than 5 years (regulation currency matters)
- You're a sales-side professional (product rep, merchant BDM) — the role needs neutrality
- You're not willing to commit to a 2-year engagement (V1 authoring + quarterly maintenance)
- You want equity, employment or commission — this is contracted work-for-hire

## Compensation
- **V1 authoring fee:** £8,000 – £15,000 (paid on completion of the 6 V1 modules · negotiable per experience + regional context)
- **Quarterly retainer:** £500 – £1,000 per quarter (correction review + regulation currency)
- **Sample authoring task fee:** £300 (paid to shortlisted candidates regardless of hire outcome)
- **V2 expansion honorarium:** additional payment when V2 modules are commissioned
- **Attribution:** your name, trade and credentials shown on every merchant-facing Brain surface
- **Reference right:** you may cite this work in your CV, portfolio and marketing

## Application process (4 stages · ~4-6 weeks)
1. **Application review** (Week 1-2): CV · certifications · 3 references (2 professional, 1 trade body) · portfolio of 5 written technical guidance examples
2. **Trade authenticity interview** (Week 2-3): with the CTO, Product Lead and one merchant from Nex's advisory panel. ~60 minutes.
3. **Sample authoring task** (Week 3-4): write a workflow module on a topic we agree together (e.g. "how to first-fit a cut-string staircase"). £300 paid on submission.
4. **Contract negotiation** (Week 4): IP · attribution · retainer · timeline
