## Section 3 · Amendment clause (locked per D-17)

**The 30% Levenshtein threshold is INITIAL. It may be changed ONLY through an explicit founder-authored versioning-policy ADR.**

- Master AI cannot tune the threshold based on observed change rates (per feedback_observation_is_not_constitutional_authority.md)
- Master AI cannot fit the threshold to production data
- Master AI cannot lower the threshold to reduce version-row growth
- Master AI cannot raise the threshold to reduce noise
- Master AI may surface runtime observations as founder-facing recommendations
- Founder authors amendment ADR to change the threshold · with its own decision provenance footer + `versioning_threshold.v1.N.N` version tag

## Section 4 · Diff algorithm · founder-authored

**Levenshtein / edit distance.** Deterministic · language-agnostic · surface-level · fast · reproducible.

Not: semantic-similarity models (non-deterministic across model versions) · Jaccard word-level (loses positional information) · structural JSON diff (over-specifies for free-text body content).

Guardian applies Levenshtein at the row body field at write time. Score = `edit_distance / max(len(old), len(new))`. If score > 0.30 → automatic version created.

## Section 5 · Version row shape (locked)

Every version row in `nex.record_versions` carries:

- `version_number` (monotone integer per parent record)
- `previous_version_id` (link to prior version · null for first)
- `rule_set_version` (per R-18 · composed manifest)
- `verifier_instance_id` (per R-18)
- `change_summary` (founder-authored OR auto-generated diff description)
- `changed_by` (founder identity OR verifier identity)
- `changed_at` (ISO-8601)
- `trigger_reason` (which trigger fired · e.g. `body_diff_30pct` · `claim_delta_star_rating` · `authority_change` · `status_transition` · `domain_reclassification` · `founder_declared`)
- `versioning_threshold_version` (currently `versioning_threshold.v1.0.0-initial`)

Version content is **immutable snapshot** per LAM Rule 5 provenance.
