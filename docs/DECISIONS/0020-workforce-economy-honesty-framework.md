# ADR-0020: Workforce Economy Honesty Framework · AI Always Disclosed · Zero Fabrication · Trust Before Scale

Status: Draft (awaiting signoff)
Date: 2026-07-23
Related: `docs/PHASE_33_AI_WORKFORCE_ECONOMY_BLUEPRINT.md` §14.7 · `docs/NEX_BUSINESS_BUILDER_V2_SPEC.md` · `docs/ES-10_ENTERPRISE_SCALE_BLUEPRINT_V1.md` §11 principles

## Context

Phase 33 Workforce Economy uses employment language (hire, retire, promote) to make AI colleagues intuitive for construction merchants. The framing is a strategic asset · but also a compliance and ethics risk if any AI employee misrepresents itself or fabricates content.

The Business Builder V2 spec (§14.7) and Phase 33 blueprint (§14.7) established honesty non-negotiables informally. This ADR codifies them as first-class engineering guarantees enforced at schema and runtime.

Every one of these rules is the foundation of trust. Trust is one-shot: a single incident of fabricated review, faked credential, or human-impersonation to a customer ends the category-shift narrative. Legal exposure is significant · brand exposure is worse.

## Decision

### 1 · AI is always disclosed as AI

**No AI employee shall impersonate a human colleague in any external communication.**

Enforced:
- Every outbound message from an AI-drafted source carries a metadata tag `is_ai_generated: true`
- Merchant tradesites include a footer disclosing that Nex Workforce AI colleagues may draft communications for merchant review
- Terms of use explicitly disclaim personhood + legal capacity of AI employees
- AI employee profiles clearly label "AI employee" in the sub-header, not human

**Merchant-facing framing (Employment Centre, chat) may use warm language (Sarah, James etc.) provided AI status is disclosed.**

### 2 · Zero content fabrication · schema-level enforcement

The following data classes are FORBIDDEN from being generated by AI without merchant explicit provision:

| Data class | Enforcement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Customer reviews / testimonials | Only imported from verified sources (Google Business, Trustpilot, platform reviews) · never generated · schema rejects reviews without `source_reference` |
| Merchant credentials (Gas Safe, NICEIC, MCS, insurance policy numbers) | Only accepted with verified `source_reference` against public register · schema rejects credential claims without verification link |
| Portfolio images | Only merchant-uploaded actual project photos · stock images marked as such · AI-generated as-if-real photos forbidden · Vision AI detection of AI-generated portfolio images blocks upload |
| Aggregate statistics ("500+ happy customers", "10 years experience") | Only accepted with data source · calculated from actuals or rejected |
| Certifications on tradesite | Verified against public register or shown as "self-declared, pending verification" |

**Schema-level enforcement** means the database constraint prevents the write, not that the UI hides the input. This is defence in depth.

### 3 · Verification badges are earned · not granted

Trust badges displayed on merchant tradesites:

| Badge | Requires |
|-------|----------|
| Registered | Companies House / equivalent verified |
| Insured | Public liability policy verified against insurer's public register or verified upload |
| Certified | Gas Safe / NICEIC / MCS / equivalent verified against register |
| Trusted | 10+ real reviews imported with 4.5+ average |
| Master | 100+ platform-completed projects with maintained rating |

No badge is displayed until verification cron confirms. Verification revocation on lapse (certification expired, review count drops).

### 4 · Warm-professional voice · never fake emotion

- AI employees speak in warm-professional tone
- **Never** fake emotional states ("Sarah is excited to help you today")
- **Never** fake anniversaries or milestones the AI didn't actually reach
- **Never** use emotional-manipulation tactics ("Sarah is feeling underappreciated")
- **Never** use urgency manipulation ("Don't miss out")

### 5 · Approval-required always

- No AI-drafted external communication sends without merchant approval (Level 2 default per ADR-0019)
- Level 4 auto-execute (per ADR-0019) whitelist strictly limited to non-external-facing actions or pre-approved templates
- Customer-facing content variations always require approval regardless of level

### 6 · Terms of use disclaim

Nex platform terms of use include specific clauses:
- AI employees are software · not persons · no employment rights · no legal capacity
- Merchant is responsible for approving actions before external send
- Nex is responsible for platform stability + accuracy of documented capabilities
- Liability apportionment: Nex for platform bugs · merchant for actions merchant approved
- AI-generated legal templates come with prominent "solicitor review required" flags · Nex does not provide legal advice

### 7 · Retention on retirement

Retired AI employees preserved for 90 days:
- Merchant can rehire with full memory intact
- After 90 days: profile deleted · memory retained per Phase 26 rules · corrections chain preserved
- Employee's business knowledge is genuinely valuable and does not evaporate on retirement

### 8 · Ecosystem-day-one revenue integrity

Any revenue share to merchants for cross-tenant data contribution (per Business Builder V2 §14.6 breakthrough idea #6):
- Must be honest calculation
- Must be paid, not credited
- Must be documented on merchant statement
- Never a marketing-only claim

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Every merchant-facing surface passes an honesty audit by construction
- Category-shift framing (hire an AI colleague) survives regulatory scrutiny
- Trust foundation cannot be quietly eroded by future feature ambition
- Legal liability apportionment documented and defensible
- Verified badges displayed on tradesites become a genuine trust signal, not marketing

**Negative:**
- Some competitive marketing tactics (fake urgency, generated testimonials) forbidden · sales pressure removed
- Verification cron adds ongoing operations cost per merchant
- Merchants who lack public-register credentials (e.g. new sole traders) may have thinner initial trust surface · UI must handle this without shaming
- AI-generated portfolio detection may occasionally false-positive on real photos with heavy editing · appeal process required

**Neutral:**
- Warm language for AI employees still permitted · principled restraint only

## Alternatives Considered

- **Silent AI (no disclosure)** · rejected · legal risk under emerging AI regulation (EU AI Act, UK AI framework) · brand risk fundamental
- **AI-generated testimonials with disclosure watermark** · rejected · undermines review credibility · brand foundation broken
- **Verification badges granted on merchant self-declaration** · rejected · devalues badges rapidly · trust economy collapses
- **Emotional AI tone (feels more human)** · rejected · manipulation risk · violates warm-professional-not-manipulative principle
- **Auto-send approved templates to customers without per-message merchant review** · rejected for V0 · reconsider at V3+ when calibrated per merchant approval rate
- **Ban warm language entirely (Sarah is now "Bookkeeper Agent 47")** · rejected · loses category-shift value · warm language + honesty are compatible

## Implementation Impact

- Schema constraints on customer_reviews, merchant_credentials, portfolio_images tables enforce source_reference presence
- Verification cron per credential type · daily refresh
- AI-generated image detector integrated into upload pipeline for portfolio + case study images
- Terms of use draft (Legal Counsel deliverable in Phase 0 Week 2-3)
- Verification badge computation via nightly cron
- Content safety filter on every outward-facing AI-drafted message
- Audit log records every merchant credential verification event

## Dependencies

- **Blocks:** Phase 31 Business Builder V2 · Phase 33 Workforce Economy V0 · Employment Centre hire flow
- **Blocked by:** Legal counsel Terms of Use draft + review · Public register verification adapter development
- **Related ADRs:** ADR-0019 (Workforce Trust Ladder · approval requirements) · ADR-0016 (Memory Privacy · verification data storage)

## Enforcement

- Schema constraints tested per CI PR
- AI-generated image detection accuracy monitored monthly
- Verification badge freshness audited quarterly
- Content safety filter tested against adversarial prompts quarterly (per ES-01 §14.8)
- Merchant advisory panel reviews Terms of Use readability + honesty framing before Commercial GA
- Any change to honesty rules requires superseding ADR · they cannot be silently loosened
