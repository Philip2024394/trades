- External authorities (Approved Docs · BS · EN · WHO · UK Building Regulations · Indonesia kemenparekraf · BMKG · OSM · Wikidata · MSME Registry · etc.) are authored in a founder-authored registry

- Guardian rejects citations to unregistered authorities (fail-closed to `authority_check_disabled_pending_registry` or `unregistered_authority`)
- Country-scoped filtering via `geography_scope` field (per §7.10 M1 Location metadata)
