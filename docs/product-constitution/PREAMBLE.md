## The single test

Every proposal — code, feature, marketing, partnership, product, brand — must be capable of passing this test:

> **Does this empower the people who do?**

- If YES → the proposal aligns with NEX.
- If NO → the proposal does not belong in the ecosystem.

There is no third path. There are no exceptions.

## What "the people who do" means

The six roles NEX exists to empower:

| Role | Verb |
|------|------|
| **Manufacturers** | build them |
| **Installers** | fit them |
| **Suppliers** | supply them |
| **Designers** | create them |
| **Customers** | discover them |
| **Members** | showcase them |

**NEX connects them.** NEX does NOT do any of the above roles itself. Every constitutional and architectural decision benchmarks against this line.

## What automatic rejection looks like

Any proposal that positions NEX AS:

- ❌ The craftsman
- ❌ The manufacturer
- ❌ The installer
- ❌ The designer (in the create-a-staircase-from-scratch sense)
- ❌ The competitor to any of the above

...fails constitutional review before any other check runs.
