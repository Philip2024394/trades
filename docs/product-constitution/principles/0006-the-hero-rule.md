# Principle 0006 · The Hero Rule

**Status:** Active · Immutable
**Established:** 2026-07-30 · Philip O'Farrell
**Applies to:** Every NEX surface — marketing pages · product pages · onboarding flows · brochure · print collateral · social / video · pitch decks · any page or document where a *"hero"* (the subject the reader's attention lands on first) appears
**Governs:** who or what may occupy the hero position on any NEX-branded surface

---

## The Rule

> **The hero of every NEX page must be one of:**

- ✅ The craftsman
- ✅ The member
- ✅ The staircase
- ✅ The homeowner
- ✅ The knowledge
- ✅ The connected ecosystem

> **The hero is NEVER:**

- ❌ AI
- ❌ Software
- ❌ NEX itself (as an abstract technology)
- ❌ Technology / the platform

## Why this exists

NEX is downstream of the Constitutional Preamble: *"NEX doesn't make staircases. NEX powers the people who do."* Any page that positions NEX as the hero implicitly claims NEX is the doer — the maker, the creator, the source of the value.

That's the failure mode Philip has been correcting throughout the 2026-07-30 branding session. Left unchecked, marketing gravity pulls every page toward *"NEX creates..."* / *"NEX makes..."* / *"our AI generates..."* — the exact opposite of the constitutional preamble.

The Hero Rule closes the gap at the page level.

## Approved / rejected examples

| Wrong | Right |
|-------|-------|
| *"NEX creates beautiful staircases."* | *"Discover extraordinary staircases created by our members."* |
| *"NEX intelligent manufacturing system."* | *"Manufacturing intelligence designed for the people who build extraordinary staircases."* |
| *"NEX AI installation software."* | *"Connected installation intelligence for staircase professionals."* |
| *"Our AI understands your staircase."* | *"Your staircase, understood — through the intelligence connecting the industry."* |
| *"NEX designs your dream staircase."* | *"Designers create dream staircases. NEX helps you find them."* |
| *"NEX generates concept renders."* | *"Members showcase concepts they've created."* |

The pattern in each swap: **move the human doer into the subject position, move NEX into a supporting clause.**

## Composition with adjacent principles

- **Principle 0005 (Transparent AI Identity)** — runtime honesty when asked *"are you an AI?"* — governs identity answers in chat. Compatible.
- **Principle 0005a (Brand-Voice Never Lead with AI)** — marketing copy never LEADS with *"AI"*. Different scope, same direction.
- **Principle 0006 (this Hero Rule)** — the SUBJECT of a page is never NEX / AI / Software / Technology.

All three compose. Every marketing page passes all three or does not ship.

## The three-question ship gate

1. Does this page have a hero from the approved list? *(Principle 0006)*
2. Does this page LEAD with the approved voice, without leading with "AI"? *(Principle 0005a)*
3. Does this proposal *"empower the people who do"*? *(Preamble)*

If any of the three fail, the page does not ship. No exceptions.

## The specific drift the Hero Rule catches

Marketing gravity produces these failure patterns naturally:

- **Feature-as-hero drift** — *"AI-powered staircase generation"* headlines. Fails: AI is the hero.
- **Platform-as-hero drift** — *"The NEX Platform"* headlines. Fails: NEX itself as hero, not the ecosystem it serves.
- **Technology-as-hero drift** — *"Powered by tomorrow's intelligence"* headlines. Fails: technology is the hero, not the people the technology serves.

The Hero Rule blocks all three at the same time.
