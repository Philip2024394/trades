# The Five Different Skill Levels Within Staircase Work

A staircase installer can be a carpenter, but not every staircase installer is a carpenter. It depends on the depth of their knowledge and what they are capable of doing.

There are really five different skill levels.

## Staircase Fitter (Installer)

* Can unload and assemble a staircase.
* Reads fitting drawings.
* Knows how to fix squeaks and movement.
* Can scribe skirts and trims.
* Knows how to level and plumb components.
* Understands fixing methods.
* Usually does not make staircases.

**Knowledge level:**

* Installation: 90%
* Joinery: 30%
* Carpentry: 40%

## 2. Carpenter

A carpenter is expected to know far more than simply fitting staircases.

They should understand:

* Roofing
* First fixing
* Second fixing
* Floors
* Doors
* Stud walls
* Kitchens
* Timber construction
* Building regulations
* Setting out
* Structural loading
* Timber movement
* Different materials

A carpenter can generally build most things from timber.

**Knowledge level:**

* Installation: 80%
* Carpentry: 100%
* Joinery: 70%

## 3. Joiner

A joiner is different again.

They are generally workshop based and understand:

* Timber species
* Moisture movement
* Machinery
* Construction methods
* Laminations
* Veneers
* Grain matching
* Finishing
* Precision manufacturing

They may never fit what they build.

**Knowledge level:**

* Manufacturing: 100%
* Installation: 30%
* Carpentry: 50%

## 4. Staircase Manufacturer

This is where people become confused.

Someone that manufactures staircases is usually a specialist joiner.

They understand:

* Rise and going calculations
* Winder geometry
* Structural requirements
* Timber movement
* Material selection
* Handrail geometry
* CNC manufacturing
* Traditional joinery
* Building regulations
* Manufacturing tolerances
* Production methods

They can spend twenty years learning nothing but staircases.

Many cannot fit one particularly well because fitting is an entirely different skill set.

## 5. Specialist Staircase Installer

This is the level most people don't appreciate.

A specialist staircase installer may know:

* Building movement
* Site tolerances
* Plaster tolerances
* Structural requirements
* Packing methods
* String line accuracy
* Laser measurements
* Load transfer
* How houses are constructed
* How to overcome site problems
* Building regulations
* Finish quality expectations

They may install £50,000 staircases every week but never manufacture one.

Their mindset is completely different.

## Three Different Questions, Three Different Skills

A staircase manufacturer asks:

> *"Can I build this?"*

A staircase installer asks:

> *"Will this fit?"*

A carpenter asks:

> *"Can I make this from timber?"*

They are three different questions requiring three different skills.

## Example — A Wall That Is 12 mm Out of Square

If a wall is 12 mm out of square:

* The manufacturer has done nothing wrong.
* The carpenter may decide to plane material.
* The staircase installer may need to move the entire staircase, alter packings, re-level strings, adjust wall margins and remake trims.

That knowledge only comes from installation experience.

## Skill Comparison

| Trade | Manufacturing | Installation | Building Knowledge |
|---|---|---|---|
| Carpenter | High | High | Very High |
| Joiner | Very High | Low | Medium |
| Staircase Manufacturer | Expert | Medium | High |
| Staircase Installer | Low-Medium | Expert | Very High |
| General Builder | Medium | Medium | High |

The interesting thing is that staircase installation is often underestimated. Installing a high-end staircase in an imperfect building can require knowledge from several trades:

* Carpentry
* Joinery
* Structural construction
* Plastering tolerances
* Roofing and floor levels
* Geometry
* Building regulations
* Timber movement
* Problem solving

A staircase installer may spend years learning how to make a staircase fit buildings that are rarely square, level, or plumb. Conversely, an excellent staircase manufacturer may spend years learning timber selection, machining, and joinery techniques without ever developing those installation skills.
