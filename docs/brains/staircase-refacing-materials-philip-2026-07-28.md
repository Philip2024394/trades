# What Materials Can Be Used to Reface a Staircase?

Refacing a staircase offers an opportunity to completely change its appearance without necessarily replacing the entire staircase. A wide range of materials and finishes are available, allowing the staircase to complement both traditional and modern interiors.

The best material depends on your budget, the style of your home, the condition of the existing staircase, and the finish you would like to achieve.

## Timber

Timber remains the most popular choice for staircase refacing because it offers a natural appearance and can often be repaired or refinished in the future.

Popular timber species include:

* American White Oak
* European Oak
* Ash
* Walnut
* Pine
* Beech
* Sapele
* Maple

Each timber has its own colour, grain pattern, hardness and finishing characteristics.

## Engineered Timber

Some manufacturers use engineered timber products with a real hardwood veneer.

These products can provide excellent stability and consistency while maintaining the appearance of solid timber. The suitability of engineered products depends on the intended use and the manufacturer's specification.

## Painted Finishes

Many homeowners choose to paint parts of their staircase rather than exposing the natural timber.

Popular combinations include:

* White strings with oak treads.
* White risers with natural oak treads.
* White newel posts with stained handrails.
* Fully painted staircases in contemporary colours.

Painted finishes are often chosen to brighten hallways or achieve a more traditional or modern appearance.

## Glass

Glass can be incorporated into many staircase refacing projects by replacing traditional timber balusters with toughened safety glass.

Glass panels can create a more open appearance and allow natural light to pass through the staircase.

Whether glass is suitable depends on the staircase design and supporting structure.

## Metal

Metal components can be introduced during refacing, including:

* Stainless steel balusters.
* Powder-coated steel balusters.
* Decorative metal spindles.
* Steel handrail details.
* Concealed structural steel where required.

Metal is commonly used to create contemporary staircase designs.

## Stair Runners and Carpet

Some homeowners combine timber refacing with new carpet or stair runners.

This can soften the appearance, reduce wear on the timber and complement the overall interior design.

## Can I Mix Materials?

Yes.

Many of today's staircases combine different materials to create a unique appearance.

Examples include:

* Oak treads with white painted strings.
* Walnut handrails with glass balustrades.
* Timber treads with metal balusters.
* Painted staircases with timber feature handrails.

The choice should complement the style of your home rather than simply follow current trends.
