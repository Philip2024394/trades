# What Does Refacing a Staircase Mean?

Refacing a staircase is the process of improving the appearance of an existing staircase by replacing or covering visible components while retaining most or all of the original structural framework.

Rather than removing and replacing the entire staircase, refacing allows many staircases to achieve a completely new appearance at a lower cost and with less disruption, provided the existing staircase is structurally suitable.

## What Parts Are Usually Replaced?

A staircase refacing project may include replacing or covering:

* Treads
* Risers
* Handrails
* Newel posts
* Balusters (spindles)
* Strings or string cladding
* Newel caps
* Decorative mouldings

The amount of work depends on the condition of the existing staircase and the appearance you wish to achieve.

## Is Every Staircase Suitable for Refacing?

Not always.

Before deciding to reface a staircase, the existing structure should be assessed to ensure it is suitable.

If the staircase has excessive movement, structural damage, significant wear, or design issues, repairing or replacing the staircase may be more appropriate than simply improving its appearance.

Refacing should not be used to hide structural defects.

## What Are the Benefits of Refacing?

Refacing can offer several advantages:

* A completely updated appearance.
* Less disruption than replacing the entire staircase.
* Existing structural framework can often remain.
* A wide choice of timber species and finishes.
* Opportunity to modernise handrails, newel posts and balustrades.

Many homeowners choose to reface their staircase during wider home renovations to match new flooring, doors or interior décor.

## Can I Change the Style Completely?

In many cases, yes.

A traditional staircase may be updated with more contemporary handrails and balustrades, while maintaining the existing structural staircase.

Depending on the design, it may also be possible to introduce glass panels, metal balusters, feature newel posts, or different timber species to achieve a completely different appearance.

The available options depend on the construction of the original staircase.

## Is Refacing Cheaper Than a New Staircase?

Often, but not always.

If the existing staircase is in good structural condition, refacing can be a cost-effective way to transform its appearance.

However, if significant structural repairs are required before refacing can begin, the cost difference between refacing and replacement may become much smaller.

Each staircase should be assessed individually.

## Expert Tip

Refacing changes how a staircase **looks**, not necessarily how it **performs**.

Before investing in new finishes, ensure the existing staircase is structurally sound. Cosmetic improvements should enhance a good staircase, not disguise underlying structural problems.
