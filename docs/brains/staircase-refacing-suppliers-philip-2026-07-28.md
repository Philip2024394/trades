# Who Can Reface a Staircase?

The type of company you choose depends on the amount of work involved.

## Staircase Manufacturers

Many staircase manufacturers offer staircase refacing as part of their services.

They are often the best choice where structural alterations, new balustrades, replacement strings, or bespoke timber components are required. They can also manufacture matching parts that are not available off the shelf.

## Specialist Staircase Refurbishment Companies

Some companies specialise entirely in staircase renovations and refacing.

These businesses focus on updating existing staircases and may offer complete design, manufacture and installation services.

## Carpenters and Joiners

Experienced carpenters or joiners may also carry out staircase refacing, particularly where the work involves replacing timber components, fitting new handrails or balustrades, or carrying out bespoke joinery.

Their experience with staircase construction is often more important than their job title, as staircases require accurate fitting and an understanding of structural components.

## Which Is the Best Choice?

There is no single answer.

If the project is mainly cosmetic, an experienced carpenter, joiner or staircase refurbishment specialist may be suitable.

If structural changes, bespoke manufacturing or major alterations are required, a staircase manufacturer is often the most appropriate choice.

## Expert Tip

Before choosing a contractor, ask to see examples of previous staircase refacing projects similar to your own. Staircases combine structural performance with fine joinery, so experience with staircase work is usually more valuable than general carpentry experience.

Also discuss exactly what is included in the quotation. Some companies only replace visible components, while others can redesign, manufacture and install an almost completely new staircase using parts of the existing structure where appropriate.
