# Always Put Staircase Measurements in Writing – Protecting Everyone Involved

There is an old saying within construction:

> *"The shortest pencil is better than the longest memory."*

Few things are more important within staircase manufacturing than accurate site measurements and clear communication. A staircase may take weeks to manufacture and can remain within your home for generations, so taking a little extra time during the measuring stage can prevent significant problems later.

Whenever staircase measurements are provided by a homeowner, carpenter, builder, or another trade, they should always be supplied in writing and accompanied by clear supporting information.

This protects everyone involved.

## Never Rely Upon Verbal Measurements

Construction projects involve hundreds of conversations taking place over many weeks and sometimes many months.

Measurements provided verbally can easily become:

* Forgotten.
* Misheard.
* Mixed with another project.
* Written down incorrectly.
* Assumed rather than confirmed.
* Changed without being communicated.

What was clearly understood today may become uncertain tomorrow.

For this reason, staircase dimensions should never rely solely upon telephone conversations or verbal instructions wherever possible.

## Always Send Official Confirmation

Measurements should always be confirmed by:

* Email.
* Written messages.
* Official quotations.
* Technical drawings.
* Approved site surveys.
* Staircase measurement forms.

Even something as simple as a screenshot can prove extremely valuable later.

If measurements are being supplied through messaging applications such as WhatsApp, ensure that screenshots are taken and retained by both parties before manufacture begins.

This creates a permanent record of:

* Dimensions supplied.
* Dates measurements were provided.
* Any amendments requested.
* Questions raised before manufacture.
* Approved staircase specifications.

Good record keeping protects both homeowners and staircase companies equally.

## Include More Than Just Numbers

Simply sending:

> "2800 mm x 920 mm"

is rarely sufficient information when manufacturing a bespoke staircase.

Where possible, measurements should always include:

* Photographs.
* Simple sketches.
* Dimension lines.
* Ceiling heights.
* Wall positions.
* Staircase opening dimensions.
* Flooring information.
* Notes regarding plaster or wall finishes.

The more information provided during the measuring stage, the fewer assumptions need to be made during manufacture.

## Why Written Records Matter

Occasionally, disagreements arise regarding dimensions supplied during a project.

Without written records it can become extremely difficult to establish:

* Which measurements were provided.
* Whether dimensions were later changed.
* Who supplied the information.
* Whether allowances had been discussed.
* If amendments were approved before manufacture began.

By comparison, written confirmation provides clarity for everyone involved.

Rather than becoming a disagreement between two parties, the information simply speaks for itself.

The objective should never be to prove someone wrong—it should be to establish the facts quickly and professionally so that solutions can be found where necessary.

## Protecting Homeowners and Manufacturers

Many homeowners assume that written confirmations are intended to protect manufacturers.

They are equally important for protecting homeowners.

Written records may confirm that:

* Measurements supplied were incorrect.
* Measurements supplied were correct.
* Manufacturing dimensions differed from those provided.
* Amendments were requested before manufacture.
* Additional work was discussed and approved.

Construction projects involve many conversations and decisions. Keeping important information in writing protects everyone involved throughout the process.

## A Few Extra Minutes Can Save Weeks

Taking a few extra minutes to produce a simple sketch or email confirmation can prevent weeks of unnecessary delays and expense later.

Professional staircase companies will always prefer receiving:

* Clear dimensions.
* Supporting photographs.
* Written confirmations.
* Site sketches.
* Approved drawings.
* Detailed notes regarding the project.

Remember, a bespoke staircase cannot simply be exchanged from stock if measurements prove incorrect after manufacture has begun.

Accuracy at the beginning of the project remains one of the most important investments you can make.

## A Final Thought

Verbal conversations remain an important part of every construction project, but they should never become the only record of important dimensions or decisions.

Today's measurements can easily become tomorrow's forgotten conversation.

Always confirm staircase measurements in writing and retain copies of all correspondence, drawings, screenshots, and approvals throughout the project.

Doing so protects homeowners, manufacturers, installers, and everyone else involved in delivering a beautiful finished staircase.
