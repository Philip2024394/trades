# Who Should Measure Your Staircase? Why This Decision Matters More Than You Think

One of the most important stages of any bespoke staircase project happens long before manufacture ever begins—the site survey and staircase measurements.

Unfortunately, it is also one of the areas where costly mistakes can occur.

Homeowners are sometimes advised that their local carpenter, builder, or another tradesperson can provide the dimensions required to manufacture their staircase. While this may appear to save both time and money, it can introduce unnecessary risks into what is often one of the most expensive furniture features within the home.

Our advice is simple.

> **The company manufacturing your staircase should always take responsibility for the staircase measurements from start to finish whenever possible.**

## Why Measurements Matter

A bespoke staircase is manufactured to millimetre tolerances. Unlike many other construction products, staircases cannot simply be cut shorter or made wider once they arrive on site.

Measurements affect:

* Every tread.
* Every riser.
* Staircase geometry.
* Wall allowances.
* Floor finishes.
* Landing dimensions.
* Structural fixings.
* Handrail positions.
* Manufacturing tolerances.
* Installation methods.

A small measurement discrepancy at site survey stage can become a major manufacturing issue several weeks later.

## Staircase Measuring Is a Specialist Skill

Professional carpenters are highly skilled tradespeople. Builders are experts in construction. Site managers understand how projects come together.

However, staircase measuring remains a specialist discipline.

Modern house building methods have changed considerably over recent decades. Many trades are no longer routinely involved with traditional staircase setting out and bespoke staircase manufacture.

Questions that staircase surveyors consider include:

* Will flooring be installed before or after the staircase?
* How much plaster build-up is expected?
* Is the staircase opening square?
* Are the trimmers correctly positioned?
* Will wall tolerances affect installation?
* Has sufficient clearance been allowed for handrails and volutes?
* Are there any manufacturing allowances required?

These are questions specialist staircase companies deal with every single day.

## Who Is Responsible?

One of the greatest advantages of allowing the staircase company to undertake the site survey themselves is accountability.

There is no uncertainty regarding:

* Measurements.
* Manufacturing dimensions.
* Installation requirements.
* Site tolerances.
* Responsibility should problems arise.

The staircase company owns the process from beginning to end.

Introducing multiple parties into the measuring process can unnecessarily complicate matters.

For example:

* The homeowner measures the staircase.
* The builder confirms the dimensions.
* The carpenter submits the measurements.
* The staircase company manufactures the staircase.
* The installers arrive on site.

If a problem occurs, determining responsibility immediately becomes considerably more difficult.

By comparison:

> **The staircase company measures, manufactures and installs the staircase.**

There is simply no confusion regarding responsibility.

## What If My Carpenter Is Measuring the Staircase?

There are occasions where staircase companies work alongside trusted local carpenters and builders whom they know well. In these situations, both parties understand the information required and have established working relationships developed over many years.

Where this relationship exists, measurements can often be managed successfully because expectations and responsibilities have been clearly defined.

However, homeowners should avoid assuming that any carpenter will automatically understand the manufacturing requirements of a bespoke staircase simply because they work within construction.

Specialist trades exist for a reason.

## Consider Using Local Companies

Price is naturally an important consideration when choosing a staircase company.

Occasionally, homeowners are attracted by companies located many hundreds of miles away offering significantly lower prices. There is absolutely nothing wrong with obtaining quotations nationally, but it is worth considering the practicalities involved should problems arise.

Ask yourself:

* Who is undertaking the site survey?
* Who is responsible for the measurements?
* Who is returning if alterations are required?
* How quickly can problems be resolved?
* Are installation teams travelling significant distances?

Sometimes a local specialist staircase company may offer significant advantages simply because they remain readily available throughout the project.

## When Measurements Go Wrong

Unfortunately, staircase measurement errors can occasionally be extremely costly.

Unlike flooring or doors, bespoke staircases cannot simply be exchanged from stock.

In severe cases, measurement discrepancies can result in:

* Major manufacturing alterations.
* Significant installation delays.
* Structural modifications.
* Partial remanufacture.
* Complete staircase rebuilds.

The cost of rebuilding a bespoke staircase can sometimes approach the cost of manufacturing the original staircase itself.

What initially appeared to be a saving during the measuring stage can quickly become the most expensive decision made during the project.

## Your Staircase Is Furniture

Many homeowners understandably view staircases simply as part of the building structure.

Professional staircase manufacturers view them rather differently.

Your staircase is one of the largest bespoke furniture installations within your home.

It has been designed, manufactured, finished, transported, and installed specifically for your property.

Would you ask another trade to measure bespoke stone worktops or manufacture fitted furniture for your kitchen?

The same principles apply here.

## A Final Thought

The simplest way to avoid unnecessary problems is also usually the best.

Allow the staircase company to take responsibility for:

* Site measurements.
* Manufacturing.
* Installation.
* Final fitting.

Keeping responsibility with one specialist company from beginning to end protects both the homeowner and the manufacturer.

There is no confusion over dimensions, no uncertainty regarding accountability, and no unnecessary assumptions during manufacture.

Your staircase may remain within your home for decades. Taking the time to ensure that it has been measured correctly from the very beginning is one of the best investments you can make.
