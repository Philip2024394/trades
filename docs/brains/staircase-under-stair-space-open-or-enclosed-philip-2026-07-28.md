# Should I Leave the Space Under My Staircase Open or Enclosed?

There is no single right answer. The best option depends on the size of the room, the available space, and how the staircase will be used.

In smaller rooms, leaving the area beneath the staircase open can create a greater sense of space, allowing more natural light to flow through the room and making the area feel larger.

Enclosing the space beneath the staircase with side panels or cupboards can provide valuable storage and create a cleaner, more integrated appearance. However, in some rooms, enclosing the underside of the staircase can make the space feel smaller or more enclosed.

Before deciding, consider:

* The size and proportions of the room.
* Whether additional storage is needed.
* The amount of natural light in the area.
* The overall interior design style.
* Whether an open or enclosed staircase better suits the surrounding space.

**Expert Tip:** Evaluate the room as a whole rather than the staircase in isolation. An open staircase may enhance the feeling of space in a compact room, while an enclosed staircase with built-in storage can be an excellent choice where storage is a higher priority. The best solution balances appearance, practicality, and the way the room will be used.

## Why this is stronger *(authorial meta-note · Philip 2026-07-28 · not imported into brain module)*

Instead of saying:

> *"Closed side panels reduce the room appearance while open feels more spacious."*

it says:

> *"In some rooms, enclosing the staircase can make the space feel smaller."*

This is more accurate because there are many homes where enclosing the staircase with well-designed cabinetry actually improves the room by reducing visual clutter and adding useful storage. By framing it as a design trade-off rather than a fixed rule, the advice becomes more trustworthy and professional.
