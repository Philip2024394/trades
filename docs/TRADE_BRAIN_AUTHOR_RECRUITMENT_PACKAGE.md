## Section 1 · What we're actually hiring

Not employees. Not consultants. **Trade Brain Authors** — a new role that combines: master trade practitioner · content editor · knowledge architect · long-term platform contributor.

Each Author owns the intellectual authority for one trade's Brain. Their name appears on every Brain output. Their reputation is on the line. Their expertise compounds Nex's moat over years.

## Section 3 · Author profile · what "master" means

**Non-negotiable qualifications:**

- Minimum 15 years hands-on trade experience (not just supervisory)
- Verified certification from recognised body (e.g. NICEIC/NAPIT for electrician · Gas Safe for heating · Federation of Master Builders member · CITB card holder)
- Experience across multiple regional contexts (worked in ≥2 UK regions ideally)
- Track record of training apprentices or writing trade content
- References from trade body or established employers

**Highly desirable:**

- Regional accent + vocabulary breadth (helps with vocabulary layer)
- Digital literacy sufficient to edit structured JSON via GUI tool (Nex will provide the tool · they need not be programmers)
- Willingness to be named publicly on every Brain surface
- Experience with commercial + domestic work
- History of continuing professional development

**Sourcing channels:**

- Trade unions (Unite Construction, etc.)
- Federation of Master Builders (FMB)
- National Federation of Roofing Contractors
- Guild of Master Craftsmen
- Trade colleges (senior instructors)
- Trade press writers
- Nex's own advisory panel merchants (may know candidates)

## Section 4 · Not who we hire

Explicitly rejected profiles:

- Generalists with breadth but not depth
- Sales-side trade professionals (BDMs, product reps) — bias toward promoted materials
- Retired tradespeople with >5 years out of practice (regulation currency lost)
- Consultants who write generic construction content (no personal skin in the game)
- Anyone unwilling to sign work-for-hire IP terms
- Anyone who cannot commit to quarterly Brain maintenance for minimum 2 years

## Section 5 · Compensation structure

Per ADR-0017 §4.7 authoring is contracted work-for-hire · not employment.

**Structure:**

- **Honorarium per Brain authored** — one-time payment on completion of V1 (6 modules)
- **V1 authoring fee target:** £8,000-£15,000 per Brain (~120 hours × market rate for master tradesperson consultant time · negotiable per author + regional cost of living)
- **V2 module expansion honorarium** — additional payment for the 4 deferred modules
- **Quarterly maintenance retainer** — £500-£1,000/quarter for correction review + regulation updates
- **Attribution:** author's name displayed on every merchant-facing Brain surface with their trade + certifications
- **Reference right:** authors may cite their Nex work in their CV + marketing

**Explicitly not offered at V1:**

- Royalty per merchant subscription — revisit at Y3+ when revenue supports
- Equity — not appropriate for contracted role
- Full-time employment — no need at author scale

## Section 6 · Author interview process

### Stage 1 · Application review (Week 1-2 of recruitment)
- CV + certifications verified against public registers
- 3 references (2 professional + 1 trade body)
- Portfolio: 5 written examples of technical trade guidance they've produced

### Stage 2 · Trade authenticity interview (Week 2-3)
Conducted by CTO + Product Lead + 1 pilot merchant from advisory panel.
Questions target:
- Depth of regional variance knowledge
- Ability to explain concepts clearly (test the voice pack contribution)
- Comfort with structured content editing
- Alignment with Nex honesty principles (per ADR-0020)
- Openness to merchant corrections influencing content
- Willingness to have their name on it

### Stage 3 · Sample authoring task (Week 3-4)
- Author writes a **workflow module** for a chosen sub-specialisation (e.g. "how to first-fix a bathroom" for plumber candidate)
- Compensated £300 for the task regardless of hire outcome
- Nex evaluates: technical accuracy · clarity · evidence citations · voice quality · adherence to Brain schema (schema is provided)

### Stage 4 · Contract negotiation (Week 4)
- Work-for-hire IP terms clarified
- Attribution + reference rights confirmed
- Maintenance retainer negotiated
- Timeline for V1 delivery agreed (6 weeks post-signing typical)

## Section 7 · Author onboarding · post-signature

**Week 1 of authoring engagement:**

- Introduction to Nex platform (2-3 hours)
- Trade Brain schema training (2 hours)
- Editing tool training (JSON-editor with schema validation and pack preview — Nex builds this in Phase 0 Week 2-3)
- Pair with existing Author if one is on retainer (mentorship)

**Week 2-6 · Authoring V1 modules:**

- 6 modules to deliver (craft · regulations · materials · workflow · defects · pricing_model)
- Weekly checkpoint with Product Lead
- Merchant advisory panel review at Week 4
- Sign-off at Week 6

## Section 8 · Author retention · long-term

Authors are the moat. Retention matters more than any code contributor.

**Retention mechanisms:**

- Named authorship on every Brain surface (career-visible)
- Quarterly retainer (paid, not aspirational)
- Annual "Authors Summit" for cross-Brain collaboration (paid attendance)
- Right to co-author sub-specialisation expansions (revenue-share when specialist marketplace opens Y3+)
- Invitation to Nex advisory board (Y2+)
- Priority access to Nex platform features for their own trade business

**Exit handling:**

- Retiring Author's Brain content remains their credited work
- Successor Author onboarded with mentorship where possible
- Correction chain preserves attribution across version changes

## Section 9 · Author's commercial expectations

**What Nex asks of Authors:**

- Truthful content · evidence-cited · regulation-current
- Timely review of merchant corrections (weekly)
- Regulation currency (author flags regulation changes when they occur in their trade)
- 2-year minimum engagement (renewable)
- Public attribution acceptance

**What Nex commits to Authors:**

- Honorarium paid on schedule
- No unauthorised edits to their Brain content
- Attribution preserved regardless of platform evolution
- Content ownership clear (work-for-hire but attribution rights protected)
- 6-month notice period on termination
